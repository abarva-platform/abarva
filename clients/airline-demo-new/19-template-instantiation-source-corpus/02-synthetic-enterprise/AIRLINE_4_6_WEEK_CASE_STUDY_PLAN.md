# Airline 4-6 Week Case Study Plan

Week 1: kickoff, business-problem framing, source inventory, data-plane readiness, public research prefill, executive interview scheduling.

Week 2: collect source files and system extracts for technology procurement scope; register source manifests; begin interview synthesis; land parser-visible source corpus only.

Week 3: parse contracts, rate cards, invoices, SLA/ticket history, proposals and relationship template; extract candidate facts; resolve entities and source-native IDs.

Week 4: client review of conflicts, missing evidence, proposal claims, KPI disclosure modes and relationship graph; prepare Source-ready baseline candidate.

Weeks 5-6: run reconstruction validation against restricted truth, publish approved Knowledge Baseline, build Source event projections, prepare executive case-study narrative and proof bundle.

The case study is intentionally business-problem led: sourcing decisions are evaluated against operational resilience, continuity, transition risk, value realization and evidence quality, not against technology detail for its own sake.
