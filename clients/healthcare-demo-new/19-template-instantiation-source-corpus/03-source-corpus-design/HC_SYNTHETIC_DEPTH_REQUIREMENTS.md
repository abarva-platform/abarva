# Healthcare Synthetic Depth Requirements

Required proof areas:

- Epic modules, environments, integrations, analytics and revenue-cycle dependencies.
- On-prem SQL Server/SSIS/SSAS/SSRS/legacy EDW estate at enterprise scale.
- Provider and payer operations across clinical, revenue-cycle, health-plan, pharmacy and member-service domains.
- Medicare/MA assumption explicitly tenant-specific.
- Star Ratings, HEDIS, CAHPS and RAF measures connected to source systems, populations, definitions and contracts.
- Azure current foundation and use cases plus AWS future agentic/transformation pivot.
- Contract and procurement evidence deep enough for Source, including pricing, SLAs, service credits, data residency, transition assistance, proposals and BAFO.
