# Healthcare Source Family Strategy

Clients should not fill one workbook per object. The source corpus uses a few large extracts by owner plus representative documents where ambiguity lives.

The most important families are Epic module/environment extracts, interface inventories, SQL/EDW catalogs, quality/Stars workbooks, payer platform exports, vendor/contract registers, MSA/SOW/BAA documents, proposal/BAFO/pricing files and executive interviews.
