# Healthcare 4-6 Week Case Study Plan

## Week 1: Intake and operating context
Collect enterprise profile, population mix, Epic inventory, payer platforms, core apps, contracts, interviews and first-pass source registry.

## Week 2: Data and measures
Load SQL Server/EDW, data products, BI/report catalog, Stars/HEDIS/CAHPS/RAF definitions and conflicting measure evidence.

## Week 3: Relationships and source event
Build Epic-to-process-to-data-to-measure chains, vendor/contract mapping and sourcing-event baseline.

## Week 4: Review and corrections
Human review for Medicare/MA assumption, Star measure definitions, source ownership, contract links, Azure/AWS posture and evidence gaps.

## Weeks 5-6: Publication candidate
Publish Knowledge baseline candidate, Source projection, Tower measures and Moves backlog only after reconstruction and governance gates pass.
