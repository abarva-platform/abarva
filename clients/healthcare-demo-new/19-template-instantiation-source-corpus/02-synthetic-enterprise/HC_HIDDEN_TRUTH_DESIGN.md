# Healthcare Hidden Truth Design

Restricted evaluator truth is separated from the parser-visible corpus. The corpus should allow reconstruction, but not as a perfect one-to-one export.

Truth themes:

1. Tenant-specific Medicare/MA exposure: approximately 80% of covered lives.
2. Epic-centered provider operations with deep module/environment/reporting dependencies.
3. SQL Server, SSIS, SSAS, SSRS and legacy EDW debt materially constrain data and AI.
4. Azure is the current data foundation and hosts a few current AI/use-case workloads.
5. AWS is the likely future pivot for agentic and transformational use cases.
6. Stars/HEDIS/CAHPS/RAF measures are commercial and regulatory operating levers.
7. Source decisions must account for contracts, BAA/data residency, transition assistance, service credits, rate cards, invoices and proposal exceptions.
