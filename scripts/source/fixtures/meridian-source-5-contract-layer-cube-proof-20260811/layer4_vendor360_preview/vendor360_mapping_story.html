CPO promise

Vendor 360 will show where the evidence came from, what grain it arrived at, what canonical object it became, and whether Procurement is allowed to act.

Layer 1

Source Extract

CLM, documents, AP/PO, SLA, operations, transition, controls, sourcing, and Finance stay owner-shaped.

Layer 2

Adapter

Preserves source file, row, system, record ID, timestamp, owner, and native grain.

Layer 3

Canonical

Contracts, suppliers, documents, clauses, invoices, SLAs, controls, facts, conflicts, and calculations get stable IDs.

Layer 4

Vendor 360

Procurement sees evidence counts, commercial exposure, gates, and why action is allowed or blocked.
