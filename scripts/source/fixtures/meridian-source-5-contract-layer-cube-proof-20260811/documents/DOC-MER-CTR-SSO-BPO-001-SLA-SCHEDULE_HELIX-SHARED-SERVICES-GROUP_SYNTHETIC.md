## Page 1 - Service Level Definitions

Primary KPIs are cross-process governance cycle time, labor transition accuracy, knowledge transfer backlog age, and exit readiness first-pass quality with metric definitions tied to native exports.

## Page 2 - Fee Base

Monthly fee base excludes taxes, pass-throughs, transition charges, and disputed one-time items. Credits use the current operative pricing line for the service tower.

## Page 3 - Exclusions

Client-caused dependency holds, policy freezes, force majeure, and approved transition blackouts are excluded only when evidenced by issue IDs.

## Page 4 - Claim Window

Claims are due by day fifteen of the following month and must include breach row ID, supplier responsibility state, fee base, and reviewer decision.
