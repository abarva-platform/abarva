## Page 1 - Rate Card

Rates include FTE/month, transaction, outcome, transition fee, retained-cost offset, volume band, UOM, effective period, and operative/superseded state.

## Page 2 - Volume Bands

Bands reset monthly by service tower. Minimum commitments may not be blended across accounts payable, month-end close, reconciliations, and management reporting.

## Page 3 - Uplift and COLA

Annual uplift is capped at 3.5 percent after the first twelve months unless the renewal quote lowers the commercial baseline.

## Page 4 - Value Treatment

Avoided, recoverable, negotiated, and finance-confirmed value are separate measures; unresolved conflicts block action and value claims.
