Source: ONC Health IT Dashboard 2025, HL7 FHIR Maturity Data, CommonWell Health Alliance 2025 Report, Epic UserWeb Data

INTEROPERABILITY — STRATEGIC CONTEXT

Healthcare AI depends on data. Interoperability determines whether AI models can access the breadth of clinical, financial, and social data they need. FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) R4 has become the US standard for health data exchange, mandated by CMS and ONC. Health systems that achieve FHIR maturity levels 3 and above can deploy AI at full fidelity. Those with batch-only ETL pipelines miss entire categories of clinical AI use cases.

INTEROPERABILITY STANDARDS ECOSYSTEM

HL7 FHIR R4:
- Current standard for US healthcare data exchange
- Mandated by CMS Patient Access Rule and Interoperability Rule
- Used by: Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH, all major EHRs
- Resource types: 156 standard resource types (Patient, Condition, Observation, Procedure, DiagnosticReport, etc.)

Da Vinci Project (HL7 accelerator for payer-provider exchange):
- Prior Authorization Support (PAS): provider-to-payer PA transactions
- Coverage Requirements Discovery (CRD): real-time PA checking at point of order
- Documentation Templates and Rules (DTR): structured PA documentation
- Clinical Data Exchange (CDex): retrieval of clinical documents for payer review
- Payers with Da Vinci in production: United (PAS live 2024), Humana (CRD live), BCBS plans (18 states)
- Providers with Da Vinci CRD: Epic (live), Oracle Health (pilot), MEDITECH (in development)

HL7 V2 (LEGACY BUT STILL DOMINANT):
- ADT (Admit/Discharge/Transfer): still the most common real-time event notification standard
- ORM/ORU (orders and results): laboratory and radiology results
- HL7 v2 interfaces per large health system: 1,840 average
- Migration cost from HL7 v2 to FHIR: $0.8-1.8M per health system
- Timeline: 2-4 years to fully migrate (HL7 v2 will not disappear before 2030)

USCDI (UNITED STATES CORE DATA FOR INTEROPERABILITY):
- ONC-mandated data set for patient data sharing
- USCDI v3 (2024): adds new data classes including SDOH, health status assessments
- Covered by EHR certification: all certified EHRs must support USCDI export
- Patient access: any patient can request their USCDI data via FHIR API

DICOM (imaging):
- Standard for medical imaging data exchange (radiology, pathology, cardiology)
- DICOM + FHIR ImagingStudy resource: enables AI radiology apps to access images via standard APIs
- DICOM viewers with FHIR integration: Ambra Health, Sectra, Intelerad

Does NOT support: bulk AI training, real-time streaming, payer data exchange

Cost: $0-$280,000 (mostly Epic build time)

Supports: AI model training on EHR data, population health analytics, Epic Cosmos participation
Timeline from Level 1: 3-6 months
Cost: $280,000-640,000 (data pipeline to AI platform)

LEVEL 3 — FHIR SUBSCRIPTION (REAL-TIME EVENTS):

Supports: sepsis AI, ED capacity AI, any real-time clinical AI
Timeline from Level 2: 6-12 months
Cost: $640,000-1.8M (streaming infrastructure + integration)

LEVEL 4 — DA VINCI PAYER INTEGRATION (CRD/DTR/PAS):

LEVEL 5 — FULL INTEROPERABILITY AI PLATFORM:
