VENDOR EVALUATION FRAMEWORK — HEALTHCARE AI

HOW TO EVALUATE AI VENDORS (ABARVA FRAMEWORK)

Dimension 1 — Clinical evidence:
FDA clearance or CE marking: does the AI have regulatory clearance for its claimed use?
Peer-reviewed outcomes: published clinical studies with health system (not vendor) authorship preferred
Reference sites: same size, same EHR, same payer mix as your organization?
Minimum acceptable evidence: 1 published study + 3 reference sites with measurable outcomes

Dimension 2 — Technical integration:
EHR compatibility (Epic, Oracle, MEDITECH): native integration vs. API vs. custom?
Integration cost: Epic-certified developer hours required (budget $180-320/hour)
Data requirements: batch vs. real-time? What data latency can the model tolerate?
Infrastructure: cloud (which provider)? HIPAA BAA in place? SOC 2 Type II?

Dimension 3 — Commercial terms:
Contract structure: SaaS subscription vs. outcome-based vs. per-transaction?
SLA definitions: are performance metrics defined in contract? What are the penalty clauses?
Implementation timeline: committed go-live date in contract?
Outcome commitments: does vendor commit to specific ROI or improvement metrics?
Exit terms: data portability on contract termination; transition period assistance

Dimension 4 — Vendor stability:
Funding/profitability: is vendor venture-backed? What is their runway? Risk of acquisition or shutdown?
Customer retention rate: > 90% annual retention = good; < 85% = warning sign
Market position: KLAS ranking (for clinical IT), Black Book ranking, reference volume
Acquisition risk: Epic, Oracle, and UHG/Optum actively acquire AI vendors; integration disruption after acquisition

Dimension 5 — Implementation support:
Implementation team dedicated vs. shared?
Training programme: hours, format, physician adoption support
Post-go-live support: customer success manager (dedicated or pooled)? Response SLA?
Ongoing optimization: does vendor offer quarterly model retraining? Performance reviews?

VENDOR PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT (ONGOING)

Industry standard: quarterly vendor performance reviews with scorecard
Metrics to track quarterly:
- Automation rate vs. contracted (PA automation, denial automation)
- Denial rate trend (baseline vs. current vs. contracted target)
- System uptime vs. SLA (> 99.5% expected for clinical AI)
- User adoption rate (% of eligible users actively using the tool)
- Model accuracy drift (re-run accuracy test quarterly; alert if > 5% degradation)
- Support ticket resolution time vs. SLA (< 4 hours for production-impacting issues)

Organizations that track all 6 metrics quarterly: 18%
Organizations with no formal vendor performance tracking: 44%
Impact: organisations tracking performance 2.1x more likely to enforce SLA penalties; 1.8x more likely to renew on favorable terms

- AI and analytics vendors: 14.2 average
- Patient engagement tools: 3.8 average (rationalize to 1-2)
- Population health platforms: 2.4 average (rationalize to 1)

Rationalization approach:
Step 1: AI-assisted spend analysis (identify overlap in functionality across 14+ vendors)

Step 3: Platform consolidation (replace multiple point solutions with unified platform where possible)
Step 4: Contract renegotiation (leverage consolidation to improve terms with retained vendors)
Step 5: Transition management (migrate data and workflows from deprecated tools; manage change)

Rationalization savings: 8-14% of total AI/analytics spend
