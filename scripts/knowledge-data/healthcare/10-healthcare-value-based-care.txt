RISK STRATIFICATION — DETAILED METHODOLOGY

RISK STRATIFICATION MODEL TYPES:
Claims-based only: widely available; 12-18 month lag; misses recent clinical events
EHR-based only: real-time; misses utilization outside health system
Claims + EHR (hybrid): best accuracy; requires payer data sharing agreement
Claims + EHR + SDOH (comprehensive): 18% higher AUC than claims-only; required for equity-focused programmes

HIGH-RISK PATIENT IDENTIFICATION (AI benchmarks):
AUC (Area Under the Curve) for best-in-class hybrid risk model: 0.84
AUC for claims-only model: 0.72
AUC for rule-based (high-utilizer only): 0.64
Precision at top 5% risk tier: AI identifies patients who will generate 38% of total spend
Recall at top 5% risk tier: AI correctly identifies 82% of actual high-cost patients before the fact
False discovery rate (patients in top tier who are not actually high-cost): 18% (acceptable for care management investment)

CARE MANAGEMENT AI WORKFLOW:
Step 1: AI risk stratification generates weekly priority list for care managers
Step 2: AI summarizes each high-risk patient's drivers (diagnoses, gaps, social risks, predicted cost)
Step 3: Care manager outreach via preferred channel (AI recommends phone/text/portal based on patient history)
Step 4: AI documents care plan and monitors adherence via EHR + RPM integration
Step 5: AI measures outcome: ED avoided, hospitalization avoided, care gap closed
