The adoption gap between large systems (61%) and community hospitals < 200 beds (18%) represents the largest opportunity surface in healthcare AI.

GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS IN CLINICAL AI

F007 — No physician champion at clinical AI launch: 62% end-user rejection rate without clinical champion; physicians disable or bypass AI tools
F041 — Alert fatigue from high false positive AI: when specificity < 85%, alert fatigue destroys adoption; 28% of clinical AI deployments are currently disabled due to this
F019 — Data latency makes real-time AI impossible: batch pipelines (18-36 hour latency) disqualify health systems from deploying sepsis, deterioration, and ED capacity AI
F034 — No baseline clinical metrics at programme start: outcomes from clinical AI cannot be attributed without pre-deployment baseline; required for regulatory justification and board reporting
F058 — Clinical AI purchased, not integrated: 44% of health systems have purchased clinical AI tools that are not integrated into EHR workflow; clinicians must access a separate application, driving abandonment

CLINICAL AI GOVERNANCE AND OVERSIGHT

CLINICAL AI COMMITTEE REQUIREMENTS:
Organizations with a dedicated Clinical AI Review Committee: 24%
Required members: CMO (chair), CNO, CIO, CDO, legal/compliance, bioethics, patient advocate
Review criteria: clinical evidence of effectiveness; FDA status; data requirements; bias testing; alert design; opt-out process; deactivation criteria
Committee review timeline for new clinical AI: 6-12 weeks (does not slow deployment if initiated early)
Clinical AI that bypasses committee review: 44% of deployed tools have no formal governance sign-off — liability exposure

POST-DEPLOYMENT MONITORING (best practice):
Model performance monitoring: AUC, sensitivity, specificity tracked monthly
Alert volume tracking: alerts per 1,000 patient-days (early warning of alert fatigue)
Override rate tracking: if override rate > 80%, AI needs recalibration or workflow redesign
Clinical outcome tracking: mortality, LOS, readmission rate compared to pre-AI baseline
Bias monitoring: quarterly subgroup performance analysis (race, sex, insurance, age)

CLINICAL AI DEPLOYMENT CHECKLIST:
Before go-live: baseline metrics locked, clinical champion identified, physician training complete, EHR workflow tested, governance approval obtained, vendor SLA signed, PHI/HIPAA compliance verified
At go-live: parallel running period (AI alerts generated but not shown to clinicians for first 2 weeks — staff observe accuracy before taking action); rapid response team for workflow issues
Post-go-live 30 days: adoption rate measurement; end-user feedback; alert volume review; first accuracy audit
Post-go-live 90 days: ROI first measurement; clinical outcome first measurement; decision to expand or adjust
