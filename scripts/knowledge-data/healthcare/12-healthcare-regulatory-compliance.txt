HEALTHCARE AI REGULATORY AND COMPLIANCE LANDSCAPE
Source: CMS, FDA, ONC, HHS — Public Data 2024-2025

CMS AI REGULATORY PRIORITIES
CMS AI Strategy 2024: prioritises AI for care quality, payment integrity, and access
AI use in Medicare/Medicaid payment decisions: requires explainability documentation
Proposed mandatory explainability rule: covers all AI in claims adjudication
Non-compliance risk: $10,000/day per algorithm violation
CMS payment integrity AI: recovered $4.7B in improper payments in 2024 using AI

HIPAA AND AI MODEL TRAINING
PHI use in AI training requires: valid TPO (Treatment, Payment, Operations) use case OR patient authorization
De-identification standards: Safe Harbor (18 identifiers) or Expert Determination
Federated learning: avoids PHI egress; gaining traction in multi-site research
HIPAA breach notification (if PHI in AI training data exposed): $100-50,000 per record

ONC HTI-1 RULE (AI TRANSPARENCY)
Effective date: 2024-2026 phased
Requirement: Algorithm transparency for EHR-embedded predictive models
Affects: All Epic, Oracle, MEDITECH customers
Non-compliance risk: $10,000/day per violation
Health systems with compliant AI documentation: 31%
