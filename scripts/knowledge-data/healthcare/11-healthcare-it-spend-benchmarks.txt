HEALTHCARE IT SPEND AND BUDGET BENCHMARKS
Source: HIMSS Analytics 2025, Gartner Healthcare IT Spending Forecast 2025, Advisory Board IT Benchmarking Survey, AbarVa Engagement Data

HEALTHCARE IT SPEND — HEADLINE BENCHMARKS

Total US healthcare IT market size (2025): $189B
Healthcare IT spend as % of total healthcare spending: 4.2%
Annual healthcare IT spend growth rate: 8.4% CAGR (2023-2026)
AI-specific IT spend within healthcare IT: 12% of total ($22.7B in 2025; projected $44B by 2028)

IT SPEND AS % OF NET PATIENT REVENUE BY SYSTEM TYPE:
Large IDN (> $5B revenue): 3.8-5.2%, median 4.3%
Mid-size health system ($1B-$5B): 3.2-4.6%, median 3.8%
Community hospital (< $1B): 2.8-4.1%, median 3.4%
Academic Medical Center: 4.8-6.2%, median 5.4%
Children's hospitals: 4.1-5.8%, median 4.9%
Critical access hospitals: 3.1-5.4% (wide variance driven by EHR modernization cycles)
Health plans: 2.8-4.2% of premium revenue

ABSOLUTE IT SPEND BY SIZE:
Health system > $5B revenue: $190-260M annually
Health system $1-5B: $38-190M annually
Community hospital $300M-$1B: $10-38M annually
Independent physician group (100 providers): $1.4-3.2M annually
Health plan (1M+ members): $28-84M annually

IT BUDGET ALLOCATION (% of total IT budget — typical breakdown):

Infrastructure and operations (servers, network, storage, cloud): 38-45%
Applications (EHR, ancillary, clinical): 28-35%
Security and compliance: 10-14%
Analytics and AI: 6-12% (top performers: 14-18%)
End-user computing (devices, helpdesk): 6-9%
Innovation and emerging tech: 2-5%

TOP-PERFORMER VS. LAGGARD COMPARISON:
Top quartile CIO (measured by outcomes): 14-18% of IT budget on AI/analytics
Bottom quartile: < 5% on AI/analytics
Outcome gap: 2x more AI use cases in production at 24 months
Revenue impact gap: top-quartile IT organizations generate $28-44M more per $1B revenue from IT investments than bottom quartile

EPIC-SPECIFIC COST BENCHMARKS

Epic license and maintenance as % of total IT spend (Epic-live organizations): 18-28%
Epic implementation spend per hospital (mid-size, 200-400 beds): $12-22M
Epic implementation spend (AMC or complex multispecialty): $38-68M
Epic optimization post-go-live (ongoing annual investment): $3.8-6.4M per year
Epic go-live year IT spend spike: 1.8-2.4x baseline during go-live year (one-time)
Normalization timeline post-go-live: 18-24 months to return to baseline IT spend trajectory
Epic upgrade annual cost (large IDN): $1.2-2.8M per major release cycle

EPIC TOTAL COST OF OWNERSHIP (10-YEAR VIEW):
400-bed community hospital: $42-68M total Epic TCO over 10 years
Large IDN (10 hospitals): $280-480M total Epic TCO over 10 years
Competing EHR (Oracle Health Cerner): approximately 18-24% lower TCO but lower KLAS performance scores
MEDITECH Expanse: 38-44% lower TCO for community hospitals vs. Epic

CYBERSECURITY SPEND

Healthcare cybersecurity spend as % of IT budget: 10-14%
Recommended spend (NIST Cybersecurity Framework for healthcare): 12-15%
Organizations meeting or exceeding recommendation: 38%

BREACH COST BENCHMARKS:
Average healthcare data breach cost (IBM 2024): $10.9M (highest of any industry — 13th consecutive year healthcare leads)
Average ransomware recovery cost: $1.4-4.2M per incident (does not include reputational and competitive loss)
Change Healthcare cyberattack (February 2024): $872M initial UnitedHealth Group disclosed cost; broader ecosystem impact estimated $4-6B
Scripps Health cyberattack (2021): $112M recovery cost
CommonSpirit Health cyberattack (2022): $160M+ EHR downtime and recovery
HIPAA breach notification costs alone (legal, notification, credit monitoring): $400K-2.1M per incident

CYBERSECURITY INVESTMENT BREAKDOWN:
Endpoint detection and response: 24% of cybersecurity budget
Network security (NGFW, SIEM): 22%
IAM (identity and access management): 18%
IoMT (medical device security): 14% (growing fastest — up from 8% in 2022)
Data protection and DLP: 12%
GRC (governance, risk, compliance): 10%

ZERO-TRUST ARCHITECTURE ADOPTION:
Health systems with zero-trust architecture: 22%
Health systems with partial zero-trust (segmented networks): 44%
Health systems with legacy flat network architecture: 34% (highest risk; ransomware can spread laterally)
Zero-trust implementation cost for large health system: $12-28M over 3 years
Zero-trust ROI: reduces successful ransomware attack probability 68%; at $2.8M average ransomware cost, break-even within 2-4 years

CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE SPEND

Cloud as % of total IT budget: 28% (growing from 18% in 2021 — fastest category growth)
Cloud-primary organizations: 18% of health systems
Hybrid cloud: 48%
Predominantly on-premise: 34%
5-year projection: cloud to represent 42% of IT budget by 2028

CLOUD SPEND BENCHMARKS:
Annual cloud spend — health system > $5B: $42-68M
Annual cloud spend — health system $1-5B: $12-28M
Annual cloud spend — community hospital: $3.2-8.4M
Annual cloud spend per 100 staffed beds: $1.4-2.8M

CLOUD WASTE AND OPTIMIZATION:
Average cloud overspend (no FinOps programme): 36-44% above optimal
Organizations with formal FinOps practice: 24%
FinOps annual savings (large IDN): $4.2-8.8M
Reserved instance savings vs. on-demand pricing: 38-52%
Organizations with > 50% reserved instance coverage: 31%
Spot/preemptible instances for AI training: saves 60-80% vs. on-demand; used by 28% of AI-active systems

TECHNOLOGY DEBT

Average healthcare technology debt (applications + infrastructure): $48M per $1B net patient revenue
IT systems / applications older than 10 years: 41% of all applications
Average age of clinical application portfolio: 8.2 years
Annual technical debt interest (excess maintenance cost): $6.8-12.4M per $1B revenue

TECHNOLOGY DEBT BY CATEGORY:
EHR and clinical applications: 34% of tech debt (most strategic; Epic migrations drive this)
Data infrastructure (legacy data warehouses): 28% of tech debt
Integration layer (HL7 v2, legacy interfaces): 18% of tech debt
Business applications (ERP, supply chain): 12% of tech debt
Other: 8%

GENOME PATTERN F003 — PLATFORM EOL IMPACT:
Organizations with planned platform migrations (proactive): 62% — average cost 22-28% of IT budget
Organizations with forced/unplanned migrations (reactive): 38% — average cost 38-47% premium above planned
Unplanned migration triggers: vendor end-of-support, payer audit requirement, security breach forcing migration

VENDOR SPEND CONSOLIDATION OPPORTUNITY

Average number of clinical application vendors per large IDN: 84
Top quartile (optimized, post-rationalization): 54
Minimum practical floor (clinical diversity): 40-50 vendors even in best-optimized systems

VENDOR RATIONALIZATION DATA:
Vendor rationalization savings: 8-14% of application spend
AI-assisted vendor rationalization timeline: 12-18 months (vs. 36-48 months manually)
Annual application rationalization savings (large IDN, $60M application spend): $3.2-6.8M
Organizations that have completed formal vendor rationalization in past 5 years: 28%

APPLICATION OVERLAP EXAMPLES:
Patient engagement: average IDN has 3.8 patient engagement / portal tools (Epic MyChart + 2.8 others)
Population health: average IDN has 2.4 population health platforms (before rationalization)
Analytics/BI: average 3.6 BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Epic Radar, others)
Telehealth: average 2.1 telehealth platforms (not yet rationalized)
Rationalization opportunity (patient engagement alone): $1.2-2.8M annually

AI SPEND BENCHMARKS AND TRENDS

Current healthcare AI spend by category:
- Clinical AI (purchased from vendors): 38%
- Analytics and population health AI: 28%
- RCM / administrative AI: 22%
- Infrastructure AI (cloud AI services): 12%

AI CONTRACT STRUCTURES IN HEALTHCARE:
SaaS subscription (per user, per month): 44%
% of documented savings (outcome-based): 28%
Flat enterprise license: 18%
Per-transaction (per claim, per PA request): 10%

AI CONTRACT VALUE RANGES:
Small point solution (single use case, community hospital): $180,000-480,000/year
Mid-scale AI platform (3-5 use cases, mid-size system): $480,000-1.8M/year
Enterprise AI platform (IDN, full portfolio): $1.8-8.8M/year
Mega contract (AMC or top-10 IDN): $8.8-24M/year (typically multi-year)

AI ROI VALIDATION AT CONTRACT RENEWAL:
Contracts with defined ROI metrics that vendors must demonstrate: 34%
Contracts with outcome-based pricing (pay only if results achieved): 18%
Contracts with unlimited term (no renewal trigger unless health system cancels): 44% (dangerous — creates vendor complacency; Genome Pattern F011)

IT GOVERNANCE AND BUDGET ALLOCATION

DECISION RIGHTS:
CIOs with full budget authority over AI spend: 44%
CIOs where AI spend is split with CDO or CMO: 38%
Organizations where AI budget is allocated by service line (no central IT control): 18%
Outcome: fragmented AI budget allocation leads to duplicate tools, integration gaps, and 38% higher total AI spend for equivalent capability

CAPEX vs. OPEX SHIFT:
Healthcare IT CAPEX (on-premise): declining 8% per year
Healthcare IT OPEX (cloud, SaaS): growing 18% per year
2025 CAPEX/OPEX split: 42% CAPEX, 58% OPEX
2028 projected: 28% CAPEX, 72% OPEX
Implication: budget models must shift to accommodate OPEX growth; CMS reimbursement does not differentiate CAPEX vs. OPEX for depreciation purposes

BENCHMARK COMPARISON — AI INVESTMENT VS. RETURN:
Health systems in top AI investment quartile: $3.8-5.2% of IT budget on AI
Health systems in bottom AI investment quartile: < 2.1% of IT budget on AI
Revenue performance gap: top-quartile AI investors generate $12-24M more per $1B revenue from AI-driven improvements vs. bottom quartile
Conclusion: AI IT investment has a measurable and significant ROI; the investment gap between leaders and laggards is widening

IT INVESTMENT PRIORITY TRENDS (2025-2027)

INVESTMENT INCREASING (growing share of IT budget):
AI and machine learning: growing from 6-12% to projected 14-20% by 2027
Cloud migration and FinOps: growing from 28% to projected 38% of IT spend
Cybersecurity (IoMT, zero-trust): growing from 10-14% to projected 16-20%
Interoperability and API management: growing with FHIR mandate compliance
Ambient documentation and clinician-facing AI: high priority in clinical operations budgets

INVESTMENT DECREASING (shrinking share):
On-premise infrastructure (servers, storage, networking): declining 8% per year as cloud absorbs
On-premise data center footprint: 34% still primary; shrinking; many divesting owned data centers
Legacy application maintenance (EHR versions > 5 years old): declining as migrations complete
Mainframe computing: < 4% of health systems (extremely legacy); declining

BOARD-LEVEL IT GOVERNANCE:
Health systems with a board-level technology or digital transformation committee: 44%
CEOs who list IT/digital as top 3 strategic priority: 62%
Health systems with IT included in strategic planning cycles: 78%
IT capital vs. operating budget approval threshold: most systems require board approval for capital IT > $5M
AI programme board approval threshold: $2-5M (lower than traditional IT due to novelty risk concern)

RETURN ON IT INVESTMENT — EVIDENCE BASE

Industry average ROI on healthcare IT investment (all categories): 160% over 5 years
AI-specific IT investment ROI: 210-380% over 5 years (varies by use case portfolio)
Epic ROI (measured 5 years post-go-live): 180% (KLAS Epic outcomes data, 2024)
Cloud migration ROI: 280% over 5 years (IDC study, healthcare cohort)
Cybersecurity ROI (preventing one major breach): $10.9M avoided cost vs. $1.4-2.8M annual investment — 4-8x ROI per prevented incident

Healthcare IT ROI key driver: IT investment enables clinical and operational AI that generates patient revenue and cost reduction. IT spend is not a cost center — it is a capability investment. Organizations that treat IT as overhead rather than strategic investment show 2.4x higher operational cost growth over 5 years.
