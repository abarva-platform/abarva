HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY STACK PATTERNS AND AI READINESS
Source: KLAS Research, HIMSS Analytics 2025, AbarVa Technology Assessment Data

COMMON HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY STACK PATTERNS

Pattern A: Epic-Centric (37% of large IDNs)
EHR: Epic | Data: Azure Synapse or Epic Chronicles | BI: Tableau/PowerBI | AI: Azure OpenAI + Epic AI
AI readiness score: 58 (medium) | Cloud: Azure primary
Key strength: tight EHR integration | Key weakness: Epic dependency for all data pipelines

Pattern B: Best-of-Breed Clinical (28% of large IDNs)
EHR: Epic + multiple ancillary systems | Data: Snowflake or Databricks | BI: Tableau | AI: vendor-specific
AI readiness score: 51 (medium) | Cloud: Multi-cloud (AWS + Azure)
Key strength: clinical flexibility | Key weakness: integration complexity

Pattern C: Cloud-Native Emerging (12% of IDNs)
EHR: Epic or Oracle | Data: Databricks lakehouse | BI: Databricks SQL/Power BI | AI: Azure OpenAI
AI readiness score: 72 (high) | Cloud: Azure or AWS-primary
Key strength: AI-ready architecture | Key weakness: talent and change management gaps

Pattern D: Legacy/On-Premise (23% of IDNs)
EHR: MEDITECH or older Epic | Data: on-premise SQL Server or Teradata | BI: Crystal Reports/SSRS
AI readiness score: 28 (low) | Key risk: platform EOL, AI deployment impossible

INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE BENCHMARKS
Average number of interfaces (HL7 v2) per health system: 1,840
Active FHIR integrations: 94 average (growing 38% per year)
Interface engine market: Rhapsody (32%), Mirth Connect (28%), InterSystems (22%)
API-first architecture adoption: 22% of health systems
Average integration maintenance cost: $0.8-1.4M per $1B revenue annually

DATA PIPELINE MATURITY
Batch ETL only (no streaming): 44%
Hybrid (batch + limited streaming): 38%
Real-time streaming data pipeline: 18%
Average data latency to analytics: 18-36 hours (batch), 2-8 seconds (streaming)
Data quality SLA achievement: 62% of organisations meet internal data quality targets

ANALYTICS MATURITY LEVELS
Descriptive only (what happened): 34% of health systems
Descriptive + diagnostic (why it happened): 42%
Predictive (what will happen): 18%
Prescriptive (what should we do): 6%
AI-driven autonomous (act on predictions): 2%

TECH DEBT QUANTIFICATION
Average healthcare technology debt: $48M per $1B revenue
Systems with quantified tech debt assessment: 22%
Annual tech debt interest (maintenance cost premium): $6.8-12.4M per $1B revenue
Estimated time to resolve ($1B health system): 4-6 years at adequate investment
Tech debt that blocks AI deployment: 61% of assessed tech debt items
