MEDICARE ADVANTAGE — MARKET OVERVIEW

Medicare Advantage is the fastest-growing and most AI-relevant segment of US healthcare financing. At 52% penetration of all Medicare beneficiaries in 2025 (up from 28% in 2015), MA now represents the majority of Medicare. The financial model of MA — capitated per-member-per-month payments adjusted for risk — creates structural AI incentives and tension points not present in fee-for-service. Understanding MA AI dynamics is essential for any health system or payer operating in 2025.

RISK ADJUSTMENT AND HCC CODING

RISK ADJUSTMENT FUNDAMENTALS:
MA payment formula: base rate x risk adjustment factor (RAF) x location-based adjustments
RAF range: 0.62 (healthy, low-risk) to 3.8+ (high-complexity, multiple conditions)
Average RAF for MA beneficiary: 1.18 (2025)
1 RAF point = approximately $11,000 annual per-member payment (2025 base rate)
RAF per beneficiary difference between best and worst documentation: 0.3-0.6 (translates to $3,300-6,600 per patient per year)

HCC (HIERARCHICAL CONDITION CATEGORY) CODING AI:
HCC coding accuracy improvement with AI: +12-18 percentage points
AI-assisted HCC identification: finds conditions documented in clinical notes but not coded as HCCs
HCC undercoding cost per patient annually: $1,200-2,400 (provider loses this revenue if RAF is understated)
AI average RAF improvement: +0.08-0.12 per patient
Revenue impact per 0.1 RAF improvement (per 10,000 patients): $1.8M annually

PAYER RISK ADJUSTMENT AI:

Major payers conducting AI chart reviews: UHG/Optum, Humana, Aetna
DOJ/OIG MA audit focus (2024-2025): MA plans submitting unsupported diagnosis codes (AI-generated codes without clinical documentation)
Payer AI risk adjustment audit risk: 3 major plans under DOJ investigation for AI-assisted upcoding (2025)

PROVIDER HCC AI:

GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS IN MA AND PAYER AI

F011 — Payer SLA non-enforcement in MA: providers that do not enforce MA contract SLAs lose $4.2-12.1M annually in avoidable denials, slow processing, and undervalued contracts
F019 — HCC data fragmentation: payer claims data and provider EHR data not connected; AI cannot identify undercoded HCCs without clinical + claims data integration
F034 — No baseline MA financial metrics: without Day 0 measurement of MA denial rate, RAF score, and shared savings performance, MA AI ROI cannot be demonstrated or contested
F027 — MA coding and billing system fragmented: separate systems for FFS billing vs. MA value-based reporting; AI cannot reconcile; HCC submissions missed
F007 — No value-based care executive sponsor: MA and VBC AI requires VP of Value-Based Care or similar role; when IT or finance owns VBC AI without clinical/strategic sponsorship, 72% of programmes underperform
