HEALTHCARE CDO ROLE AND AI GOVERNANCE BENCHMARKS
Source: CHIME CDO Survey 2025, AbarVa Genome Data

CDO ROLE PREVALENCE
Health systems with CDO or Chief AI Officer: 44%
Health systems with CDO in place before AI programme: 18%
Health systems with CDO vacancy during AI programme: 34%
Average CDO tenure: 2.8 years (lower than other C-suite roles)

CDO IMPACT ON AI OUTCOMES (AbarVa Genome)
Systems with CDO at AI launch: 84% programme success rate
Systems without CDO at AI launch: 38% programme success rate
Delta: 46 percentage points
CDO with budget authority (not just advisory role): 91% success rate

CDO REPORTING STRUCTURE
CDO reporting to CEO: 41% (most effective)
CDO reporting to CIO: 32% (mixed outcomes)
CDO reporting to COO: 18% (operational focus)
CDO reporting to CFO: 9% (least effective for AI)

AI GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE PREVALENCE
Health systems with formal AI governance committee: 31%
AI committee meeting frequency:
- Monthly: 44% of those with committees
- Quarterly: 38%
- Ad hoc: 18%
AI governance committees with budget authority: 28%

GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE IMPACT
Systems with AI committee: 2.1x higher AI programme success rate
Systems with AI committee + budget authority: 2.8x higher success rate
Systems with quarterly-only review: 38% of decisions reversed within 90 days
Systems with monthly review + consequences: 91% decision adherence

AI POLICY COVERAGE GAPS (2025)
Health systems with comprehensive AI use policy: 38%
Health systems with no formal AI policy: 34%
Health systems with policy for EHR AI but not general AI: 28%
Most common policy gap: non-FDA-cleared AI in clinical workflows (61% have no policy)
Liability exposure from unvetted clinical AI: $2.4-8.8M per incident estimate

AI ETHICS AND BIAS MONITORING
Health systems with AI bias monitoring programme: 14%
AI models audited for bias within 12 months of deployment: 28%
Racial/ethnic disparity in AI performance (unmonitored models): found in 34% audited
CMS bias equity requirements for AI in value-based contracts: effective 2026

SHADOW AI RISK
Health systems with employees using unauthorised AI tools: 91%
Most common unauthorised AI: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot in clinical documentation
PHI exposure risk from unauthorised AI: $100-50,000 per record (HIPAA)
Health systems with shadow AI monitoring: 18%
