HEALTHCARE PATIENT ACCESS AND ENGAGEMENT AI BENCHMARKS
Source: Press Ganey Patient Experience Survey 2025, MGMA Physician Practice Benchmark, Epic User Group Research 2025, AbarVa Engagement Data

PATIENT ACCESS — MARKET CONTEXT

Patient access is the first touchpoint of the healthcare experience and one of the highest-leverage areas for AI. Scheduling friction, no-shows, and call center inefficiency collectively cost the US healthcare system $48-84B annually. AI in patient access reduces wait times, improves schedule utilization, reduces no-shows, and converts more inquiries to kept appointments. ROI is measured in months, not years.

GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS IN PATIENT ACCESS

F019 — Patient data fragmentation: scheduling, billing, insurance, and clinical data not connected; AI cannot build complete patient profile for personalization; no-show prediction accuracy drops 28pp
F041 — Patient access AI pilot succeeds in one clinic, fails enterprise-wide: workflow standardization required before scaling AI scheduling; 44% of patient access AI fails this transition
F058 — No patient access champion (operational leader): without a VP Patient Experience or COO sponsoring AI, front-desk staff resist automation; adoption < 18% without leadership mandate
F034 — No baseline patient access metrics: if 3NAA, no-show rate, call resolution rate not measured on Day 0, AI vendor claims on improvement cannot be verified
