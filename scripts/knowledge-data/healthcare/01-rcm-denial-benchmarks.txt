DENIAL MANAGEMENT WORKFLOW — DETAILED PROCESS BENCHMARKS

CLAIM LIFECYCLE STAGES AND AI TOUCHPOINTS:
Pre-registration (eligibility + benefit verification): AI checks insurance eligibility at scheduling; reduces eligibility denials from 19% to 8% of total denials
Pre-service authorization: AI submits PA request at time of order; reduces PA-related denials 28-44%
Charge capture: AI identifies missing charges within 48 hours of service; reduces missing charge rate from 4.2% to 1.8%
Coding: AI-assisted or autonomous coding assigns ICD-10 and CPT codes; reduces coding error rate from 8.4% to 3.2%
Claim scrubbing: AI pre-screens claim for payer-specific edit rules before submission; reduces rejected claims 38-52%
Electronic submission: FHIR-based or 837 EDI submission; AI monitors acknowledgment (999/277)
Claim status tracking: AI monitors every claim daily; flags claims not adjudicated within expected timeframe
Denial receipt: AI classifies denial by root cause within 2 minutes of 835 remittance receipt
Denial triage: AI scores each denial by overturn probability and revenue value; prioritizes work queue
Appeal: AI generates appeal letter with supporting documentation; success rate 71% vs. 41% manual
Collection: patient statement or secondary payer submission; AI determines optimal collection pathway
