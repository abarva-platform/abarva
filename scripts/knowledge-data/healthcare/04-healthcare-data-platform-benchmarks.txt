HEALTHCARE DATA PLATFORM AND CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE BENCHMARKS
Source: HIMSS Analytics 2025, Gartner Healthcare IT Survey

DATA READINESS BENCHMARKS (AbarVa 0-100 scale)
Distribution across assessed health systems:
- Score > 70 (AI-ready): 8% of health systems
- Score 50-70 (foundation building): 29%
- Score 35-50 (significant gaps): 41%
- Score < 35 (pre-foundation): 22%

Impact on AI programme outcomes:
- Score > 65: 2x use case deployment in 24 months
- Score 50-64: median programme outcomes
- Score 35-49: 44% higher programme cost than projected
- Score < 35: 68% of AI initiatives stall at proof-of-concept

TOP DATA READINESS BLOCKERS (by frequency in health systems)
1. No enterprise data catalogue: 67% of assessed organisations
2. Epic/EHR data not integrated with enterprise data warehouse: 61%
3. Master Patient Index (MPI) duplicate rate above 3%: 54%
4. Multiple conflicting definitions for the same metric: 51%
5. No self-service analytics for clinical/operational users: 48%
6. Data quality score below 70% on critical datasets: 44%
7. No real-time data pipeline (batch-only): 41%

MPI QUALITY BENCHMARKS
Average MPI duplicate rate (no active deduplication): 4.8%
Top quartile (with MDM programme): 1.2%
Industry median: 3.1%
AI model accuracy degradation at 3% duplicate rate: 40-60%
Cost to remediate MPI (per 1M records): $0.8-1.4M
Ongoing annual deduplication cost: $0.3-0.6M per 1M records

CLOUD PLATFORM ADOPTION IN HEALTHCARE
Microsoft Azure: 37% primary cloud (Epic partnership, HIPAA tooling)
AWS: 31% primary cloud (strong ML/AI tooling)
GCP: 19% primary cloud (BigQuery analytics strength)
Multi-cloud: 76% use at least two providers

Cloud spend as % of total IT budget: 28%
Average annual cloud spend ($5B+ health system): $42-68M
Organisations with formal FinOps: 24%
Average cloud waste without FinOps: 36-44%

DATA WAREHOUSE / LAKEHOUSE PATTERNS
On-premise data warehouse (Teradata, IBM): 41% still primary
Snowflake: 24% deployed (fastest growing)
Databricks: 18% deployed (strongest for ML workloads)
Azure Synapse: 12%
AWS Redshift: 8%

Teradata end-of-life risk: average health system Teradata installation age 11.4 years
Cost to migrate from Teradata to cloud DW: $3.8-9.2M per $1B revenue

SELF-SERVICE ANALYTICS MATURITY
Health systems with mature self-service BI: 21%
Average BI tools in use (per system): 3.6 (fragmented)
Tableau penetration: 44%
Power BI penetration: 38%
Epic Radar/Reporting Workbench as primary: 29%
Organisations with consistent metric definitions across tools: 19%
