Factor 2 — Clean data foundation established first: 2.8x higher ROI
Factor 3 — Baseline metrics locked and signed Day 0: required for any ROI claim
Factor 4 — Phased rollout (1 use case at a time): 2.1x less likely to be abandoned
Factor 5 — Vendor outcome accountability clauses: 1.9x better vendor performance
Factor 6 — CDO or CDIO role filled before launch: 84% programme success rate
