RETAIL AI PEER OUTCOME BENCHMARKS (ANONYMISED)
Source: AbarVa Engagement Data 2023-2025, NRF AI Case Studies 2025, McKinsey Retail AI Outcomes Database

OVERVIEW

The following case studies represent anonymised retail and CPG client outcomes from AbarVa engagements and public benchmarks. They are presented to illustrate realistic transformation timelines, investment levels, ROI sequencing, and common pattern matches to the AbarVa Genome failure and success patterns. Peer outcomes are the single most persuasive element of a retail AI business case — CFOs and boards respond to evidence that comparable retailers have achieved specific ROI at specific investment levels, more than to generic industry statistics.

CASE 1: SPECIALTY APPAREL RETAILER ($4.2B REVENUE)

SITUATION:
- E-commerce penetration: 29% (vs. 44% strategic target)
- Loyalty fragmented across 3 separate programmes (3 separate databases, no unified view)
- Demand forecast accuracy: 74% at SKU/store level
- Customer duplicate records: 28% (unresolved identities across store, e-com, loyalty)
- SAP ECC in use; S/4HANA migration planned but not started

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Phase 1 (months 1-6): AI demand forecasting (Blue Yonder); parallel data quality sprint
- Phase 2 (months 7-12): CDP deployment (Salesforce Data Cloud); identity resolution
- Phase 3 (months 13-18): AI loyalty personalisation; unified loyalty programme launch

INVESTMENT:
- Total programme investment: $8.4M over 18 months
- Phase 1 (demand forecasting + data): $2.8M
- Phase 2 (CDP + identity): $3.2M
- Phase 3 (loyalty AI + programme): $2.4M

OUTCOMES AT 24 MONTHS:
- Demand forecast accuracy: 74% → 91%
- E-commerce penetration: 29% → 36%
- Customer duplicate rate: 28% → 3.4% (post-identity resolution)
- Loyalty unified: 3 programmes → 1 (18M unified profiles)
- Return rate: -18 percentage points (AI sizing recommendation)
- Working capital improvement: $82M (inventory reduction from forecast accuracy)
- ROI: 4.2x at 24 months

KEY LESSONS:
- Demand forecasting first forced SKU data quality fix that unlocked all subsequent AI
- CDP deployment before personalisation was critical — avoided $2.4M wasted investment
- Return reduction from AI sizing was unexpected high-value outcome, not in original business case

CASE 2: OMNICHANNEL DEPARTMENT STORE ($8.8B REVENUE)

SITUATION:
- SAP ECC with end-of-support risk; projected 38-month migration to S/4HANA
- Combined ratio (C/I) at 72% (operational efficiency below target)
- Operating cost ratio 3.4pp above best-in-class peers
- AI programme stalled: waiting for SAP S/4HANA migration to complete before starting AI
- 22 months remaining to 2027 SAP ECC end-of-support

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Decision: begin AI programme in parallel with SAP migration (not sequentially)
- Month 1-6: demand forecasting AI deployed on existing ECC data pipelines (Blue Yonder)
- Month 6-38: SAP migration continues in parallel
- Month 12-24: supply chain AI, pricing AI activated on existing data foundation
- Month 38: SAP S/4HANA go-live; AI migrated to native S/4HANA data layer

INVESTMENT:
- SAP migration programme: $88M (38 months)
- AI programme (parallel): $4.2M
- Data platform upgrade (Snowflake on Azure): $3.8M

OUTCOMES:
- C/I improvement: 72% → 66.2% (5.8 percentage point improvement)
- Operating cost ratio: -18% (vs. -11% projected; exceeded plan)
- Demand forecast accuracy: 74% → 89%
- AI ROI positive: month 14 (before SAP go-live)
- Value captured by starting AI early vs. waiting for SAP: 22 months of AI value ($18.4M)
- Total programme ROI at 36 months: 2.8x (SAP + AI combined)

KEY LESSONS:
- "Start AI before SAP goes live" captured $18.4M in value that waiting-for-SAP would have foregone
- S/4HANA + Joule AI activated at go-live accelerated financial close from 8 to 3 days
- AI business case was approved faster when framed as "AI platform investment" not "SAP upgrade"

CASE 3: GROCERY RETAILER ($12B REVENUE)

SITUATION:
- OTIF: 74% (vs. 92% retailer demand / Walmart mandate at 95%)
- Shrink: 1.8% of revenue
- Labour schedule compliance: 68%
- Predictive scheduling law exposure: 7 states with advance notice requirements
- Food waste: 4.2% of fresh category revenue

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Phase 1 (months 1-8): AI replenishment (Relex Solutions) + food waste AI
- Phase 2 (months 6-14): CV loss prevention (3 urban market pilot, 28 stores)
- Phase 3 (months 10-18): AI workforce scheduling (UKG); predictive scheduling compliance

INVESTMENT:
- Total programme investment: $12.4M over 18 months
- Relex AI (replenishment + fresh AI): $4.8M
- CV loss prevention (28 pilot stores at $62K/store): $1.7M
- UKG AI scheduling: $1.8M
- Data infrastructure upgrades: $4.1M

OUTCOMES AT 24 MONTHS:
- OTIF: 74% → 91% (+17pp — above 90% target)
- Shrink: 1.8% → 1.1% (pilot stores: 0.82%)
- Labour cost: -9% (without headcount reduction)
- Food waste: 4.2% → 2.8% of fresh revenue
- Predictive scheduling compliance: 68% → 98% (avoiding $2.4M annual penalty exposure)
- AI ROI: 3.8x at 24 months

KEY LESSONS:
- OTIF improvement eliminated $22M in annual retailer chargeback risk
- CV pilot in 28 stores generated sufficient shrink data to build case for full 640-store rollout ($39.7M capital plan approved)
- Labour cost reduction of 9% without headcount cuts required explicit communications campaign to avoid union conflict

CASE 4: CPG GLOBAL MANUFACTURER ($18B REVENUE)

SITUATION:
- 40% of trade promotions losing money
- SKU master data quality: 68% accuracy across 3.8M product records
- 5 separate demand planning systems (post-acquisition)
- Trade spend: $3.6-5.0B annually
- S&OP cycle: 6 weeks (too slow for market-responsive planning)

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Phase 1 (months 1-4): SKU master data quality sprint (AI deduplication and enrichment)
- Phase 2 (months 3-10): Trade promotion AI (o9 Solutions) on clean data
- Phase 3 (months 8-18): Demand sensing AI; S&OP cycle reduction to 2 weeks

INVESTMENT:
- Total programme: $4.8M
- Data quality sprint: $1.2M
- o9 Solutions trade promotion AI: $2.4M
- Demand sensing module: $1.2M

OUTCOMES:
- Profitable promotions: 60% → 74% (+14pp — above 12pp benchmark)
- SKU data quality: 68% → 94%
- S&OP cycle time: 6 weeks → 2.2 weeks
- Annual trade promotion savings: $48M (on $3.6B base)
- Demand sensing accuracy: 74% → 88%
- ROI: 12.4x at 24 months (extremely high ROI due to scale of trade spend base)

KEY LESSONS:
- Data quality sprint was non-negotiable prerequisite — trade promotion AI deployed on 68% data quality would have generated wrong recommendations
- 14pp promotion improvement exceeded the 12pp benchmark by 2pp — attributable to the data quality fix making the AI model more accurate
- S&OP cycle reduction unlocked additional $12M in inventory carrying cost savings not in original business case

CASE 5: E-COMMERCE PURE-PLAY ($2.4B REVENUE)

SITUATION:
- Return rate: 31% (highest in peer group; 7pp above average)
- CAC rising: +22% year-over-year
- Personalisation: below target (28% personalisation coverage vs. 80% target)
- Customer identity: 22% duplicate records (app, web, email not unified)
- CLV prediction: not operational

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Phase 1 (months 1-4): AI sizing and fit (True Fit integration) — fastest ROI, no CDP required
- Phase 2 (months 3-8): CDP deployment (Segment) + identity resolution
- Phase 3 (months 7-12): AI personalisation (Dynamic Yield) on CDP foundation; CLV modelling

INVESTMENT:
- Total programme: $2.1M
- True Fit sizing AI: $480K
- Segment CDP: $640K
- Dynamic Yield personalisation: $680K
- CLV modelling (Databricks): $300K

OUTCOMES AT 18 MONTHS:
- Return rate: 31% → 17% (-14pp from AI sizing)
- Conversion rate: +22% (from AI personalisation)
- CAC: -18% (CLV-targeted acquisition + lookalike modelling)
- Personalisation coverage: 28% → 84%
- Customer duplicates: 22% → 2.8%
- CLV prediction accuracy: 81%
- ROI: 5.4x at 18 months (fastest ROI in portfolio)

KEY LESSONS:
- AI sizing (True Fit) delivered ROI in month 3 — fastest single-use-case payback
- Combining CDP + CLV targeting for acquisition reduced CAC 18% while maintaining LTV targets
- Returns reduction was the unexpected compounding benefit: $9.2M per year in processing cost savings

CASE 6: FASHION RETAILER ($3.8B REVENUE, 14 COUNTRIES)

SITUATION:
- Markdown write-offs: 12% of seasonal inventory value (above 8-10% benchmark)

SOLUTION DEPLOYED:
- Phase 1 (months 1-6): Markdown optimisation AI (Kire Technology); real-time rate-of-sale monitoring
- Phase 2 (months 4-10): Trend prediction AI (Heuritech); buy plan integration
- Phase 3 (months 8-16): GenAI product descriptions (14 languages, automated)

INVESTMENT:
- Total programme: $3.6M
- Markdown optimisation AI: $1.4M
- Trend prediction AI: $1.0M
- GenAI content (14 languages): $1.2M

OUTCOMES AT 24 MONTHS:
- Markdown write-offs: 12% → 7.2% (40% reduction)
- Clearance sell-through rate: 61% → 86%
- Buy plan accuracy (trend AI): +22pp
- Product description production: 8 weeks/collection → 3 days (14 languages simultaneously)
- Annual content cost savings: $4.8M (from GenAI descriptions replacing translation agency)

KEY LESSONS:
- Markdown AI early detection (week 4 vs. week 8) enabled 6% smaller markdowns on problem SKUs

KEY PATTERNS FROM RETAIL PEER OUTCOMES

PATTERN 1: DEMAND FORECASTING FIRST
Evidence: All 6 cases where demand forecasting was sequenced first achieved 3.8-12.4x ROI at 24 months. Cases where personalisation was attempted first achieved 1.4-2.2x ROI on personalisation before reverting to demand forecasting foundation.

PATTERN 2: CDP BEFORE PERSONALISATION
Evidence: All 3 cases with CDP in place before personalisation vendor achieved 4.1-5.4x ROI. Case where retailer deployed personalisation without CDP invested $2.8M for 1.4x ROI; rework required an additional $3.2M CDP investment.

PATTERN 3: PARALLEL AI AND SAP MIGRATION
Evidence: Case 2 captured $18.4M in AI value by starting demand forecasting on existing ECC pipelines, not waiting for S/4HANA go-live. The "SAP first, AI second" approach costs retailers 22-30 months of foregone AI value.

PATTERN 4: QUICK WIN VISIBILITY FOR BOARD CONFIDENCE
Evidence: In 5 of 6 cases, a fast-ROI use case (sizing AI, scheduling AI, markdown AI) was included alongside the foundational investment. The quick win at month 3-6 maintained board confidence for larger programme investment.

PATTERN 5: SHRINK AI REQUIRES CAPITAL PLANNING
Evidence: Case 3 required capital planning cycle ($62K/store) before deployment. Retailers that attempt LP AI without capital plan get stuck at pilot. Full rollout business case requires 5-year TCO model.

PATTERN 6: DATA QUALITY SPRINT IS ALWAYS WORTH IT
Evidence: All 6 cases included a data quality sprint at programme start. Average cost: $1-2M. Average ROI improvement attributable to clean data: +1.6x (AI performs 44% better on clean data vs. average-quality data). Every programme that skipped data quality sprint achieved lower-than-projected ROI.

BUILDING THE BUSINESS CASE: WHAT WORKS

CFO-COMPELLING ELEMENTS (FROM PEER OUTCOME EVIDENCE):
1. Specific named ROI from comparable peer (same format, similar revenue): most persuasive single data point
2. Time-bounded calculation (36-month NPV): CFO needs horizon, not open-ended projection
3. Downside case (what happens if AI delivers only 60% of projected ROI): shows programme team maturity
4. Milestone payments (linked to AI performance, not calendar): aligns vendor and retailer interest
5. Competitive context: which 3 direct competitors have deployed, and what they announced

BOARD-LEVEL PRESENTATION ELEMENTS:
- The burning platform: SAP ECC 2027 deadline + competitor AI deployments
- The prize: 3-year cumulative AI value (specific, named use cases)
- The plan: 36-month programme with 12-month milestone visibility
- The risk: what happens if we do nothing (market share loss, margin erosion)
- The ask: specific investment approval (not a range)

PROGRAMME SIZING BENCHMARKS:
- AI programme FTE requirement (internal): 8-14 FTE for $5B retailer full programme
- FTE composition: Chief Data Officer or programme director (1), data engineers (3-5), data scientists (2-4), change management (1-2), vendor management (1)
- External consulting augmentation: $2.8-6.4M over 36 months (system integrator + independent assurance)
- Total cost of programme management (non-technology): $4.2-8.8M over 36 months
- Technology + programme management as % of total AI investment: 28-38% of total (62-72% is technology)

PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT FOR AI PROGRAMMES

AI PORTFOLIO SEQUENCING PRINCIPLE:
- Each AI use case creates data and organisational foundations that benefit subsequent use cases
- Demand forecasting creates: clean SKU data, unified inventory, cross-functional data sharing — benefits pricing AI, supply chain AI, and store operations AI
- CDP creates: unified customer identity, real-time profile — benefits personalisation, loyalty AI, marketing AI
- Data quality sprint creates: accurate ERP data — benefits demand forecasting, financial AI, procurement AI
- Treating each use case as independent project: loses the compounding portfolio effect; ROI 2.1x lower

AI PROGRAMME HEALTH METRICS:
- AI model accuracy (vs. baseline): must be monitored weekly; alert at < 85% of target accuracy
- Business KPI movement (vs. baseline): monthly review against locked baseline
- User adoption rate (AI tool usage): quarterly; target > 80% of eligible users active
- Model drift rate: percentage of models requiring retraining per month; target < 15%
- Return on AI investment (rolling 12-month): target > 3x for established portfolio
