RETAIL IT SPEND AND TECHNOLOGY INVESTMENT BENCHMARKS
Source: Gartner Retail IT Spending Forecast 2025, NRF Technology Survey

IT SPEND AS % OF REVENUE BY FORMAT
Pure-play e-commerce: 6-9% (technology is core competency)
Specialty retail (top quartile): 2.4-3.8%
Specialty retail (median): 2.1%
Department stores: 1.6-2.4%
Grocery/supermarket: 1.2-1.8%
Home improvement: 1.8-2.4%
Below 1.4%: digitally at risk, losing share to digital competitors

IT BUDGET ALLOCATION IN RETAIL
Store operations and POS: 22-28%
E-commerce and digital: 24-32%
Supply chain and logistics: 18-24%
AI/analytics: 8-14% (leaders: 15-20%)
Corporate systems (ERP, HR, Finance): 14-18%

DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION INVESTMENT
Top quartile retailers invest: 3-4% of revenue in technology and digital
Bottom quartile: 1.2-1.6%
Outcome gap: top quartile achieves 44-58% e-commerce penetration
Bottom quartile e-commerce: 14-22%
Technology gap closure timeline (from bottom to median): 3-4 years at increased investment

TECHNOLOGY DEBT IN RETAIL
Average technology debt: $36M per $1B revenue
Systems older than 10 years: 44% of retail application portfolio
Annual technical debt interest: $4.8-8.4M per $1B revenue
Most common legacy system risk: SAP ECC EOL 2027 (affects 54% of $1B+ retailers)

CLOUD INVESTMENT TRAJECTORY
Cloud as % of IT budget (current): 31%
Cloud as % of IT budget (2027 projection): 48%
Capital expenditure shift to cloud (OpEx vs. CapEx): 38% already OpEx-dominant

AI-SPECIFIC INVESTMENT
AI and analytics as % of IT budget (leaders): 15-20%
AI and analytics as % of IT budget (laggards): < 5%
Correlation: leaders with 15%+ AI spend achieve 2x ROI on IT investment
AI investment ROI in retail (structured programme): 3.4x at 24 months
Common mistake: treating AI as a line item vs. a programme (project AI ROI: 1.8x vs. portfolio: 3.8x)

CYBERSECURITY IN RETAIL
Cybersecurity spend as % of IT budget: 8-12%
PCI DSS compliance cost: $1.2-4.8M annually
Average retail data breach cost: $3.6M per incident
Most common retail breach vector: point-of-sale malware (34%), phishing (28%)
Ransomware attack on retail: 22% experienced in last 3 years
AI-powered threat detection: reduces breach cost 28-44%
