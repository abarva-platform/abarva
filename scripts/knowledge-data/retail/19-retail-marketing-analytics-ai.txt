RETAIL MARKETING ANALYTICS AND AI BENCHMARKS
Source: Salesforce State of Marketing 2025, Klaviyo E-Commerce Marketing Study 2025, Nielsen Marketing Mix Modelling Benchmarks 2025

OVERVIEW

Marketing analytics maturity in retail spans four levels — descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive — and the gap between levels is widening. Only 6% of retailers have reached prescriptive/autonomous AI, while 34% remain at basic descriptive analytics. The financial stakes are significant: AI-personalised email generates $0.14 per email vs. $0.08 generic (a 75% uplift on the largest owned media channel in retail). AI bid management improves ROAS 24%. And retail media networks, a largely analytics-driven revenue stream, are growing 44% annually and could generate $18-44M per year for a $5B retailer. Marketing AI is also the use case most immediately impacted by the deprecation of third-party cookies, creating urgency around first-party data strategy that intersects directly with CDP investment.

PERSONALISATION MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

INCREMENTALITY TESTING:
- Retailers with mature incrementality testing programme: 18%
- Incrementality definition: true causal lift from marketing vs. what would have happened without it
- Methods: holdout groups (22%), geo-based tests (18%), matched-market tests (14%)
- AI incrementality measurement accuracy: 88% vs. 62% for matched-market without AI
- Incrementality findings: average 34% of attributed marketing revenue is non-incremental (customer would have purchased anyway)

MARKETING ROI RECALIBRATION:
- Retailers who have run incrementality tests: reduce apparent marketing ROI by 28% on average
- But: reallocate budget based on true incremental signals → ROAS improves 18% on reallocated spend
- Net effect: same budget, higher incremental revenue (less waste on non-incremental placements)

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — MARKETING ANALYTICS

F007: ATTRIBUTION WITHOUT MULTI-TOUCH MODEL — 62% of retailers use last-click attribution as primary marketing measurement. Last-click over-credits paid search by 34% and email by 22%. Budget allocation follows the wrong signal. Annual mis-allocation: -18% ROAS impact on total media portfolio. Fix: AI multi-touch attribution or MMM before making media budget reallocation decisions.

F011: RETAIL MEDIA NETWORK WITHOUT CLEAN ROOM — Retailers launch retail media network without clean room infrastructure. CPG advertisers cannot get attribution data without exposing PII. CPG advertisers churn in 6 months. Fix: Snowflake Clean Room or LiveRamp infrastructure is prerequisite to retail media network launch.

F019: EMAIL AI WITHOUT LIST HEALTH MANAGEMENT — Retailers deploy AI email personalisation on a degraded email list (38% invalid/stale addresses). AI personalisation cannot overcome deliverability issues. Inbox placement: 62% vs. 94% achievable. Fix: email list hygiene (Kickbox, ZeroBounce) and deliverability audit is prerequisite to AI email programme.
