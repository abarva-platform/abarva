RETAIL PRICING AI AND MARKDOWN OPTIMISATION BENCHMARKS
Source: McKinsey Retail AI Report 2025, Boston Consulting Group Pricing Excellence Study 2025, NRF Pricing Technology Survey 2025

OVERVIEW

Pricing AI is the second-fastest-payback AI investment in retail (8-12 months) and generates gross margin improvements of 1.2-2.8 percentage points — a significant impact in an industry where total gross margins average 30-42% for specialty retail. The discipline splits into three distinct but related categories: everyday pricing (competitive and value-based), markdown and clearance optimisation (seasonal and end-of-life), and promotional pricing (trade-funded and self-funded). A large retailer makes 10 million+ pricing decisions per year. Without AI, fewer than 2% of those decisions involve rigorous elasticity analysis. With AI, 100% of decisions are elasticity-informed in real time.

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — PRICING AI

F007: MARKDOWN AI WITHOUT RATE-OF-SALE INTEGRATION — Retailers deploy markdown AI without real-time POS integration, relying on weekly sales reports. AI identifies markdown trigger 6 weeks later than possible, losing 38% of potential markdown savings. Fix: real-time POS data feed is prerequisite for markdown AI.

F011: COMPETITIVE PRICING WITHOUT KVI SEGMENTATION — Retailers match all competitor prices without identifying KVI (Key Value Item) vs. non-KVI. Margin impact: -2.4pp from over-matching on non-KVI items. Fix: KVI AI identification is the prerequisite for competitive pricing strategy.

F019: PROMOTION AI WITHOUT BASELINE ROI LOCKING — Retailers deploy promotion AI without measuring baseline promotion ROI before deployment. Cannot prove the 12pp improvement. 84% of promotion AI deployments fail to establish baseline (same pattern as F011 in AI use cases). Fix: 3-month baseline measurement period before promotion AI goes live.
