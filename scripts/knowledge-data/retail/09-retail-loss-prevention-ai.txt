RETAIL LOSS PREVENTION AND SHRINK AI BENCHMARKS
Source: NRF National Retail Security Survey 2025, Gartner Loss Prevention Technology Report 2025, Appriss Retail Benchmarks 2025

OVERVIEW

Retail shrink — the loss of inventory due to theft, error, fraud, and damage — cost the US retail industry $112B in 2024, equivalent to 1.6% of total retail sales. The problem is worsening: ORC (organised retail crime) incidents increased 28% since 2022, and self-checkout shrink rates are running 2x attended checkout rates without AI intervention. Computer vision AI, exception-based reporting, and RFID+AI inventory systems represent the primary technology responses, with proven ROI of 2.1-3.8x at 24 months. The capital intensity of hardware (cameras, sensors, RFID infrastructure) is the primary barrier to adoption, with per-store implementation costs of $42,000-88,000 creating a significant capital planning requirement for multi-hundred-store retailers.

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — LOSS PREVENTION AI

F007: CV AI WITHOUT ALERT CALIBRATION — Retailers deploy computer vision LP without calibrating false alert rate. Alert fatigue (>24% false alert rate) causes LP associates to begin ignoring alerts within 90 days. System effectiveness drops to 12% of design capability. Fix: calibrate to <12% false positive rate before production launch; ongoing monitoring required.

F011: RFID WITHOUT DATA RECONCILIATION AI — Retailers deploy RFID infrastructure but do not deploy AI reconciliation layer. Inventory accuracy achieves 91% (vs. 65% without RFID) but not the 97.8% possible with AI reconciliation. BOPIS cancellations remain elevated at 14% vs. target 3%. Fix: AI inventory reconciliation is required layer above RFID hardware.

F019: SELF-CHECKOUT AI WITHOUT ASSOCIATE WORKFLOW — Retailers deploy SCO AI that generates alerts without a defined associate response workflow. Alerts generated; no one acts within the 60-second window before customer exits. Alert-to-action rate: 14% vs. 76% target. Fix: associate alert response protocol must be designed and trained before SCO AI goes live.
