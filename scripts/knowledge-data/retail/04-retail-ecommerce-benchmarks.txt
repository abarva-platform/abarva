RETAIL E-COMMERCE AND DIGITAL PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS
Source: NRF Digital Commerce Report 2025, Digital Commerce 360 Annual Review 2025, Forrester Retail CX Index 2025

OVERVIEW

E-commerce penetration across retail has plateaued at the broad industry level following the post-pandemic correction but continues to grow within omnichannel retailers that are investing in digital execution. As of 2025, the median specialty retailer generates 31% of revenue from digital channels, while top-quartile performers achieve 44-58%. The defining performance gap in 2025 is not whether retailers have an e-commerce channel but how efficiently they operate it — conversion rates, return rates, fulfillment cost, and AI-driven personalisation separate the top quartile from the median by 2-3x across every dimension.

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — E-COMMERCE

F007: BOPIS WITHOUT INVENTORY ACCURACY — Retailers launch BOPIS with store inventory accuracy below 92%, resulting in 18% cancellation rate. Customer NPS -22 points on cancelled BOPIS orders. Fix: RFID or real-time inventory update (< 5 minute latency) is prerequisite.

F011: CONVERSION OPTIMISATION WITHOUT PERSONALISATION FOUNDATION — Retailers invest in A/B testing platforms without CDP-based personalisation, achieving 3-4% conversion improvements vs. 18-34% possible with AI personalisation. Wrong tool for the goal.

F019: RETURNS RATE IGNORED IN UNIT ECONOMICS — Retailers celebrate e-commerce revenue growth without netting out return rates. Apparel retailers with 29% return rate and $14 return processing cost have unit economics 34% worse than headline revenue implies.
