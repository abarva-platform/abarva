RETAIL TECHNOLOGY VENDOR LANDSCAPE
Source: AbarVa Vendor Intelligence 2025, Gartner Magic Quadrant Retail Technology 2025, Forrester Wave Retail Commerce 2025

OVERVIEW

The retail technology vendor landscape has undergone consolidation and significant disruption between 2022 and 2025. Private equity has driven consolidation in legacy players (Aptos, NCR Voyix, JDA/Blue Yonder under Panasonic), while AI-native entrants have captured share in point solutions (pricing, personalisation, trend prediction, loss prevention). The most consequential platform decisions facing retailers in 2025 are: SAP S/4HANA migration approach, cloud data platform (Snowflake vs. Databricks), and e-commerce platform (Salesforce Commerce Cloud vs. commercetools vs. Shopify Plus). Each is a multi-year, multi-million-dollar commitment that determines which AI capabilities are natively available and which require expensive integration.

VENDOR SELECTION RISK PATTERNS

RISK 1: SAP RISE PARTNER QUALITY VARIATION
- Tier 1 SI partners (Accenture, Deloitte, IBM): range from 58-85 on 100-point delivery scorecard
- Tier 2 SI partners (Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant): range from 40-74
- Consequence of selecting 40-point SI: 2.1x cost overrun, 1.8x timeline overrun
- Required due diligence: minimum 3 reference calls with comparable retail clients; delivery scorecard analysis

RISK 2: DEMAND FORECASTING DATA REQUIREMENT
- All major demand forecasting vendors require 24+ months of clean history at SKU/location level
- Retailers who start vendor POC before data assessment: discover data issues mid-contract, add 4-9 months
- Due diligence: data readiness assessment (2-3 months) before any forecasting vendor contract
- SAP ERP data quality threshold: 85%+ for demand forecasting AI to outperform statistical baseline

RISK 3: PERSONALISATION REQUIRES CDP FIRST
- Wrong sequence: personalisation vendor contracted before CDP is live
- Result: personalisation AI trained on fragmented customer data delivers 34% inaccurate recommendations
- Wasted investment: $1.2-2.8M
- Correct sequence: CDP live + identity resolution complete → then personalisation vendor

RISK 4: SUPPLY CHAIN VISIBILITY REQUIRES CARRIER AGREEMENTS
- Supply chain visibility vendors (project44, FourKites) require carrier API agreements
- Average carrier API onboarding time: 4-8 weeks per carrier (28+ carriers for large retailer)
- Procurement complexity: contracts with carrier network not included in visibility platform fee
- Budget impact: carrier API costs $280K-640K annually for large retailer visibility needs

RISK 5: CV LOSS PREVENTION HARDWARE REFRESH
- CV vendors typically represent 5-year hardware life; actual hardware degrades faster
- 5-year TCO: $42,000-88,000 initial + $8,000-16,000 years 2-5 per store maintenance
- Vendors oversell AI accuracy on new hardware; accuracy degrades 18% in year 3+ without maintenance
- Required: 5-year TCO model before contract, including hardware refresh schedule

SAP ECOSYSTEM IN RETAIL

INVESTMENT DECISION FRAMEWORK

PLATFORM SELECTION CRITERIA FOR RETAIL AI PROGRAMME:
1. Data foundation (Snowflake vs. Databricks): determines which AI workloads are native vs. custom build
2. ERP (SAP vs. Oracle): determines which planning and supply chain AI is natively embedded
3. E-commerce platform (Salesforce vs. commercetools vs. Shopify Plus): determines personalization stack
4. CDP (Salesforce Data Cloud vs. Adobe): determines customer identity and personalization foundation
5. Demand forecasting (Blue Yonder vs. o9 vs. Relex): determines supply chain AI capabilities

VENDOR CONTRACT NEGOTIATION BENCHMARKS:
- Average contract length (enterprise SaaS in retail): 3 years
- Discount from list price (enterprise, competitive process): 22-38%
- Implementation included vs. separate: 68% of platforms charge separately for implementation
- Key negotiation points: data portability (exit clauses), SLA credits, customisation rights, AI data usage rights
- AI model data rights: 38% of AI platform contracts allow vendor to use retailer data for model improvement — retailers should review and restrict

VENDOR EVALUATION AND SELECTION PROCESS

RFI/RFP PROCESS FOR RETAIL AI:
- Typical RFP timeline (enterprise AI platform): 4-6 months from briefing to contract
- Shortlist size (from RFI to detailed evaluation): from 8-12 to 3-4 vendors
- POC (Proof of Concept) duration: 8-12 weeks (minimum for meaningful AI accuracy validation)
- POC cost (typical vendor expectation): $80K-240K (retailers should require vendors to contribute)
- Reference check requirement: minimum 3 comparable retailers (similar revenue, similar use case)
- AI accuracy validation (POC): vendor must demonstrate forecast accuracy on retailer's own historical data — not generic benchmarks

KEY CONTRACT TERMS FOR RETAIL AI:
- Data portability: retailer owns all training data and derived models; can export on contract termination
- AI model rights: retailer data cannot be used to train vendor's models for other clients without explicit consent
- SLA: 99.5% uptime minimum for AI serving infrastructure; credits for downtime
- Performance guarantee: vendor guarantees minimum AI accuracy threshold (e.g., 88% demand forecast accuracy); credits if not achieved
- Expansion rights: contractual caps on price increases after year 2 (22-38% discount negotiable in year 1)
- Exit provisions: 6-month transition assistance; data export in standard format

POST-IMPLEMENTATION VENDOR MANAGEMENT:
- Quarterly business review (QBR) with AI vendors: 44% of large retailers conduct formal QBR
- KPIs reviewed: accuracy metrics, uptime, support response time, roadmap progress
- Vendor health score (AbarVa framework): accuracy (40%), reliability (30%), partnership quality (20%), innovation (10%)
- Vendors scoring below 60: placed on performance improvement plan or replacement process initiated

EMERGING TECHNOLOGY VENDOR CATEGORIES (2025-2026)

AGENTIC AI FOR RETAIL:
- AI agents (autonomous AI that executes multi-step tasks): earliest commercial deployment stage
- Salesforce Agentforce (retail): AI agents for customer service, inventory lookup, returns processing
- ServiceNow (store operations): AI agents for maintenance requests, compliance tasks
- Microsoft Copilot (retail associate): AI agent for schedule management, product lookup, training
- Projected adoption: 18% of $1B+ retailers piloting AI agents by end of 2025

AUTONOMOUS RETAIL:
- Cashierless stores (Amazon Go model): 28 Amazon Go stores operating as of 2025; broader adoption stalled on cost
- Autonomous AI for checkout: Standard AI, Grabango, Trigo (vision-based; no sensors in shelves)
- Adoption barrier: $3.2-8.4M per store implementation cost; ROI requires high-volume urban format
- Most viable format: convenience (c-store), grab-and-go, campus, airport, stadium
- Cashierless grocery: 4 deployments at scale; economics require > 1,200 transactions/day

EDGE AI (IN-STORE AI PROCESSING):
- Edge AI adoption: 22% of large retailers with at least one edge AI application
- Use cases: real-time CV (loss prevention, checkout), RFID processing, ESL management
- Edge hardware: NVIDIA Jetson (28%), Intel (22%), Qualcomm (14%), AMD (12%)
- Edge vs. cloud AI: edge is 10-40x lower latency; required for < 100ms use cases
- Edge AI management platforms: NVIDIA Fleet Command, Azure IoT Edge, AWS Greengrass

SYNTHETIC DATA FOR RETAIL AI:
- Synthetic data definition: AI-generated data with same statistical properties as real data; used to augment limited datasets
- Retail use cases: new product demand forecasting (no history), store opening simulation, fraud model training
- Synthetic data adoption: 12% of AI-active retailers
- Quality of synthetic data vs. real: 88% of real data quality for training purposes
- Benefit: addresses privacy concerns (synthetic data contains no real customer PII)

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — VENDOR SELECTION

F007: VENDOR SELECTED BEFORE USE CASE DEFINED — 61% of retailers sign AI vendor contracts without specific use cases defined. Result: platform utilisation 38% below potential at 24 months; 2.1x higher total cost per use case vs. point solution. Fix: define 3 priority use cases first; evaluate vendors specifically against those.

F011: ERP VENDOR BUNDLED AI ACCEPTED WITHOUT BENCHMARK — Retailers accept AI capabilities bundled in ERP upgrades (SAP Joule, Oracle AI) without benchmarking against specialist vendors. Bundled AI performs 28-44% below specialist vendor AI at equivalent maturity. Fix: define required AI accuracy benchmarks before accepting bundled AI as production system.

F019: MULTI-VENDOR WITHOUT INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE — Retailers contract 4-6 AI vendors in parallel without designing the integration architecture. Data flows between systems manually or not at all. AI systems make recommendations that conflict. Fix: integration architecture is prerequisite to multi-vendor AI contracting.
