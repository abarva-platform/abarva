RETAIL SUPPLY CHAIN AI AND OPERATIONS BENCHMARKS
Source: Gartner Supply Chain Top 25 2025, NRF Supply Chain Survey 2025, McKinsey Operations Excellence Report 2025

OVERVIEW

Supply chain AI in retail is the second-highest-ROI AI investment area after demand forecasting, with a 2.6-4.2x return at 24 months and payback periods of 10-18 months depending on the specific use case. The supply chain opportunity is fragmented across transportation, warehousing, supplier management, and fulfillment — and the highest-performing retailers build an integrated AI layer that connects demand signals through to last-mile execution. As of 2025, only 8% of $1B+ retailers have deployed end-to-end AI across the supply chain; most have point solutions in one or two areas. The gap between top-quartile and bottom-quartile supply chain performance has widened to its largest level since 2010 as AI-enabled leaders compound their advantages.

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — SUPPLY CHAIN AI

F007: SUPPLY CHAIN AI WITHOUT DEMAND FORECASTING FOUNDATION — Retailers deploy transportation or warehouse AI without first establishing AI demand forecasting. Downstream AI cannot optimise what upstream planning has not predicted. Supply chain AI ROI 34% lower when deployed in isolation vs. as part of integrated demand-supply plan.

F011: SUPPLIER RISK UNMONITORED — 82% of retailers monitor <30% of their supplier base for risk. AI supplier risk monitoring identifies 14% of suppliers at high risk at any time. The cost of a single unmanaged major disruption ($1.4-4.2M) exceeds the annual cost of AI supplier risk monitoring.

F019: NETWORK OPTIMISATION WITHOUT NODE P&L — Retailers optimise supply chain without understanding per-node economics. Ship-from-store AI requires confirmed store inventory accuracy > 97% and store labor cost model to avoid generating negative-contribution orders. Deploy node P&L model before network AI.
