CPG AND CONSUMER GOODS AI BENCHMARKS
Source: Deloitte CPG Transformation Study 2025, McKinsey Consumer Goods AI Report 2025, NielsenIQ/Circana CPG Analytics Benchmarks 2025

OVERVIEW

Consumer packaged goods (CPG) and fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) companies face a distinct but intersecting set of AI opportunities compared to retailers. Their primary AI investment areas are trade promotion optimisation (where 40% of promotional spend generates negative ROI without AI), revenue growth management (RGM), supply chain demand sensing, and digital shelf intelligence. The stakes are high: a $5B CPG company spends $900M-1.4B annually on trade promotion, and a 12 percentage point improvement in promotion ROI (from 60% profitable to 72%) generates $108-168M in annual savings. CPG AI has now surpassed retailer AI in enterprise AI strategy adoption — 54% of CPG companies have at least one AI use case in production vs. 44% for retail.

ABARVA GENOME FAILURE PATTERNS — CPG AI

F007: TRADE PROMOTION AI WITHOUT BASELINE MEASUREMENT — 84% of CPG trade promotion AI deployments launch without establishing baseline promotion ROI. Cannot prove the 12pp improvement. Budget renewal delayed 8-12 months due to inability to demonstrate ROI. Fix: 3-month baseline measurement of promotion P&L before AI deployment.

F011: SUPPLY CHAIN AI WITHOUT RETAILER DATA SHARING — CPG deploys demand sensing AI using only internal sell-in data (not sell-out/POS from retailers). Accuracy: 78% vs. 88% possible with retail POS integration. Fix: prioritise retail POS data access agreements before demand sensing AI deployment.

F019: DIGITAL SHELF AI WITHOUT CONTENT GOVERNANCE — CPG monitors digital shelf with AI but cannot update non-compliant content quickly (3-4 week escalation to retailer). AI identifies the problem; process cannot fix it. Fix: establish retailer content portal access and content syndication workflow before digital shelf AI investment.
