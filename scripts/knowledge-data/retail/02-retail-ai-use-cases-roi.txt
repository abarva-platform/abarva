RETAIL AI USE CASES AND ROI BENCHMARKS
Source: NRF Technology Survey 2025, McKinsey Retail AI Report

TOP ROI AI USE CASES IN RETAIL

Demand Forecasting AI:
- ROI: 3.2-5.8x at 24 months
- Payback: 6-9 months (fastest in retail)
- Forecast accuracy improvement: from 74% to 91-94% at SKU/store level
- Inventory cost reduction: 1-1.4% of revenue per 1pp accuracy gain
- Out-of-stock reduction: 28-44%

Pricing and Markdown Optimisation:
- ROI: 2.8-4.4x at 24 months
- Payback: 8-12 months
- End-of-season write-off reduction: 22-34%
- Gross margin improvement: 1.2-2.8pp
- Clearance rate improvement: +24%

Personalisation AI:
- ROI: 2.4-4.1x at 24 months
- Payback: 10-16 months
- E-commerce conversion uplift: 18-34%
- Average order value increase: 12-22%
- Email/push engagement improvement: +38%

Supply Chain Optimisation:
- ROI: 2.6-4.2x at 24 months
- Payback: 12-18 months
- On-time-in-full (OTIF) improvement: +14pp
- Logistics cost reduction: 8-14%
- Distribution network savings: $3.8-8.2M per $5B revenue

Loss Prevention AI (Computer Vision):
- ROI: 2.1-3.8x at 24 months
- Payback: 14-20 months
- Shrink reduction: 18-31%
- Average industry shrink (% of revenue): 1.6%
- AI-driven shrink reduction value (per $1B revenue): $2.9-5.0M

Customer Service AI:
- ROI: 3.4-5.2x at 24 months
- Payback: 6-10 months
- Contact center deflection: 44-68%
- CSAT improvement: +14 points
- Cost per contact reduction: 52-68%

IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY SEQUENCE
Recommended sequence for highest ROI:
1. Demand forecasting (fastest ROI, enables all supply chain AI)
2. Pricing and markdowns (high margin impact)
3. Personalisation (requires customer data foundation)
4. Supply chain optimisation (requires demand forecasting baseline)
5. Loss prevention (significant but capital-intensive)

Retailers that start with personalisation before demand forecasting:
- Achieve 41% less ROI on personalisation (wrong data foundation)
- 8 months longer to positive ROI

CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS IN RETAIL AI
Clean customer identity (no duplicate customers): required for personalisation
Unified inventory data across stores/DC/e-com: required for supply chain AI
SAP or ERP data quality > 85%: required for demand forecasting
Single view of SKU: required for pricing AI
