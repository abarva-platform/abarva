FINANCIAL SERVICES IT SPEND AND TECHNOLOGY INVESTMENT BENCHMARKS
Source: Gartner IT Spending Forecast 2025, Deloitte FS Technology Survey

IT SPEND AS % OF REVENUE BY SEGMENT
Large global banks (>$50B revenue): 8.4-11.2%, median 9.6%
Regional banks ($1-10B revenue): 7.8-10.4%, median 8.9%
Asset managers ($50-200B AUM): 7.2-9.8%, median 8.1%
Large asset managers (>$200B AUM): 9.4-13.2%, median 11.1%
Insurance (global): 4.8-7.2%, median 6.1%
FinTech (native digital): 18-28% (technology is the product)

IT BUDGET ALLOCATION IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Run and maintain (infrastructure, operations): 52-58%
Change and grow (application, AI, innovation): 28-34%
Compliance and risk technology: 12-18%
AI and analytics (dedicated): 6-14% (leaders: 18-22%)

TECHNOLOGY DEBT IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Average legacy system age: 22-28 years (mainframe, COBOL)
Annual legacy maintenance cost premium: 18-28% above modern alternative
Total FS industry technology debt (global estimate): $1.1T
Average FS firm technology debt: $8.4B (global bank), $340M (regional bank), $48M (asset manager)

VENDOR SPEND CONSOLIDATION
Average number of technology vendors (large bank): 1,200+
Top quartile (optimised): 600-700
Vendor consolidation savings: 8-14% of technology spend
AI-assisted vendor rationalization: 12-18 months to complete

CLOUD SPEND GROWTH
Cloud as % of IT spend (FS): 28% (2025)
On-premise still dominant (% of IT): 72%
Cloud spend growth rate: 24% annually
Projected cloud as % of IT spend by 2028: 44%

CYBERSECURITY SPEND
Cybersecurity as % of IT budget (FS): 12-18%
FS target (best practice): 18-22%
Average cost of FS data breach: $5.9M per incident
Average cost of ransomware attack (FS): $3.4M per incident
FS cyberattack frequency: 300x higher than other industries
Top FS cybersecurity vendors: CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Darktrace (AI-native)

DATA AND ANALYTICS INVESTMENT
Data vendor spend (large asset manager): $28-48M annually
Enterprise data management investment: 8-12% of IT budget
Data quality ROI: 3.8x within 24 months
Business intelligence tool spend: $2.4-6.8M annually (varies by size)
Self-service analytics penetration in FS: 38% (faster growing than other industries)
