FINANCIAL SERVICES DATA INFRASTRUCTURE AND PLATFORM BENCHMARKS
Source: Celent Data Management in Financial Services 2025, Gartner FS Data Survey

DATA FRAGMENTATION IN ASSET MANAGEMENT
Average number of separate data environments (large asset manager): 11-16
Percentage of investment data siloed by business unit: 68%
Estimated cost of data fragmentation: $18-28M annually per $100B AUM firm
Annual duplicated data engineering effort: 34% of total data team time

DATA PLATFORM LANDSCAPE IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Snowflake adoption: 38% of large asset managers
Databricks adoption: 29% (preferred for ML workloads)
Azure Synapse: 18%
AWS Redshift: 11%
On-premise warehouses still primary: 31%

DATA CATALOGUE ADOPTION
Asset managers with enterprise data catalogue: 28%
Leading platforms: Collibra (32% FS market), Alation (24%), Microsoft Purview (21%)
Data catalogue ROI: 3.2x within 24 months (reduced rework, faster AI)
Time saved finding data with catalogue vs. without: 64% reduction
Asset managers without catalogue experiencing "data swamp": 62%

DATA QUALITY BENCHMARKS
Data quality score (FS industry average on 100-point scale): 71%
Top quartile: 88%
Bottom quartile: 54%
Data quality investment as % of data budget: 8% (industry), 18% (top performers)
Cost of poor data quality in FS: $12.6M per $100B AUM firm annually

MARKET DATA COSTS
Average annual market data spend (>$200B AUM): $28-48M
Bloomberg: 35-45% of total market data budget
Refinitiv/LSEG: 18-22%

Alternative data: 14-22% (and growing 34% annually)
Market data renegotiation success rate: 71%

REAL-TIME DATA CAPABILITIES
Asset managers with real-time portfolio analytics: 44%
Average data latency (batch): 4-8 hours
Average data latency (streaming): 18-45 seconds
Firms with sub-second trade data: 28% (primarily systematic traders)
Event-driven architecture adoption: 31%

MLOPS AND MODEL MANAGEMENT
