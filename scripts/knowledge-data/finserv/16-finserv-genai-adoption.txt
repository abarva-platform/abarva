GENAI RISK AND GOVERNANCE
FS firms with GenAI governance policy: 52%
FS firms with GenAI human review requirement (before client use): 78%
GenAI hallucination rate in unguarded mode: 8-14% of outputs contain factual errors
Hallucination rate with retrieval augmentation (RAG): < 2%
FS firms using RAG for GenAI: 38%
Key risk: GenAI fabricating financial data or regulatory citations — requires RAG and human review
