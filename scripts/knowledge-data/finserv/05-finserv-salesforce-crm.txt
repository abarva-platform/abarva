SALESFORCE AND CRM ADOPTION IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Source: Salesforce State of Financial Services 2025, KLAS FinServ CRM Research

CRM MARKET SHARE IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Salesforce Financial Services Cloud (FSC): 38% market share (firms >$50B AUM)
Microsoft Dynamics 365: 19%
Proprietary/legacy CRM: 29%
Other (Backstop, Altvia, DealCloud): 14%

SALESFORCE ADOPTION RATE BENCHMARKS
Daily active use by relationship managers (industry median): 54%
Top quartile: 78%
Bottom quartile: < 31%
Revenue leakage at < 40% adoption: 18-26% of cross-sell opportunities missed
Cross-sell revenue improvement (>70% CRM adoption): +22-31%

SALESFORCE DATA QUALITY BENCHMARKS
CRM data completeness required for Einstein AI: 85%+
Industry median CRM data completeness: 61%
Gap between required and actual: 24 percentage points
Einstein AI unreliability below 70% completeness: 68% of recommendations unreliable
Top quartile data completeness: 89%

DATA QUALITY IMPROVEMENT TIMELINE
From 61% to 85% completeness: 8-14 months
Required activities: data cleansing programme, MPI deduplication, data governance
Annual ongoing maintenance cost: $280,000-480,000 for large firm
ROI of data quality investment: 4.2x within 24 months (from Einstein activation)

SALESFORCE EINSTEIN AI PERFORMANCE
Einstein Lead Scoring accuracy: 84%
Next Best Action recommendation accuracy: 78% (at 85% data completeness)
Email engagement prediction accuracy: 81%
Firms with Einstein activated: 41% of FSC clients
Firms that consider Einstein "materially impactful": 18%
Activation gap: 23 percentage points — due to data quality dependency

RELATIONSHIP INTELLIGENCE AI
Firms using relationship intelligence AI: 28%
Vendors: Hearsay Systems, Seismic, Salesforce Einstein Relationship Insights
Relationship manager productivity improvement: 22-31%
Relationship health scoring accuracy: 82%
At-risk client identification 90 days in advance: 78% accuracy
Retention rate improvement with early intervention: +14 percentage points

SALESFORCE IMPLEMENTATION BENCHMARKS
Average FSC implementation timeline: 6-12 months
Average implementation cost ($20B AUM firm): $1.2-2.8M
Implementations that meet original go-live date: 44%
Primary delay causes: data migration (58%) and change management (34%)
Post-go-live optimization investment (recommended): 20-30% of initial cost
