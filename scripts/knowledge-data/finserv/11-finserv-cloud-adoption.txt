FINANCIAL SERVICES CLOUD ADOPTION AND INFRASTRUCTURE BENCHMARKS
Source: IDC FS Cloud Adoption Survey 2025, AWS FS Benchmark Report

CLOUD ADOPTION OVERVIEW IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
FS firms using at least one cloud provider: 88%
Primarily cloud-first (>60% workloads): 22%
Hybrid: 54%
Predominantly on-premise: 24%

CLOUD PROVIDER MARKET SHARE IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
AWS: 34% primary (trading, quant, US capital markets)
Microsoft Azure: 31% primary (UK/EU regulated, Microsoft-shop)
Google Cloud: 22% primary (analytics, AI innovation)
Oracle Cloud: 8% primary (legacy ERP lift-and-shift)
IBM Cloud: 5% (mainframe-adjacent workloads)

CLOUD PLATFORM SELECTION BY SEGMENT
Hedge funds: AWS 48%, GCP 31% (ML/quant focus)
Asset managers: Azure 38%, AWS 31% (Microsoft 365 dependency)
Regional banks: Azure 42% (compliance tooling, Teams), AWS 29%
UK/EU-regulated firms: Azure dominant (62%) — DORA compliance tooling

SOVEREIGN CLOUD AND REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS
UK FCA: FCA expects cloud outsourcing risk management, not prohibition
EU: DORA requires ICT risk management, third-party resilience testing
US OCC: supervisory expectations for cloud risk management, exit planning
Multi-cloud for regulatory resilience: adopted by 38% of large FS firms

FINOPS AND COST MANAGEMENT
Average cloud overspend (no FinOps): 34-42%
FS firms with formal FinOps: 34% (higher than other industries)
Annual FinOps savings (large firm): $8.4-18.2M
Reserved and savings plan coverage (top performers): 72%
Spot/preemptible instance use (for ML training): 44% of AI workloads

AI/ML INFRASTRUCTURE IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
AWS SageMaker + Bedrock adoption: 28% of AWS-primary FS firms
Azure Machine Learning + OpenAI: 34% of Azure-primary FS firms
Google Vertex AI: 22% of GCP-primary FS firms
On-premise GPU clusters (large quant firms): 41%
Model training cloud cost (large quant firm, annual): $2.8-8.4M

MAINFRAME MODERNISATION
FS firms with mainframe: 72%
Average mainframe age: 26 years
Annual mainframe cost: $8-24M (large bank)
Cloud migration cost vs. mainframe TCO: breakeven at 4.2 years
Mainframe exit strategy: 41% have formal exit plans; 31% have no plans
AI capabilities on mainframe: severely limited — all modern AI requires cloud connectivity
