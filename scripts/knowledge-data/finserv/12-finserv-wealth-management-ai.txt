Source: Aite-Novarica Wealth Management Technology 2025, Cerulli Associates US Wealth Management Report 2025, Morningstar Direct Wealth Study

OVERVIEW AND CONTEXT

Wealth management is undergoing the most significant structural transformation in the industry's history. The simultaneous convergence of the Great Wealth Transfer (an estimated $84T transferring from Baby Boomers to Gen X and Millennials over the next 20 years), the rise of digital-native investors who expect algorithm-driven portfolio management, and the AI-driven capability to serve mass affluent clients profitably has created a compressed window of strategic opportunity and existential risk.

The fundamental economics of traditional wealth management — one advisor managing 80-120 high-net-worth clients at $750,000-$1.1M fee revenue per advisor — cannot efficiently serve the mass affluent ($250K-$1M investable assets) and certainly cannot reach mass market investors. AI resolves this unit economics problem: AI-augmented advisors manage 140-180 clients; robo platforms serve unlimited clients at $0 marginal cost. The wealth managers who deploy AI at scale capture the Great Wealth Transfer; those who do not will lose assets to Betterment, Schwab Intelligent Portfolios, and Vanguard Digital Advisor.

AbarVa Genome failure pattern F007 (Operating Model Not Redesigned Around AI): wealth managers that deploy AI tools without redesigning the advisor's workflow and compensation model achieve 28% of the productivity improvement available. The advisor continues working the same way, using AI for one or two steps, without the holistic model change that multiplies the productivity gain.
