FINANCIAL SERVICES AI PEER OUTCOME BENCHMARKS AND CASE PATTERNS
Source: AbarVa engagement data (anonymised), Oliver Wyman Digital Banking Study 2025, McKinsey FS AI Transformation Research

OVERVIEW AND CONTEXT

Peer outcome benchmarks are the most credible evidence for AI investment decisions. Abstract ROI projections are less persuasive than "a firm very similar to you, in a similar situation, achieved these specific outcomes." This document contains anonymised case studies from AbarVa engagements (38 client relationships, combined AUM $14T, representing banking, asset management, wealth management, and insurance), supplemented by public case evidence from Oliver Wyman, McKinsey, and regulatory submissions.

The cases are selected to represent the full pattern library observed in FS AI transformations: successful transformations at different scales, programmes that failed and why, quick wins that funded larger change, and the pattern of data quality remediation as a precondition for AI success.

AbarVa Genome pattern codes identify the structural dynamic at work in each case. These codes are referenced across all AbarVa deliverables to enable consistent diagnosis and cross-client learning.


PEER OUTCOME CASE STUDIES — ANONYMISED

CASE 1: Global Asset Manager ($420B AUM) — Data Platform Transformation and Salesforce AI Activation

Situation at assessment:
    14 fragmented data environments across investment, risk, finance, and client services divisions
    Salesforce FSC deployed but at 44% daily adoption with 58% data completeness (below Einstein threshold)
    CDO vacancy — data governance headcount reporting to CIO, no investment authority
    AI programme announced 18 months prior, $4.2M spent with no measurable business impact
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F011 (AI Without Data Foundation) + F007 (CDO vacancy driving governance void)

Programme design:
    Phase 1 (months 1-8): CDO appointment, Databricks enterprise lakehouse replacing 9 of 14 environments, Collibra data catalogue deployed, CRM data remediation programme (MPI deduplication + completeness drive)
    Phase 2 (months 9-18): Einstein AI activation, AI client reporting deployment, NLP research synthesis on Databricks

Outcomes at 18 months:
    Salesforce adoption: from 44% to 78% daily active use
    Einstein AI ROI: 3.2x (first 12 months post-activation, measured against baseline cross-sell performance)
    C/I improvement: 4.2 percentage points (driven by reporting AI and operations automation)
    Total investment: $12.4M
    ROI at 18 months: 2.8x; projected 4.6x at 36 months

Key lesson: Firms that fix data quality before AI platform achieve 2.8x better outcomes. This case is the template for pattern P005 (Data Governance Before Platform).


CASE 2: Regional Asset Manager ($80B AUM) — AI Research Automation

Situation at assessment:
    Fundamental equity investment team, 22 analysts
    Research throughput: 180 documents per analyst per day (manual reading and summary)
    Earnings call cycle: 2-day delay from call to investment decision — competitors with NLP had 4-hour cycle
    No technology infrastructure for NLP; vendor evaluation had stalled after 14 months
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F003 (Technology Capability Decision Without Investment Thesis Integration)

Programme design:
    Built Azure OpenAI NLP pipeline on Databricks; avoided point vendor to maintain IP control
    Earnings call sentiment model fine-tuned on 8 years of proprietary earnings call notes
    Research synthesis module: aggregates sell-side, news, and alternative data into structured PM briefings
    Implemented in parallel with existing workflow; full adoption before legacy process disabled

Outcomes at 9 months (programme timeline):
    Research throughput: from 180 to 1,800 documents per analyst per day (10x)
    Earnings call processing: from 2 days to under 4 hours
    Investment ideas per analyst: 2.4x increase in ideas reaching investment committee
    Fund performance improvement attributable to faster response: +0.8% alpha (measured against prior-year benchmark period)

Investment: $3.2M
ROI at 18 months: 4.1x (analyst time savings + alpha capture)
Key lesson: NLP is the highest-ROI, lowest-disruption AI entry point for fundamental asset managers. The proprietary model trained on internal data significantly outperforms generic vendor solutions.


CASE 3: UK Asset Manager ($240B AUM) — ESG Data AI Programme

Situation at assessment:
    3 ESG data providers (MSCI, Sustainalytics, Bloomberg ESG)
    ESG score divergence: 52% between providers for same issuer
    SFDR Article 9 fund portfolio at regulatory risk — scores used for Article 9 classification were from single provider; multi-provider reconciliation would reclassify 31% of holdings
    SFDR PAI reporting: £384,000 annually using external consultants for manual aggregation
    No data lineage for ESG data (ESG scores in portfolio system with no record of which provider version was used)
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F019 (Regulatory Exposure Not Quantified — ESG)

Programme design:
    Clarity AI deployed as AI ESG reconciliation layer above all three providers
    Collibra data catalogue: ESG score lineage tracked from source through to portfolio system
    Automated SFDR PAI reporting pipeline: quarterly report generated in 3 days (down from 8 weeks)
    Carbon Scope 3 estimation AI: expanded portfolio emissions coverage from 28% to 72%

Outcomes at 12 months:
    ESG score confidence interval: improved 31% (less divergence, more decision-relevant)
    SFDR PAI reporting cost: from £384,000 to £62,000 annually (84% reduction)
    Regulatory risk: SFDR Article 9 classification validated and defensible (lineage audit trail in Collibra)
    New ESG mandate won: £1.2B new institutional mandate citing ESG data quality as primary selection factor
    Investment: $4.8M; ROI at 14 months: 3.4x (ESG cost savings + mandate fee revenue)

Key lesson: ESG AI investments have dual ROI — compliance cost savings and AUM capture from ESG-screened mandates. The mandate value typically exceeds the compliance savings by 4-8x.


CASE 4: US Regional Bank ($14B Assets) — Fraud AI and Core Banking Transformation

Situation at assessment:
    Legacy Fiserv Premier core banking system, 22 years in production
    Annual fraud losses: $3.8M
    C/I ratio: 68% (above target of 62%)
    Mobile banking adoption: 38% of customers (industry median 54%)
    Management team had estimated core modernisation at $28M — actual quote from Fiserv: $94M
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F007 (C/I Fixed Cost Above Revenue Capacity) + F019 (Core Modernisation Cost Shock)

Programme design:
    Phase 1 (months 1-8): ML fraud detection deployed as overlay on legacy core (Featurespace ARIC); did not require core modernisation to deliver fraud AI
    Phase 2 (months 8-24): AI deposit pricing (Nomis Solutions), digital banking AI features, contact center AI — all deployable without core modernisation
    Phase 3 (months 24-56): progressive core modernisation to Fiserv DNA (brownfield migration)
    Key insight: $12.4M in Phase 1-2 AI value was captured before the $94M core migration began

Outcomes (Phase 1-2 at 24 months):
    Fraud losses: from $3.8M to $1.4M annually (63% reduction, net savings $2.4M/year)
    C/I improvement: 6.2 percentage points (contact center AI + operations automation + deposit pricing NIM)
    Mobile adoption: from 38% to 64% (digital feature AI driving engagement)
    NIM improvement: 11 basis points from AI deposit pricing ($15.4M revenue impact at $14B assets)
    Phase 1-2 investment: $8.8M; ROI at 24 months: 4.2x

Phase 3 core modernisation (ongoing, 32 months): separate programme funded by Phase 1-2 savings
Key lesson: AI quick wins (fraud, pricing, contact center) can be deployed on legacy core via overlay architecture and fund the confidence and budget for core modernisation.


CASE 5: Large Investment Bank — AI Operations and Trade Reconciliation

Situation at assessment:
    2.4% trade reconciliation error rate (8.2 hours/day of analyst exception handling)
    Financial close: 6.8 days (well above 3-day industry target for top quartile)
    Client reporting: 4.2 hours per institutional report, 2,400 institutional clients
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F011 (Operations AI Blocked by Inconsistent Data Formats)

Programme design:
    ML reconciliation engine: AutoRek deployed with custom FS reconciliation rules
    GenAI financial close assistance: Azure OpenAI for variance commentary, intercompany reconciliation
    Client reporting AI: custom NLP pipeline generating personalised reports from portfolio analytics data

Outcomes at 14 months:
    Reconciliation error rate: from 2.4% to 0.18% (13x improvement)
    Financial close cycle: from 6.8 to 2.8 days (59% reduction)
    Client reporting time: from 4.2 hours to 22 minutes per client (91% reduction)
    Annual cost savings: $9.2M (reconciliation $8.8M + close $2.4M + reporting $4.4M, offset by shared infrastructure)
    ROI at 24 months: 7.4x — the highest ROI case in the AbarVa engagement dataset

Investment: $6.8M
Key lesson: Operations AI ROI is the most reliable and fastest in FS. Reconciliation, close, and reporting AI have quantifiable unit economics (cost per trade, cost per report) making ROI straightforward.


CASE 6: Insurance Company — AI Claims Processing and Fraud Detection

Situation at assessment:
    Average claim processing time: 12.4 days
    Claims fraud rate: 8.4% of submitted claims (above industry average of 6.2%)
    Manual claims adjudication: 82% of claims
    AbarVa pattern triggered: F011 (Operations AI Blocked by Legacy Document Processing)

Programme design:
    Computer vision + NLP for claims document intake (receipts, medical records, photos)
    ML fraud detection for claims (network analytics + anomaly detection)
    Straight-through processing: claims below $5,000 with clean documentation processed automatically

Outcomes at 18 months:
    Claim processing time: from 12.4 days to 3.2 days (74% reduction)
    Fraud detection rate: improved 34%; claims fraud cost reduced from 8.4% to 5.8% of claims
    Straight-through processing rate: 44% of claims (below-$5K, clean documentation)
    Customer NPS: +24 points (faster processing primary driver)
    Annual savings: $4.2M (processing efficiency) + $6.4M (fraud reduction) = $10.6M
    Investment: $5.2M; ROI at 24 months: 4.8x


KEY PATTERNS FROM PEER OUTCOMES

Pattern P001 — Data Quality Before AI Platform:
    Firms that fix data quality (above 85% completeness, consistency, lineage) before deploying AI platform: 2.8x better AI ROI at 24 months
    Observed in: Cases 1, 5 (both started with data quality remediation)
    Most commonly violated: firms skip data quality to save 6-9 months, then spend 18+ months debugging AI performance

Pattern P002 — Quick Win in First 90 Days:
    Programmes with a measurable AI quick win in first 90 days: 78% programme continuation rate at 24 months
    Without a quick win in 90 days: 44% continuation rate
    Best quick wins: fraud AI overlay (8-14 weeks), document processing AI (6-10 weeks), contact center AI (8-12 weeks)

Pattern P003 — CDO Appointed Before Phase 2:
    Programmes that appoint CDO before scaling beyond first AI use case: 84% success rate
    Without CDO: 46% success rate
    Observed in Case 1 (CDO appointment was Phase 1 activity before any AI deployment)

Pattern P004 — Outcome-Based Vendor Contracts:
    Firms using outcome-based vendor contracts (vendor paid on measurable business outcome, not delivery milestone): 82% SLA compliance rate versus 58% milestone-based
    Outcome: vendor has aligned incentive; slower contracting but better performance

Pattern P005 — Change Management Investment:
    Programmes allocating more than 12% of budget to change management (training, communications, workflow redesign): 76% adoption rate at 18 months
    Programmes allocating less than 5% to change management: 38% adoption rate at 18 months
    Most commonly observed gap: technology implementation funded; change management treated as optional

    Challenge: Average assets per advisor $88M (below peer $108M average); advisor time split 55% administration, 45% client-facing; retention risk in mass affluent segment
    Programme: Salesforce FSC data remediation + Einstein AI activation + AI financial planning tools (MoneyGuide AI) + AI client reporting

INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS — AI PROGRAMME SUCCESS FACTORS

Summary statistics from AbarVa analysis of 38 engagements, supplemented by McKinsey FS AI transformation study (n=84 FS firms, 2024):

Success factor impact on 24-month AI programme ROI:

    Data quality remediated before AI deployment: +1.8x ROI multiplier
    CDO appointed with investment authority: +1.6x ROI multiplier
    Quick win achieved in first 90 days: +1.4x ROI multiplier (programme momentum)
    Change management investment above 12% of budget: +1.3x ROI multiplier
    MLOps Level 2+ infrastructure in place: +1.4x ROI multiplier
    Outcome-based vendor contracts: +1.2x ROI multiplier
    Board-level AI governance: +1.1x ROI multiplier

Combinations — multiple factors compound:
    Data quality + CDO + MLOps: 3.8x ROI (all three factors combined)
    Quick win + change management: 2.1x ROI
    All 7 factors present: 4.6x average ROI at 24 months (top decile performers)
    Zero factors present: 1.1x average ROI at 24 months (near break-even)

Most common single cause of programme underperformance: data quality not remediated before AI deployment — present in 72% of programmes achieving below 2x ROI

Most common combination of failure: data quality not remediated + change management under-invested — present in 58% of failed programmes (defined as programme suspended within 24 months)

LONGITUDINAL AI PROGRAMME OUTCOMES — 36-MONTH VIEW

For the 14 AbarVa engagements with complete 36-month outcome data:

    Average 24-month ROI: 3.4x
    Average 36-month ROI: 5.8x (ROI compounds as models mature and adoption grows)
    Programmes that expanded scope (added new AI use cases after initial deployment): 78%
    Average new AI use cases added in months 25-36: 3.2 per firm
    C/I improvement at 36 months: 6.8pp average (higher than 24-month figure of 5.8pp)
    Revenue growth attributed to AI (cross-sell, NIM, fee): 4.2% additional annual revenue growth

The compounding pattern: AI programmes that succeed in months 1-24 typically expand significantly in months 25-36 as organisational confidence builds, data quality continues improving, and MLOps infrastructure enables faster model deployment. The 36-month outcome is often 2-3x the 24-month outcome.
