CORE BANKING MODERNISATION PATTERNS AND BENCHMARKS
Source: Celent Banking Technology Research 2025, IDC Financial Services

CORE BANKING MARKET LANDSCAPE
Fiserv (DNA, Premier, Cleartouch): 31% US community/regional market
Jack Henry (Symitar, SilverLake): 24%
FIS (Modern Banking Platform): 19%
Temenos: 23% global market
Average core installation age: 22 years (some exceed 35 years)
Core banking systems over 15 years old: 48% of US regional banks

PERFORMANCE IMPACT OF LEGACY CORE
Operational incident rate: legacy core 2.4x higher than modern core
Feature deployment time: legacy 8-14 months vs. modern 6-8 weeks
API capability: legacy 0-12 APIs; modern 200-800+ APIs
Mobile feature parity with digital-native competitors: legacy achieves 34%
Technical staff required: legacy 2.8x more IT FTE per $1B assets

MODERNISATION APPROACHES
Greenfield (new implementation): 34% of planned migrations
Brownfield (system conversion): 48%
Hybrid (selective data migration): 18%

Greenfield timeline ($10B bank): 36-48 months
Brownfield timeline ($10B bank): 24-36 months
Greenfield risk: higher disruption, cleaner architecture
Brownfield risk: carries forward technical debt, but faster

MODERNISATION COST BENCHMARKS
$1-5B asset bank: $20-48M total programme cost
$5-20B asset bank: $60-140M total programme cost
$20-50B asset bank: $120-280M total programme cost
Cost overrun rate: 41% exceed budget by >20%
Primary overrun cause: data migration complexity (discovered mid-project, 68% of cases)

DATA MIGRATION RISK FACTORS
Average number of accounts to migrate per $10B bank: 1.2M
Duplicate account rate (pre-migration): 3.8%
Data quality issues discovered post-migration (average): 22%
Data migration testing duration (recommended): 6-9 months
Health systems that underestimated data migration: 73%

AI CAPABILITIES UNLOCKED BY MODERN CORE
Real-time payment fraud detection (requires: streaming API)
Personalised deposit pricing (requires: event-driven data)
AI small business underwriting (requires: open banking data access)
Cash flow forecasting for business customers (requires: transaction-level data)
Personalised lending offers in mobile app (requires: API + ML model)
All require modern core: none achievable on 15+ year old core without expensive middleware

PROGRESSIVE MODERNISATION PATTERN
Deploy modern core alongside legacy: 18% choose this approach
Key benefit: reduces business risk vs. big-bang replacement
Timeline extension: 18-24 months longer than big-bang
Recommended for: banks above $5B with complex product portfolios
