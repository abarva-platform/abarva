BANKING AI USE CASES — DIGITAL LENDING, CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE, AND PERSONALISATION
Source: American Banker Technology Survey 2025, Deloitte Banking AI Report 2025, Oliver Wyman Digital Banking Study 2025

OVERVIEW AND CONTEXT

AI in banking has moved beyond the pilot phase. In 2024-2025, the use cases with proven ROI — AI underwriting, contact center automation, personalised pricing, and fraud detection — are in production at a majority of large US and European banks. The competitive differentiation has shifted from whether to deploy AI to how comprehensively and how quickly.

The critical insight from the 2024 benchmarking landscape is that speed of credit decision has become the primary conversion variable in consumer and small business lending. Customers who receive a decision within 48 hours convert at 34-44% higher rates than those waiting a week or more. AI underwriting is the primary enabler of sub-48-hour decisions, and it simultaneously improves accuracy — particularly for thin-file populations historically excluded by traditional scorecards.

AbarVa Genome failure pattern F003 (Technology Capability Without Distribution Integration): banks that deploy AI underwriting but do not redesign the origination workflow and channel experience around the faster decision time capture only 40% of the available conversion improvement. The bottleneck moves from underwriting to origination process, but the bank does not realise the revenue gain.

    Regulatory requirement: ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) compliance — AI models must be explainable; adverse action reasons must be provided (ML models require SHAP values or surrogate model for ECOA)
