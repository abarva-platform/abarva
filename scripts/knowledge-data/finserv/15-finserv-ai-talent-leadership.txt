AI TALENT, LEADERSHIP, AND GOVERNANCE IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Source: CFA Institute AI in Investment Survey 2025, Oliver Wyman FS Leadership Research

CDO AND CHIEF AI OFFICER PREVALENCE IN FS
Global banks with CDO: 74%
Asset managers with CDO: 58%
Regional banks with CDO: 34%
Firms where CDO reports to CEO: 38%
Firms where CDO reports to CIO: 44%
CDO impact on AI outcomes: 2.4x higher programme success rate

AI GOVERNANCE STRUCTURES IN FS
Firms with AI governance committee: 52% (higher than other industries, driven by regulation)
AI governance meeting frequency (monthly): 61%
Firms where AI committee has investment authority: 44%
AI model risk management (MRM) framework: 68% of large FS firms have MRM
AI model validation by risk function (before production): required at 74% of large banks

MODEL RISK MANAGEMENT (MRM) STANDARDS
SR 11-7 (Federal Reserve): governs model risk in US banks
MRM framework adoption by regional banks: 58%
Model validation cost per model: $42,000-120,000
Average time from model development to production (with MRM): 8.4 months
Average models in validation queue: 18 (large bank)
MRM bottleneck: primary AI deployment blocker in regulated banking

AI TALENT IN FINANCIAL SERVICES
Average AI engineer salary (FS, US): $196,000 fully loaded
Data scientist salary (FS, US): $168,000 fully loaded
Average time to hire AI talent: 4.8 months (FS vs. 4.2 months other industries)
AI talent retention rate (FS vs. Big Tech): 31% lower retention
FS firms offering competitive AI compensation to retain: 44%

AI LITERACY IN FS WORKFORCE
C-suite AI proficient (can direct AI strategy): 28% of FS executives
Relationship managers with AI tool proficiency: 34%
Operations staff with AI tool proficiency: 22%
Compliance staff with AI tool proficiency: 18%
Target for effective AI deployment: 60%+ workforce AI awareness

VENDOR SELECTION RISK
FS firms that selected AI vendor before defining use cases: 48%
Outcome: 2.1x higher platform cost, 38% lower utilisation at 24 months
Recommended approach: use case definition → requirements → vendor evaluation
Average vendor evaluation cycle length (FS, due to regulation): 8.4 months
RFP requirement for FS AI vendor: security, explainability, audit trail
