AI TALENT, CAPABILITY BUILDING, AND TEAM BENCHMARKS
Source: Korn Ferry AI Talent Outlook 2025, AbarVa Talent Assessment Data

AI TALENT MARKET
Global demand vs. supply gap: 3.2x (demand exceeds supply)
Average time to hire AI engineer: 4.2 months
Average time to hire senior data scientist: 5.8 months
Average time to hire CDO/Chief AI Officer: 7.4 months

AI ROLE COMPENSATION (US, fully loaded)
AI/ML Engineer: $180,000-240,000
Senior Data Scientist: $168,000-224,000
Data Engineer: $144,000-192,000
ML Architect: $210,000-280,000
Chief Data Officer: $320,000-480,000 + bonus (15-35%)
Chief AI Officer: $360,000-520,000 + bonus

BUILD VS. BUY VS. PARTNER (AI CAPABILITY)
Build internal AI team: 28% of enterprises
Primary use vendor/platform AI: 34%
Hybrid (both): 38%
Hybrid is fastest-growing approach

Time-to-value comparison:
- Build: 18-24 months to capable internal team
- Partner: 3-4 months to first AI use case
- Hybrid: 4-6 months to first AI use case, 24 months to internal capability

Cost comparison (3-year, 5 AI use cases):
- Build internal team: $8.4-14.2M
- Partner only: $4.8-9.6M
- Hybrid: $6.2-10.8M (best capability + speed balance)

AI LITERACY BENCHMARKS
AI proficient (use AI tools without support): 14% of enterprise employees
AI aware (have used AI tools): 38%
No meaningful AI tool engagement: 48%
Target for effective AI deployment: 60%+ awareness, 30%+ proficiency

AI LITERACY PROGRAMME BENCHMARKS
Organisations with formal AI literacy programme: 22%
Adoption acceleration with literacy programme: 2.4x faster
Learning time required to reach "AI aware": 4-8 hours
Learning time required to reach "AI proficient": 40-80 hours
Formats that work: scenario-based, use-case-relevant, in workflow

TEAM STRUCTURE FOR AI PROGRAMME
AI CoE minimum viable team (for >$5B organisation):
- AI Programme Director: 1 FTE
- ML Engineers: 3-4 FTE
- Data Engineers: 2-3 FTE
- Data Scientists: 2-3 FTE
- Business Translators (domain experts + AI): 2-3 FTE
- Data Steward: 1-2 FTE
Total: 11-16 FTE, $2.4-3.8M annually (fully loaded)

AI TEAM ATTRITION RISK
Annual AI team attrition rate: 28% (industry average)
Top reasons: compensation gap vs. Big Tech, slow bureaucracy, lack of compute access
Organisations that retain AI talent: pay market rate (94%), give compute access (88%), reduce approval friction (82%)
Cost of one senior AI engineer departure: $280,000-420,000 (replacement cost)

OUTSOURCING AI CONSIDERATIONS
Global AI outsourcing market: $48B (2025)
Key vendors: Accenture AI, McKinsey QuantumBlack, BCG Gamma, Deloitte AI
Average consulting rate (AI transformation): $280-480 per hour
AI-accelerated delivery models: AbarVa agent+human delivers in 55% of traditional timeline
Build vs. outsource: outsourcing accelerates speed, building accelerates long-term capability
