90-DAY SPRINT AND QUICK WIN METHODOLOGY
Source: AbarVa Phase Gate Framework, AbarVa Genome — Programme Survival Patterns

THE 90-DAY IMPERATIVE
Programme continuation rate at 24 months WITH 90-day quick win: 78%
Programme continuation rate WITHOUT 90-day quick win: 38%
Delta: 40 percentage points — the 90-day win is the most critical programme survival factor

WHY 90 DAYS IS THE THRESHOLD
Board attention span: 3 months to first evidence
Sponsor confidence: needs visible proof before internal political pressure mounts
Budget renewal cycle: many organisations have quarterly budget reviews
Workforce engagement: 90-day cycles match human psychological commitment cycles
Competitor visibility: 90 days is about the time competitor AI deployments become visible

DEFINING THE RIGHT 90-DAY WIN
Criteria for a valid 90-day quick win:
1. Measurable: specific numeric improvement vs. locked baseline
2. Visible: something a board member can see or understand
3. Attributable: clearly caused by AI, not external factors
4. Credible: based on real data, not a pilot on best-case scenarios
5. Scale-able: the first win should be replicable across the organisation

QUICK WIN SELECTION BY INDUSTRY

Healthcare:
Best 90-day wins: denial rate improvement (AI denial scrubbing), prior auth time reduction
Data requirement: Epic billing data (usually available)
Achievable improvement: 15-25% denial rate reduction in 90 days
Board visibility: CFO-level (revenue impact)

Financial Services:
Best 90-day wins: AML false positive reduction, document processing time reduction
Data requirement: transaction system data (usually available)
Achievable improvement: 30-50% false positive reduction in 90 days
Board visibility: COO/Chief Risk Officer level

Retail:
Best 90-day wins: forecast accuracy improvement in one category, loss prevention reduction in pilot stores
Data requirement: POS + inventory data (usually available)
Achievable improvement: 10-14% accuracy improvement in 90 days
Board visibility: COO/CFO level (inventory cost)

SPRINT DESIGN FRAMEWORK
Week 1-2: data access, baseline locked, model training environment
Week 3-6: model development, data pipeline validation
Week 7-8: model validation and testing in controlled environment
Week 9-10: production deployment (limited scope)
Week 11-12: measurement and results documentation
Week 12: CXO presentation with evidence

90-DAY WIN FAILURE PATTERNS
Failure: picking a use case with insufficient data (discover mid-sprint)
Mitigation: data audit Week 1 with hard go/no-go decision

Failure: picking a use case too complex for 90 days
Mitigation: pick the simplest version of the use case, not the most ambitious

Failure: vendor delay pushing past 90 days
Mitigation: internal team capability for quick win, not vendor-dependent

Failure: 90-day win is technically complete but not communicated effectively
Mitigation: board presentation designed by comms and business leader, not IT

PROGRAMME SURVIVAL AFTER QUICK WIN
After successful 90-day win: programme continuation at 78% (as above)
Phase 2 investment approval: 84% success rate (after 90-day win)
Team confidence and retention: 94% of AI team stays through Phase 2 after visible win
