DATA READINESS AND AI PROGRAMME OUTCOME CORRELATION
Source: AbarVa Assessment Database — 340+ engagements

ABARVA DATA READINESS SCORE (0-100)
Composite of: Data Quality (30%), Infrastructure Maturity (25%), Governance (25%), Talent (20%)

SCORE DISTRIBUTION (AbarVa assessed client base)
Score > 70 (AI-ready): 8% of organisations
Score 50-70 (foundation building): 29%
Score 35-50 (significant gaps): 41%
Score < 35 (pre-foundation): 22%

OUTCOME CORRELATION BY SCORE BAND

Score > 65 (AI-Ready):
- AI use case deployment rate: 2x the 35-50 band in 24 months
- Programme cost vs. forecast: 94% on or under budget
- Model accuracy at deployment: 88% average
- ROI at 24 months: 280-380%
- 90-day milestone achieved: 84%

Score 50-64 (Foundation Building):
- AI use case deployment rate: 1.4x median
- Programme cost vs. forecast: 12% over budget average
- Model accuracy at deployment: 76% average
- ROI at 24 months: 140-210%
- 90-day milestone achieved: 61%

Score 35-49 (Significant Gaps):
- AI use case deployment rate: 0.7x median
- Programme cost vs. forecast: 44% over budget average
- Model accuracy at deployment: 62% average
- ROI at 24 months: 60-110%
- 90-day milestone achieved: 38%

Score < 35 (Pre-Foundation):
- 68% of AI initiatives stall at proof-of-concept
- Deployed models: 0.3x the AI-ready band
- Cost overrun: 2.1x original programme estimate
- ROI: negative in 44% of cases

DATA READINESS IMPROVEMENT ROI
Moving from score 35-40 to 60-65:
- Required investment: $4-12M (varies by size)
- Required timeline: 12-18 months
- ROI on readiness investment: 4.2x (through downstream AI programme performance)

Top 5 data readiness blockers by frequency:
1. No enterprise data governance framework: 61%
2. Multiple conflicting master data sources (customer, product): 54%
3. Data lake without data catalogue: 48%
4. No self-service analytics for business users: 44%
5. Data quality score < 70% on critical datasets: 41%

KEY FINDING
Organisations that invest in data readiness before selecting an AI platform achieve 2.8x better AI programme outcomes than those who select the platform first.

Sequence that works: Baseline → Governance → Data Quality → Platform → Use Cases → Scale
Sequence that fails: Platform → Use Cases → (discover) Data Quality → Rework → (miss) Governance → Delay

READINESS INVESTMENT BENCHMARK
Readiness investment as % of total AI programme: top quartile 18-24%
Readiness investment as % of total AI programme: bottom quartile 4-8%
Bottom quartile programmes with low readiness investment: 2.1x higher total programme cost
(Skipping readiness is expensive — it is paid for with rework)
