OVERVIEW

AbarVa is a data and AI advisory firm that operates at the intersection of strategy, data engineering, AI delivery, and change management. AbarVa's fundamental difference from traditional technology consultancies is structural independence from technology vendors — AbarVa does not earn fees from platform vendors, does not have preferred partner relationships that create recommendation bias, and takes outcome-based financial risk alongside clients. This structure exists specifically to protect clients from the most common failure pattern in AI programmes: vendor selection before strategy (L-007).

AbarVa's engagement model is grounded in the Genome — 127 failure patterns catalogued from 340+ real engagements. Every AbarVa recommendation is traceable to a pattern that has been observed to produce success or failure in comparable organisations. This is the distinction between AbarVa advisory and theoretical frameworks: AbarVa's advice is operational, not academic.

AbarVa differentiators (summary):
  1. Genome: 127 failure patterns from 340+ engagements — pattern-based advisory, not theoretical
  2. Outcome contracts: AbarVa takes outcome-based financial risk alongside clients (skin in the game)
  3. Agent-human delivery: AbarVa agents + human experts deliver in 55% of traditional consulting timeline
  4. End-to-end: strategy through delivery — no handoff gap between strategy and execution
  5. Independent: no technology vendor relationship — recommends what is right for the client, never what is sold

  Baseline locking: AbarVa Baseline Locking Protocol completed for all Phase 2 KPIs
  Vendor renegotiation: SLA enforcement; outcome-based contract redrafting
  Change management design: lighthouse user programme; adoption tracking; manager accountability integration

ABARVA DIFFERENTIATION — DETAILED

1. GENOME: PATTERN-BASED ADVISORY
  127 failure patterns catalogued from 340+ engagements — operational, not theoretical
  Each recommendation traceable to a pattern that has been observed in real programmes
  Phase 0 diagnosis: average 4.2 active failure patterns per organisation; addressing in Phase 0 saves $2.4M per pattern vs. Phase 2 detection
  Competitive advantage: AbarVa's pattern library is updated with every engagement; knowledge compounds

2. OUTCOME CONTRACTS: SKIN IN THE GAME
  AbarVa offers outcome-based fee structures on programmes where outcomes are clearly definable and measurable
  Typical structure: 70-80% base fee; 20-30% performance fee tied to achieving KPI milestones
  AbarVa has signed outcome-based contracts in: RCM (denial rate), demand forecasting (accuracy), fraud detection (loss reduction), data platform (self-service adoption)
  Signal value: AbarVa's willingness to accept outcome risk is itself a signal of confidence in the recommended programme
  Client alignment: outcome-based structure aligns AbarVa and client incentives; both benefit from programme success

3. AGENT-HUMAN DELIVERY: 55% OF TRADITIONAL TIMELINE
  AbarVa's delivery model combines AI agents (for research, analysis, documentation, code generation) with human experts (for judgement, stakeholder relationships, design)
  Time to deliver: 55% of traditional consulting timeline for equivalent deliverables
  Cost implication: same quality at 55% of timeline = better economics for client
  Application: AI agents for: failure pattern diagnosis, use case financial modelling, data quality assessment, SLA breach detection, competitive benchmarking, report generation
  Human experts for: stakeholder interviews, governance design, board presentations, change management facilitation, vendor negotiation

4. END-TO-END: NO HANDOFF GAP
  Most AI consulting engagements have a handoff gap between strategy (consulting firm) and delivery (system integrator)
  The handoff gap creates: strategy not translated to delivery requirements; accountability gap between strategy and outcome; integration risk
  AbarVa covers: strategy (Phase 0), foundation (Phase 1 MDP), use case delivery (Phase 2 AVR/RCM/PDLC), scale (Phase 3), and compound (Phase 4)
  No handoff: the team that designs the strategy delivers it; no translation loss

5. INDEPENDENT: NO VENDOR BIAS
  AbarVa has no preferred platform vendor relationships, no platform-linked revenue, and no incentive to recommend any specific technology
  Contractual: AbarVa does not accept referral fees, volume incentives, or certification premiums from technology vendors
  Practical: every platform recommendation is derived from Phase 0 use case requirements and technology assessment — never from vendor relationship
  Client protection: this independence is the primary structural response to pattern L-007 (vendor before strategy)
  Market positioning: AbarVa explicitly positions against consultancies with embedded vendor partnerships as a conflict-of-interest risk
