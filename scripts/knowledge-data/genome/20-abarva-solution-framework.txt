ABARVA SOLUTION PORTFOLIO

SOLUTION 1: AI VALUE REALISATION (AVR) — AI STRATEGY
Focus: AI strategy, use case prioritisation, investment thesis, governance design
Best for: organisations with AI mandate but no clear strategy
Typical engagement size: $280,000-640,000 (Phase 0 strategy only)
Full programme: $2.4-8.4M (Phase 0-3)
Deliverables: Situation diagnosis, contradiction analysis, data readiness assessment, use case prioritisation, investment thesis, governance model
Time to first deliverable: 4 weeks
Time to board-ready AI strategy: 8 weeks

SOLUTION 2: MODERN DATA PLATFORM (MDP) — TECH TRANSFORMATION
Focus: data lakehouse architecture, cloud migration, data governance, self-service analytics
Best for: organisations with poor data readiness blocking AI
Typical engagement size: $4.2-12.4M
Deliverables: Data architecture design, Snowflake/Databricks implementation, data catalogue, MDM, dbt transformation layer, BI migration
Time to first production data: 4-6 months
Time to full platform: 12-18 months

SOLUTION 3: REVENUE CYCLE AI (RCM) — HEALTHCARE
Focus: denial prevention, prior authorisation automation, coding accuracy, revenue integrity
Best for: health systems with denial rates > 8% or PA manual rates > 60%
Typical engagement size: $3.8-8.4M
ROI: 4.2-8.1x at 24 months
Deliverables: RCM AI model suite, vendor SLA renegotiation support, CDI programme, payment integrity AI
Time to positive ROI: 8 months

SOLUTION 4: MARGIN INTELLIGENCE — OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY
Focus: cost structure analysis, AI-driven operational efficiency, vendor optimisation
Best for: organisations with cost-to-income or EBITDA margin pressure
Typical engagement size: $2.4-6.8M
ROI: 3.2-5.4x at 24 months
Deliverables: Margin analysis, AI process automation roadmap, vendor consolidation programme, workforce analytics

SOLUTION 5: AI PRODUCT DELIVERY LIFECYCLE (PDLC) — MLOPS
Focus: MLOps platform, model governance, model risk management, feature store
Best for: organisations with > 10 AI models in development, slow model deployment
Typical engagement size: $2.4-6.4M
Deliverables: MLOps platform, model registry, monitoring, CI/CD for ML, MRM integration (FS)
Time to first production model via new pipeline: 2.8 months

SOLUTION 6: DELIVERY AND PROGRAMME MANAGEMENT
Focus: AI programme delivery, change management, governance implementation
Best for: organisations with strategy defined but execution stalling
Typical engagement size: $1.8-4.8M
Deliverables: Programme management office, change management programme, vendor management, board reporting

ABARVA ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY

PHASE 0: AbarVa Discovery (4-8 weeks)
- Situation diagnostics (5 critical findings with financial impact)
- Contradiction analysis (strategy vs. data reality)
- Data readiness assessment (0-100 score)
- Use case prioritisation
- Investment thesis

PHASE 1: Foundation (3-6 months)
- Data governance
- Cloud platform
- Organisational capability

PHASE 2: First Use Case (3-6 months)
- Use case delivery
- Baseline KPIs locked
- 90-day quick win

PHASE 3: Scale (6-18 months)
- Portfolio of 3-5 use cases
- Self-sufficient capability

PHASE 4: Compound (18-36 months)
- Embedded AI in operations
- Self-funding programme
