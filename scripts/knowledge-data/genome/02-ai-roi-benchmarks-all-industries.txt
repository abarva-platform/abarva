OVERVIEW AND EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

AI ROI benchmarks vary enormously by use case type, industry, programme structure, and organisational readiness. AbarVa's use case library contains ROI data from 340+ engagements, cross-validated against McKinsey, Forrester, and Gartner benchmarks. The single most important finding from this data: structured AI programmes (strategy-first, baseline-locked, governance-established) achieve a median 24-month ROI of 280%, while unstructured programmes achieve only 110%. The programme structure matters as much as the use case selected.

Key headline benchmarks:
  Median 24-month ROI (AbarVa structured programmes): 280%
  Median 24-month ROI (industry average, unstructured): 110%
  Top-quartile 36-month ROI (5-use-case portfolio): 380%
  Programmes that cannot measure ROI (no locked baseline): 84%
  Fastest ROI payback: invoice processing automation (3-5 months)
  Highest ROI category: banking fraud detection (4.2-8.2x at 24 months)
  Largest ROI destroyer: no locked baseline (84% of programmes)

PORTFOLIO COMPOUNDING EFFECT

One of the most important and least understood phenomena in AI ROI benchmarks is the compounding effect of multiple use cases. Each subsequent use case benefits from data infrastructure, platform capability, organisational capability, and governance already established by previous use cases.

Portfolio ROI benchmarks (AbarVa data):
  1st AI use case median ROI (24 months): 140%
  2nd AI use case median ROI (24 months): 224% (1.6x the first)
  3rd AI use case median ROI (24 months): 294% (2.1x the first)
  5-use-case portfolio median 36-month ROI: 380%
  10-use-case portfolio (Phase 4): 480-620% (fully compounded)

Compounding mechanisms:
  1. Data reuse: data engineering work for use case 1 serves use cases 2-5 at near-zero marginal cost
  2. Platform amortisation: Snowflake/Databricks platform cost spread across more use cases
  3. Organisational learning: data science team is 2.4x more productive on 3rd use case vs. 1st
  4. Governance infrastructure: compliance, security, and risk frameworks reused without rebuild
  5. Change management: workforce that adopted AI once is 1.8x faster to adopt next use case

Implication: the business case for the first use case almost always understates total programme ROI, because it does not capture compounding.

COMMON ROI MEASUREMENT ERRORS

Error 1: No locked baseline (84% of programmes)
  Result: ROI claims unverifiable; programmes declared successful without evidence
  Financial impact: median $2.4M in unverifiable claims per programme

Error 2: Gross savings instead of net ROI
  Example: $8M cost reduction claimed, $6M programme cost excluded → actual ROI 33%, not 167%
  Prevalence: 62% of AI ROI claims are gross savings without programme cost deduction

Error 3: Attributing all improvement to AI
  External tailwinds (market recovery, seasonal uplift) mistaken for AI impact
  Correct approach: document and exclude confounding variables in baseline protocol

Error 4: Measuring too early
  AI models stabilise at month 3-6; measuring at month 6 captures 60-70% of steady-state benefit
  Prevalence: 44% of "failed" AI programmes were measured during ramp period, not steady state

Error 5: Counting potential ROI from undeployed use cases
  Programme roadmap ROI (all future use cases) claimed as current ROI
  Prevalence: 38% of AI ROI presentations include projected future use cases in current period ROI

Error 6: Model performance metrics instead of business outcomes
  AUC = 0.92 cited as success; underlying business outcome (denial rate, fraud loss) not improved
  Root cause: data science team defines success as model performance; business defines it as financial outcome

Error 7: Independent verification absent
  Average delta between claimed ROI (340%) and independently verified ROI (280%): 17%
  Only 22% of programmes use independent ROI verification
  AbarVa recommendation: independent verification for all programmes >$5M
  Verification cost: $40,000-180,000 — negligible vs. ROI being verified

ROI RISK FACTORS AND SCENARIO PLANNING

BEST-CASE SCENARIO (TOP QUARTILE — FULL STRUCTURAL COMPLIANCE):
  Phase 0 completed; CDO in place; DRS > 65; strong mandate; outcome-based vendor contract; change management 15%+
  24-month ROI: 380-480%
  Programme success probability: 84%

BASE CASE (MEDIAN — ADEQUATE STRUCTURE):
  Phase 0 completed; CDO in place; DRS 50-65; moderate mandate; traditional vendor contract; change management 12%
  24-month ROI: 210-280%
  Programme success probability: 61%

RISK CASE (BELOW MEDIAN — STRUCTURAL GAPS):
  Phase 0 abbreviated; CDO absent or advisory; DRS < 50; aspirational mandate; change management < 8%
  24-month ROI: 60-110%
  Programme success probability: 38%

WORST CASE (MULTIPLE ACTIVE FAILURE PATTERNS):
  No Phase 0; no CDO; vendor selected first; no baseline; no change management
  24-month ROI: negative in 44% of cases; median positive 28%
  Programme success probability: 14-28%

Scenario planning application: AbarVa uses this framework to show clients the ROI range under different structural assumptions. The most persuasive conversation is: "With current governance, you are in the risk case scenario (110% ROI). With these three changes, you move to the base case (280% ROI). The investment to make those changes is $1.2M. The ROI improvement is $14M on a $10M programme."
