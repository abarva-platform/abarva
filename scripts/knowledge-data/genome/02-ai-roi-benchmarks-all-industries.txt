AI ROI BENCHMARKS BY USE CASE — ALL INDUSTRIES
Source: AbarVa Use Case Library, McKinsey Global AI Survey 2024-2025

ROI RANGES BY AI CATEGORY

DATA PLATFORM AND INFRASTRUCTURE
Data lakehouse (Snowflake/Databricks): Median time to positive ROI 28 months; top-quartile 36-month ROI 340%
Failure mode: 44% of data platform investments do not progress to active AI use cases within 24 months (built without use cases)
Key driver: platforms with use cases defined before build: 2.1x ROI

MACHINE LEARNING — PREDICTIVE MODELS
Demand forecasting: ROI 3.2-5.8x at 24 months, payback 6-9 months
Fraud detection (banking): ROI 4.2-8.2x at 24 months, payback 8-14 months
Denial prediction (healthcare): ROI 4.2-8.1x at 24 months, payback 8-14 months
Churn prediction (retail/FS): ROI 2.4-4.1x at 24 months, payback 12-18 months
Predictive maintenance: ROI 2.1-3.6x at 24 months, payback 16-24 months

GENERATIVE AI
Content generation: Median time to positive ROI 7 months (fastest)
Top-quartile ROI at 12 months: 290%
Caution: 38% of organisations cannot quantify GenAI ROI accurately
Best performing GenAI use cases: code generation (41% productivity), document summarisation (52% time reduction)

AI PROCESS AUTOMATION (RPA + AI)
Document processing AI: ROI 4.1x at 24 months, payback 8-14 months
Clinical documentation AI (ambient): ROI 2.4-4.1x at 18 months
Trade reconciliation AI (FS): ROI 7.4x at 24 months
Fastest RPA + AI payback: invoice processing (payback 3-5 months)

AI-ENABLED OPTIMISATION
Supply chain optimisation: ROI 2.6-4.2x at 24 months, payback 12-18 months
Pricing/markdown optimisation: ROI 2.8-4.4x at 24 months, payback 8-12 months
Workforce scheduling AI: ROI 3.4-5.8x at 24 months, payback 8-14 months
Energy management AI: ROI 2.1-3.8x at 24 months, payback 12-18 months

PORTFOLIO COMPOUNDING EFFECT
1st AI use case median ROI (24 months): 140%
2nd AI use case median ROI (24 months): 224% (1.6x first)
3rd AI use case median ROI (24 months): 294% (2.1x first)
5-use case portfolio median 36-month ROI: 380%
Reason: data, platform, and organisational capability from first use case reduce cost of subsequent deployments

COMMON ROI MEASUREMENT ERRORS
Error 1: No locked baseline — 84% of programmes cannot prove ROI
Error 2: Counting gross savings, not net of AI programme cost
Error 3: Attributing all improvement to AI when other factors changed
Error 4: Measuring too early (measuring at 6 months before model stabilises)
Error 5: Counting "potential" ROI from use cases not yet deployed
