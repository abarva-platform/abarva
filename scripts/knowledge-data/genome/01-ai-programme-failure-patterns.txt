ABARVA GENOME — AI PROGRAMME FAILURE PATTERNS
Based on: 340+ enterprise transformation engagements
Industries: Healthcare, Financial Services, Retail, Technology
Updated: 2025

F001 — NO BASELINE LOCKED DAY 0
Failure rate: 84% (programmes without locked baseline cannot prove ROI)
Description: AI programme launched without immutable baseline metrics before go-live. ROI cannot be calculated. Programme declared successful without evidence.
Prevalence: 84% of enterprise AI programmes
Signal: If baseline was not captured before the first model went to production, it is now too late
Mitigation: Lock 5-7 KPI baselines with CXO signature before any AI vendor goes live. Third-party verification preferred.

F003 — PLATFORM EOL UNPLANNED MIGRATION
Failure rate: 82%
Description: Legacy data platform reaches end-of-life without migration plan. Forced reactive migration.
Cost premium: 38-47% above planned migration
Timeline premium: 9-18 months additional
Most affected: Healthcare (Teradata), Retail (SAP ECC 2027), Banking (legacy core)
Mitigation: Begin planning 18+ months before EOL date. Business case framing as AI unlock, not IT infrastructure.

F007 — CDO VACANCY AT AI PROGRAMME LAUNCH
Failure rate: 79%
Description: Chief Data/Digital Officer role vacant when AI programme launches. No executive accountability for data.
Prevalence: 34% of health system AI programmes have CDO vacancy at launch
Impact: Governance collapse at implementation phase. No one owns data quality or model outcomes.
Mitigation: Interim CDO appointment before Phase 2. CDO must have budget authority.

F011 — VENDOR SLA BREACH UNENFORCED
Failure rate: 74% (of SLA-breaching vendors continue to underperform)
Description: Primary IT vendor breaching SLA for multiple quarters with no penalty enforcement by client.
Average breach duration before detection: 4.8 quarters
Average recoverable value when enforced: $4.2-12.1M
Reason for non-enforcement: Legal/procurement disconnect from operations
Most common vendors: RCM outsourcers, EHR implementation partners, cloud providers
Mitigation: Quarterly SLA review with penalty automation. Separate legal authority to invoke penalties without board approval.

F019 — CUSTOMER DATA FRAGMENTATION UNRESOLVED
Failure rate: 68%
Description: AI personalisation or churn prevention programme launched before customer data unified.
Average systems containing customer data: 6.4
Time to unify with modern CDT tooling: 4-8 months
Cost of delay: Personalisation AI window closes as competitors act
Mitigation: CDT/CDP before personalisation AI vendor contract.

F027 — ERP DATA QUALITY BLOCKS AI
Failure rate: 71% (when AI attempted before data layer fix)
Description: Supply chain or inventory AI blocked by inconsistent data across multiple ERP instances.
Common scenario: Post-M&A with 4-12 ERP instances
Solution: Silver layer entity resolution (not ERP replacement)
Average resolution time: 4-6 months with Databricks DLT

F031 — CHANGE MANAGEMENT UNDER-INVESTED
Failure rate: 68%
Description: AI programme with < 10% of budget allocated to change management and training.
Outcome: 41% adoption at 18 months (vs. 76% with >12% budget)
Cost of re-adoption programme: $1.8-4.2M after the fact
Mitigation: Change management budget minimum 12% of total programme cost from Day 1.

F044 — COMMITTEE WITHOUT AUTHORITY
Failure rate: 58%
Description: AI steering committee that cannot approve spend above $100K without full board sign-off.
Decision speed: 3.4x slower than committees with delegated authority
Impact: Vendors leave, opportunities missed, urgency lost
Mitigation: Pre-approve spending thresholds for AI steering committee. CEO or board delegate authority explicitly.
