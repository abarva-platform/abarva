VENDOR SLA PERFORMANCE AND ENFORCEMENT BENCHMARKS
Source: AbarVa Vendor Intelligence Layer — 340+ engagements

VENDOR SLA COMPLIANCE RATES BY CATEGORY
RCM outsourcers (healthcare): 72% average SLA compliance
IT infrastructure vendors: 81% average SLA compliance
EHR implementation partners: 74% average SLA compliance
Cloud providers: 99.8% SLA (but SLA definition excludes most real outages)
AI/ML platform vendors: 78% average SLA compliance
Data management vendors: 76% average SLA compliance

SLA ENFORCEMENT PATTERNS
Clients that have ever enforced SLA penalty clauses: 26%
Clients that never enforce despite breach: 74%
Average breach duration before client action: 6.2 quarters (18+ months)
Primary reason for non-enforcement: legal/procurement disconnected from operations
Secondary reason: "relationship risk" perceived to outweigh penalty value

FINANCIAL IMPACT OF NON-ENFORCEMENT
Average recoverable value per enforcement action: $4.2-12.1M
Annual value left on table by non-enforcement: $2.4-6.8M per major vendor relationship
Compounding effect: vendors who see non-enforcement reduce service quality further (AbarVa data: 34% quality decline after first unenforced breach)

HIGH-RISK SLA PATTERNS TO MONITOR
Pattern 1: Vendor reporting "green" status on dashboards while operational metrics are red
Pattern 2: SLA penalties capped at small percentage of contract value
Pattern 3: SLA measured on technical uptime, not business outcome
Pattern 4: No independent third-party measurement — vendor self-reports
Pattern 5: Penalty invocation requires multi-level approval (kills enforcement in practice)

VENDOR CONTRACT BEST PRACTICES (AbarVa FRAMEWORK)
1. Business outcome SLA: tie penalties to denial rate, fill rate, system availability — not just uptime
2. Automatic penalty: penalty triggers without client action (invoice deduction)
3. Third-party measurement: independent measurement of SLA performance
4. Quarterly business review: mandatory CXO-level SLA review
5. Ratchet clauses: SLA tightens each year of contract (incentivises continuous improvement)
6. Exit clarity: defined transition assistance and data portability in all contracts

OUTCOME-BASED CONTRACT BENCHMARKS
Clients with outcome-based contracts: 22%
SLA compliance with outcome-based contracts: 84%
SLA compliance with traditional contracts: 61%
Delta: 23 percentage points
Vendor willingness to sign outcome-based contracts: 38% will negotiate; 62% resist
Signal: vendor resistance to outcome-based contract = performance confidence gap

VENDOR LOCK-IN RISK ASSESSMENT
Single vendor for multiple critical systems: adds 30-40% to 5-year TCO (AbarVa benchmark)
No alternative vendor evaluated in 3+ years: cost premium 18-28%
Proprietary data format with no export standard: switching cost multiplier 2.4x
Vendor with > 30% of IT spend: governance risk — board should be aware
AI vendor with proprietary model (no explainability): regulatory risk in FS and healthcare
