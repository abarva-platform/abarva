EXECUTIVE MANDATE PATTERNS AND AI STRATEGY FRAMING
Source: AbarVa Engagement Strategy Layer — 340+ engagements

MANDATE STRENGTH AND OUTCOME CORRELATION
Strong mandate (specific, measurable, owned): 74% programme success rate
Weak mandate (aspirational, unowned): 28% programme success rate
Delta: 46 percentage points

CHARACTERISTICS OF STRONG MANDATES
1. Specific outcome: "Reduce denial rate from 9.8% to < 5% within 18 months"
2. Named accountable executive: "CDO owns this outcome"
3. Measurable baseline: existing denial rate documented and agreed
4. Financial consequence: "$34M in recoverable revenue unlocked"
5. Time-bound: "18 months, first milestone at 90 days"

CHARACTERISTICS OF WEAK MANDATES (AVOID)
"We need to leverage AI"
"AI is a strategic priority"
"We want to be an AI-first organisation"
"We should explore machine learning opportunities"
Without specific outcomes, these mandates produce: committee proliferation, pilot culture, no accountability

STAKEHOLDER MANDATE FRAMING

CFO FRAME:
Resonates: cost reduction through automation
Numbers required: fully-loaded FTE equivalent or total cost reduction %
ROI horizon: must be within 24 months
Approval threshold: most CFOs approve at 2x ROI, 18 months payback
Framing: "AI automates [specific process], eliminating [X] FTE equivalents at $Y/FTE, delivering $Z net savings in 18 months"

CEO FRAME:
Resonates: competitive advantage and revenue growth
Data needed: peer comparison showing competitors' AI trajectory
Key risk: "We are 18 months behind our peer group in AI capability"
Framing: "Our top 3 competitors have deployed [use case]. We are losing [X]% of [revenue category] to this capability gap"

COO FRAME:
Resonates: operational excellence and delivery reliability
Numbers needed: cycle time reduction, error rate reduction, capacity scaling
Key trigger: evidence of operational pain, not aspirational language
Framing: "[Specific process] costs us [X] per [unit]. AI reduces this to [Y]. At [Z volume], this is $[amount] annually"

CHRO FRAME:
Resonates: talent retention and workforce capability
Positioning: AI as workforce empowerment, not replacement
Key insight: organisations framing AI as workforce empowerment see 2.4x faster adoption
Framing: "AI handles [routine tasks], freeing [role] to focus on [higher-value work]. This improves retention and attracts better talent"

BOARD FRAME:
Resonates: risk management and long-term value creation
Data: peer comparisons, regulatory risk, strategic risk
Key message: "We are [X] months behind the industry in AI capability. If this gap persists, [specific risk]"

MANDATE DECAY PATTERN
Mandates not translated to specific KPIs within 90 days: 68% are abandoned or de-prioritised
Mandates with quarterly steering review: 84% maintained at 18 months
Mandates with annual-only review: 62% de-prioritised before 12 months
