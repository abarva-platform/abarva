CHANGE MANAGEMENT BENCHMARKS FOR AI PROGRAMMES
Source: AbarVa Genome — 340+ engagements, Prosci Change Management Research 2025

CHANGE MANAGEMENT INVESTMENT AND ADOPTION CORRELATION
Budget allocated to change management vs. AI adoption rate at 18 months:
- < 8% of budget: 41% adoption rate
- 8-12% of budget: 58% adoption rate
- 12-15% of budget: 76% adoption rate
- > 15% of budget: 84% adoption rate

ROI on change management investment:
Programmes with < 8% change management: average ROI 140%
Programmes with 12-15% change management: average ROI 280%
Delta: 140pp — change management investment doubles ROI

ADOPTION VELOCITY BENCHMARKS
Quick win (first tangible AI result to users): within 90 days = 78% programme continuation
Quick win at 90-120 days: 61% continuation
No quick win within 120 days: 38% continuation
Key insight: the 90-day proof point is the single most important milestone in the first phase

RESISTANCE PATTERNS BY ROLE
Middle management: 41% active resistance (highest)
Reason: AI perceived as undermining their value add and decision authority
Frontline workers: 22% resistance (lower when AI framed as reducing tedious tasks)
Clinical staff (healthcare): 34% resistance (fear of AI replacing clinical judgement)
Finance analysts (FS): 18% resistance (see AI as productivity tool, not threat)
C-suite: 12% active resistance (highest sponsor of AI in most organisations)

LIGHTHOUSE USER PROGRAMME
Organisations with structured lighthouse user programme: 24%
Broad adoption rate with lighthouse programme: 2.8x faster than without
Lighthouse user selection criteria: early adopter, respected peer, enthusiastic communicator
Lighthouse user to team adoption timeline: 60-90 days per team
Return on lighthouse investment: $0 incremental cost, 2.8x adoption acceleration

TRAINING AND LEARNING BENCHMARKS
Training delivered without immediate practice: 62% revert within 30 days
Training with immediate use case and practice environment: 84% retention
Training format preference (by role):
- Executives: 90-minute case study workshops
- Managers: half-day scenario-based learning
- Frontline: micro-learning (10-15 minute modules in flow of work)

PARALLEL RUN RISK
Parallel run (AI + manual simultaneously): necessary for safety — but 60-90 day maximum
Extended parallel run (> 90 days): 2.3x cost, workforce never fully adopts AI
Hard cutover with safety net: achieves 88% adoption in first 30 days
Indefinite parallel run: adopted by 31% of organisations — a failure pattern

MEASUREMENT AND CONSEQUENCE
KPIs tracked but no manager consequence: 38% of target adoption at 18 months
Manager performance review includes AI adoption metrics: 81% of target adoption at 18 months
Monthly team-level adoption dashboard: 2.1x adoption vs. quarterly-only
Organisations that celebrate AI wins publicly: 2.4x higher adoption across other teams
