AI PROGRAMME PHASES, TIMELINES, AND MILESTONE BENCHMARKS
Source: AbarVa Phase Gate Delivery Framework

PHASE 0 — DISCOVERY AND STRATEGY (4-8 WEEKS)
Deliverables:
- Situation diagnosis (5-7 critical findings with financial impact)
- Contradiction analysis (stated strategy vs. data reality)
- Data readiness baseline (0-100 score with gap analysis)
- Use case prioritisation (5-10 use cases ranked by ROI and feasibility)
- Investment thesis (programme size, timeline, expected ROI)
- Governance model recommendation

Risk of skipping Phase 0: 2.4x more spent correcting strategic misdirection in Phase 2
Phase 0 investment: $280,000-640,000 (typical)
Phase 0 ROI: prevents $2.4-8.4M in wasted Phase 2 investment

PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION (3-6 MONTHS)
Deliverables:
- Data governance framework established
- Cloud platform selected and provisioned
- Data catalogue deployed
- Master data management initiated
- AI Centre of Excellence established (or CDO appointed)
- Data quality programme initiated on critical datasets

Common mistake: skipping foundation to "get to AI faster"
Cost of skipping: 2.1x higher Phase 2 cost (rework)
Foundation is chronically underfunded: 44% of AI programmes allocate < 15% of budget to Phase 1

PHASE 2 — FIRST USE CASE (3-6 MONTHS)
Deliverables:
- First AI use case in production
- Baseline KPIs established and locked (before go-live)
- Model monitoring in place (performance, drift)
- 90-day quick win documented and communicated

90-day milestone: working proof of concept in controlled environment
Key risk: scope creep — stakeholders want to add use cases before first is stable
Scope rule: "one use case in production before another begins"
Use case selection: highest ROI AND sufficient data — not just highest ROI

PHASE 3 — SCALE (6-18 MONTHS)
Deliverables:
- 3-5 AI use cases in production
- Self-service analytics for business users
- AI CoE running independently of external consultants
- AI model monitoring automated

Key risk: AI team attrition (28% annual attrition in scale phase)
Retention investment: pay market, give compute access, reduce bureaucracy
Governance: quarterly CXO review of AI KPIs — mandatory

PHASE 4 — COMPOUND (18-36 MONTHS)
Deliverables:
- AI embedded in core business processes
- AI programme financially self-funding through ROI
- Measurable competitive advantage vs. peers
- Institutional AI capability (not consultant-dependent)

Key risk: complacency — reducing AI investment just as compounding returns begin
Warning signal: programme budget reduced after first positive ROI
Correct response: reinvest ROI into next wave of AI use cases

TOTAL TIMELINE BENCHMARKS
Time to measurable competitive advantage:
- Large organisations (>$5B revenue): 30-48 months
- Mid-size ($1-5B): 18-30 months
- Small (<$1B): 12-18 months
Most common mistake: expecting AI competitive advantage in under 12 months (unrealistic)

PROGRAMME INVESTMENT BENCHMARKS BY SIZE
$1B revenue organisation: $4-8M AI programme (12-18 months Phase 1-2)
$5B revenue organisation: $18-38M AI programme (24-36 months Phase 1-3)
$10B revenue organisation: $42-88M AI programme (36-48 months Phase 1-4)
ROI at programme completion: 3.4-4.8x for structured programme
