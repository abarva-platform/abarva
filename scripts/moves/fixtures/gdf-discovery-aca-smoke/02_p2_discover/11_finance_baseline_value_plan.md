## Transparent model to use later

1. Separate one-time implementation effort from recurring platform, support, and
   measure-maintenance cost.
2. Estimate low/base/high hours by work package and role. Label every value as
   sourced, a planning assumption, or pending confirmation.
3. Compare internal and vendor delivery on the same scope and acceptance
   criteria; disclose rate source, overhead treatment, and excluded costs.
4. If Claude Code or Codex are assumed to accelerate implementation, show the
   eligible tasks, assumed productivity factor, retained human-review hours, and
   net hours separately. Do not reduce test, security, privacy, or clinical
   review to zero.
5. Keep benefits at zero/unquantified until Finance supplies and approves a
   defensible baseline. Do not multiply assumed hours by a made-up labor cost
   and call it savings.
