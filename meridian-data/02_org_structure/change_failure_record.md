# Meridian Health — Change Failure Record

## DENIALS-2024

**Approved date and budget:** See program archive; values preserved in evidence ledger where material.  
**Status:** Archived / scar-tissue active.  
**Failure mode tags:** PAT-AI-008, PAT-AI-005, PAT-AI-010 as applicable.

### What was promised

The program promised faster operations, better clinical or financial outcomes, and a reusable transformation muscle.

### What happened

Approved in 2023 with a $14M budget and paused in 2024 after $8M sunk. Vendor: Cohere Health. The pilot showed 80% denial-prevention on a cherry-picked cohort; production showed 22% once payer mix, specialty variance, incomplete CBO change management, and messy documentation patterns arrived. Failure modes: PAT-AI-008 and PAT-AI-005. Dr. Aiden Walsh exited Q1 2025; Patricia Okafor rotated into VP Revenue Cycle. Cohere was initially severed and later re-engaged for prior authorization, making every Cohere discussion politically sensitive.

### Personnel impact

The personnel consequence is not limited to a named leader; it changed how Meridian sponsors demand proof, change-management plans, and kill criteria.

### Organizational scar tissue

Current programs now carry explicit dissent logs, attribution methods, and operating-model workstreams because these failures taught Meridian that technology success is not the same as enterprise adoption.

### Lessons learned

- Do not scale from a curated pilot cohort without production-mix testing.
- Operating-model change must be budgeted and governed, not narrated.
- Clinical trust and regulatory accountability cannot be retrofitted at P4.
- Provider-plan integration needs named owners on both sides.
