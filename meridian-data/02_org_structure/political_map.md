# Meridian Health — Political Map & Coalition Patterns

## Executive coalition map

Meridian's executive politics are not theatrical; they are structural. Dr. Elaine Morales and the board want margin recovery without stalling the provider-plan integration story that differentiates Meridian. David Park, CFO, has built a cost-takeout coalition with Sarah O'Brien in operations and parts of the revenue-cycle function. His view is that DENIALS-2024 proved that technology programs without operating-model accountability become capital leaks.

Dr. Jennifer Wexler and Dr. Marcus Reid anchor the clinical AI coalition. They support ambient documentation and governance discipline, but they push back when finance frames clinical AI only as labor productivity. Dr. Anita Krishnamurthy is the bridge figure: new enough to avoid ownership of the 2024 failures, senior enough to reset the data and AI operating model, and still learning where Meridian's formal org chart is less important than its informal clinical alliances.

Rebecca Hollings and Karen Mercer are aligned after the board's AI risk policy. They are not anti-AI, but they are explicitly anti-shadow-AI. Their credibility rose after three unapproved AI scribe pilots surfaced in March 2026.

## Plan-vs-provider tension

Meridian Health Plans is not an accessory business. At 1.4M covered lives and about $5.0B in premium revenue, it shapes strategy. Thomas Hartwell argues that MA growth and quality bonus performance require stronger provider-plan data flows. Dr. Marcus Reid accepts the strategic logic but guards clinical autonomy and pushes back when plan medical management feels like utilization pressure disguised as quality work.

The HIPAA same-entity analysis permits many internal data flows, but practical constraints still bite: different operating committees, separate vendor contracts, narrower access controls, and different incentives. Provider leaders want capacity, quality, and clinician trust protected. Plan leaders want network adequacy, prior-auth cycle time, and STAR performance protected. Both are right often enough to make the tension persistent.

Champions: Park and Okafor. Skeptics: anyone who lived through DENIALS-2024, especially CBO supervisors who remember the pilot-to-production collapse. Marcus Chen is trusted technically but does not own the operating model.
