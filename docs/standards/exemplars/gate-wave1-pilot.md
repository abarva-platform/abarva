# Wave 1 Pilot Gate

## RACI

RACI: CTO is the single accountable owner. CFO approves value treatment. CISO approves AI egress posture. Head of Engineering owns cohort execution. DevEx Analyst owns evidence.

## Required artifacts

Required artifacts and completion criteria: signed charter, baseline report, pilot cohort roster, tool governance design, reallocation queue, and risk register. Completion criteria require evidence links and owner sign-off.

## Evidence anchors

Evidence anchors: audit trail IDs for charter approval, DORA extraction hash, survey export hash, tool license snapshot, and Tower value baseline.

## Numeric kill criteria

Kill criteria: adoption below 45 percent by week 6, DORA delta below 5 percent by week 12, change failure rate worsens by more than 3 points, license cost exceeds $45/user/month, or attrition risk rises above 5 percent.

## Sensitivity analysis

Sensitivity analysis at gate: if adoption shifts +/-20%, cohort either narrows to high-AI-fit teams or expands. If value shifts +/-20%, CFO threshold moves from $1.5M to $1.2M-$1.8M.

## Pre-mortem ritual

Pre-mortem ritual: 15 min before decision. Participants name top five reasons the pilot could be misleading, with mitigation owner.

## Decision capture and dissent log

Decision capture includes continue, narrow, expand, or kill; decision owner; rationale; dissent log; and follow-up evidence request.

## Time budget

Time budget: P50 gate review 60 min, P90 120 min. Evidence pack lock occurs 48h before the meeting.

## Hand-off ritual

Hand-off to next gate: DevEx Analyst posts final evidence pack, CTO names Wave 2 candidate teams, CFO confirms value tracking, and CISO confirms policy posture.

## Maturity model

Maturity model: stage 3 is governed pilot. Stage 5 great looks like verified quarterly reallocation, stable tool governance, and auditable kill/continue decisions.
