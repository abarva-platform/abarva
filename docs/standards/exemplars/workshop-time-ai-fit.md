# TIME x AI-fit Diagnostic Workshop

## Pre-read

Pre-read is 25 min and sequenced: 10 min TIME glossary, 10 min AI-fit scoring examples, 5 min participant ranking of five applications. Glossary defines Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate, Wardley, and Team Topologies.

## Facilitator brief

Objectives: classify 30 priority apps and agree the pilot segment. Success criteria: 80 percent of apps classified with no unresolved owner. Escalation: sponsor tie-breaks disputed cells. Time-boxes are enforced every 10 min.

## Minute-by-minute agenda

00:00-00:10 hypothesis and ground rules. 00:10-00:40 TIME classification. 00:40-00:50 break. 00:50-01:20 Wardley overlay. 01:20-01:50 Team Topologies implications. 01:50-02:00 break. 02:00-02:30 AI-fit scoring. 02:30-02:45 pre-mortem. 02:45-03:00 decision capture.

## Numerical hypothesis

Hypothesis to test: at least 60 percent of Run spend sits in Tolerate or Eliminate apps, and at least 25 percent of engineering hours are tied to low-AI-fit systems.

## Facilitation tactics

Push when a sponsor labels every app Invest. Listen when operations leaders surface regulatory constraints. Escalate trigger: any app with revenue impact above $20M and no accountable owner.

## Worksheets / canvases

Pre-built worksheet set: TIME matrix, Wardley canvas, AI-fit heat map, dependency arrow map, and decision capture canvas.

## Decision capture

Decision capture template records decision, by whom, why, dissent, follow-up, owner, and due date. Dissent is captured as a named objection, not a footnote.

## Pre-mortem

15 min pre-mortem: "It is 90 days later and the pilot failed." Participants name failure modes, leading indicators, owner, and mitigation.

## Stakeholder map

Influence x interest grid: CTO high/high, CFO high/medium, CISO high/high, app owners medium/high, works council medium/low. Pre-work 1:1s named for CFO, CISO, and the two high-risk app owners.

## Post-read and commitments tracker

Post-read ships within 24h with classified apps, dissent log, commitments tracker, next workshop, and unresolved data asks.
