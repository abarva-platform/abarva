What can we do about it?
Three Move shapes are plausible given the NIM trend, your portfolio composition, and the AI trajectory for regional banks:

1. Credit decisioning modernization (Decision Layer) — strongest evidence basis. Your portfolio is 80% front-office; middle-office is underweight. Decisioning modernization addresses NIM compression at its source (better risk-adjusted pricing) and aligns with the trajectory toward middle-office automation.

2. Deposit pricing optimization (Decision Layer) — addresses NIM directly via deposit-side rather than asset-side. Lower complexity but smaller upside.

3. Fee-income diversification (Experience Layer) — sidesteps NIM compression by reducing dependency on net interest income. Longer time-to-value; bigger strategic shift.
