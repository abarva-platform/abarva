## Plain-English Summary

This release makes the Intelligence right-side canvas explain where its content comes from. The canvas remains a fast deterministic render, but it now carries a governed pack identity, version, review status, source mode, provenance labels, validation status, and caveats. This is the first production slice toward regenerating canvas packs from tenant context plus industry corpus through an offline reviewed workflow instead of hiding hardcoded content inside the page.

## Layer Impact

- `global-control-lane`: adds a shared governed canvas-pack registry for Intelligence.
- `presentation`: adds visible provenance and validation labels to the Intelligence canvas header.
- `governance`: separates tenant-loaded context, industry corpus, derived benchmark, and inference-boundary language.
