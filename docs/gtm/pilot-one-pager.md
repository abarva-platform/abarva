# AbarVa Enterprise Pilot One-Pager

Status: sales-ready draft
Owner: founder
Backlog task: T064

## The Problem

Enterprise leaders are being asked to make high-stakes AI, sourcing,
modernization, and operating-model decisions with fragmented context:
spreadsheets, decks, vendor claims, meeting notes, disconnected systems, and
general-purpose AI tools that cannot explain what evidence they used.

The result is slow decisions, weak accountability, hallucination risk, and
limited reuse of institutional knowledge.

## The AbarVa Pilot

AbarVa provides a governed AI decision-support workspace for one enterprise
client. It combines:

- a secure client-scoped data and evidence boundary,
- curated enterprise corpus and patterns,
- specialized agents for strategy, evidence, portfolio, sourcing, and setup,
- human approval gates for consequential actions,
- release, audit, and Responsible AI controls.

## What the Client Gets

| Workstream | Pilot deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Executive Home | One place for priorities, decisions, value paths, and accountable next actions. |
| Moves / Nexus | Strategic Moves shaped from idea to gate with evidence, owners, and human approval. |
| Source / Sentinel | Sourcing event support, vendor evidence, BAFO/pricing proof, risk caveats, and decision packets. |
| Tower / Atlas | Portfolio-level synthesis, executive tradeoffs, value/risk framing, and forecast assumptions. |
| Admin / Steward | Users, setup, templates, governed data intake, quarantine, approvals, and readiness posture. |

## Why Now

The pilot gives the client a controlled way to answer:

- Which enterprise AI or modernization decisions should we make first?
- Which recommendations are grounded in evidence versus assumption?
- Which vendor/source claims survive scrutiny?
- Which patterns apply to our operating model?
- What is missing before a decision should move forward?

## Success Criteria

A successful pilot should produce:

- active use by named executive and operator personas,
- at least one strategic Move or Source workflow reaching a human decision gate,
- approved data and evidence loaded through governed intake,
- answer-quality samples that show grounded, useful, and safe responses,
- a 90-day outcome packet with conversion recommendation.

## Commercial Path

The pilot is designed to convert into a year-1 production subscription when
the success criteria are met. The current target year-1 platform fee is
approximately `$950,000` ACV before add-ons, with Source available as a
separately priced module when it is the primary wedge.

## Buyer-Friendly Summary

AbarVa is not a general chatbot. It is a governed decision-support layer for
enterprise leaders: context-bound, evidence-led, human-approved, and designed
to turn fragmented information into accountable decisions.
