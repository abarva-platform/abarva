## AbarVa Position In One Line

AbarVa is the tenant-grounded decision OS for CXO AI and business bets: it
links patterns, moves, sourcing events, portfolio posture, evidence, and human
approval into one governed operating loop.

The competitive answer is not "we have a better chatbot." It is:

- Enterprise search retrieves knowledge; AbarVa records the decision made from
  the knowledge.
- Horizontal AI platforms generate and automate work; AbarVa governs
  consequential AI/business moves with evidence, approval, and release posture.
- Consulting firms advise and implement transformations; AbarVa is the durable
  operating substrate that survives the engagement.
- Model providers supply frontier capability; AbarVa supplies tenant context,
  workflow, accountability, and customer-specific data-plane posture.

## Buyer-Question Playbook

| Buyer question | Best response |
| --- | --- |
| "Why not Glean?" | "Use Glean if the primary problem is finding knowledge across workplace systems. Use AbarVa when the problem is making and governing AI/business decisions from that knowledge: who owns the move, what evidence supports it, what changed in the portfolio, and what was approved." |
| "Why not Hebbia?" | "Hebbia is strong for heavy document analysis and research workflows. AbarVa is broader than analysis: it binds insights to Moves, Source events, Tower portfolio posture, and human accountability." |
| "Why not Writer?" | "Writer is a strong enterprise model/application platform. AbarVa can sit above model platforms because the buyer still needs tenant context, decision rights, evidence records, and cross-surface workflow." |
| "Why not just Claude Enterprise?" | "Claude Enterprise gives the organization governed access to Claude. AbarVa turns model capability into a decision operating layer with client-specific context, product surfaces, approvals, and audit evidence." |
| "Why not BCG or McKinsey?" | "BCG/McKinsey are credible transformation partners. AbarVa is the durable operating substrate: it keeps the decision workflow, evidence, and accountability alive after advisory work ends." |
| "Can AbarVa coexist with these?" | "Yes. Glean/Hebbia/Writer/Claude can be data, model, or analysis layers. BCG/McKinsey can help shape transformation. AbarVa owns the governed decision loop." |

## Where We Should Be Humble

- Glean will usually win if the buyer only wants enterprise search or employee
  assistant adoption.
- Hebbia will usually win if the buyer only wants high-volume document analysis
  for finance/legal teams.
- Writer will usually win if the buyer wants a full enterprise generative-AI
  platform or proprietary LLM stack as the center.
- Anthropic-direct will usually win if the buyer wants a model provider
  relationship and workforce Claude rollout.
- BCG/McKinsey will usually win if the buyer wants board-level advisory,
  executive alignment, and a large transformation services team.

AbarVa should win when the buyer needs a durable, auditable operating layer for
making and governing AI/business decisions.
