## Executive Read

Delta is a credible modernization and AI-governance prospect because public sources show a large global technology estate, an active digital/technology leadership transition, visible AI products, and recent cloud/digital investment themes. The safest AbarVa position is not "we provide developer methodology." It is "we provide the operating layer above modernization and AI tooling: portfolio decisions, evidence packets, value measurement, vendor governance, and human-approved execution."

## AbarVa Positioning

Lead with modernization governance:

> Delta already has digital platforms, cloud modernization, and AI pilots. The missing layer is the executive operating system that turns those programs into governed decisions, value proof, and accountable human approvals.

Use three wedges:

1. Modernization Program OS: sequence, evidence, owner, blocker, and value packets across strategic technology initiatives.
2. AI Product Governance: measure and govern AI-enabled development and customer-facing AI without claiming to replace Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor, or Delta's engineering methods.
3. Source / Vendor Decision Layer: support vendor and sourcing decisions with traceable evidence, assumptions, and approval records.

## Discovery Questions

- Who owns the operating model for AI initiatives after the digital/technology function consolidation?
- Which current modernization programs have the biggest gap between executive ambition and measurable value proof?
- Where do AI projects create governance friction: data access, model risk, customer experience, compliance, cybersecurity, or vendor accountability?
- Which decision artifacts would help the CDTO, CDO, CTO, CIO, or CFO make faster decisions with less ambiguity?
- Is the primary need methodology for engineering teams, or measurement/governance above the AI development tools?
