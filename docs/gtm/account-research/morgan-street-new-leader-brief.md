## AbarVa Positioning

Lead with first-100-days operating control:

> AbarVa gives a new IT/data/innovation leader a governed operating layer for the first 100 days: what they inherited, what matters most, what evidence supports each decision, and where human approval is required before action.

Use three wedges:

1. Portfolio Context Map: operating companies, current initiatives, data sources, leaders, vendors, and unresolved decisions.
2. Innovation / AI Governance Ledger: early AI and digital initiatives with evidence, assumptions, model-risk notes, human approvals, and executive visibility.
3. Source / Sourcing Intelligence: vendor and sourcing decisions across technology, marketing, supply chain, and operating-company services.
