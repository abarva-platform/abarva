## One-Line Positioning

AbarVa is the modernization program operating layer above Delta's cloud, digital, AI, and engineering tool investments: it helps leadership decide what to accelerate, measure what is working, govern AI-enabled execution, and keep human accountability attached to consequential decisions.

## Why This Is Not an AI Workflow Pitch

Do not lead with "AI workflow." Delta already has visible AI and digital work, including Delta Concierge and a combined Digital & Technology leadership function. The sharper pitch is:

> AI is the accelerator. The actual executive problem is operating discipline: which modernization bets matter, which AI-enabled work is safe to scale, what value is being created, and who approved the next decision.

That frame keeps AbarVa away from developer-tool replacement and into the layer where it is strongest: evidence, decisions, value, governance, and accountable execution.

## CTO / CDTO Conversation Opener

> You already have the platforms and digital ambition. The harder part is turning a portfolio of modernization and AI initiatives into a governed operating rhythm: what to prioritize, what value is proven, what risks need human approval, and which decisions should be escalated. AbarVa gives that layer a system.

## Discovery Questions

- Which modernization program would most benefit from weekly evidence-backed decisions?
- Where does leadership lose confidence today: value proof, timeline, vendor accountability, data quality, or ownership?
- What AI-enabled development work is already in motion, and how is ROI measured?
- Which decision would be easier with a sourced evidence packet and named human approver?
- What would make this pilot a clean yes/no after 60 days?

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Delta has a $3B IT budget unless a verified source is supplied.

- Do not imply autonomous decisions; every consequential decision remains human-approved.
