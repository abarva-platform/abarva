## Two-Page Teaser Draft

### Headline

First 100 Days: A Governed Operating Layer for IT, Data, Innovation, and Portfolio Decisions

### Opening Frame

New leaders inherit more than a function. They inherit decisions already in motion, vendors already embedded, data that lives in different places, and executives who need confidence fast.

AbarVa helps a leader turn that first-100-days window into a governed operating rhythm:

- what was inherited,
- what should be prioritized,
- what evidence supports each decision,
- what needs human approval,
- and what can be shown to leadership as early momentum.

### Why Morgan Street Is A Fit

Morgan Street's public profile points to portfolio complexity: multiple operating companies, global scale, supply chain, marketing, consumer products, convenience/vending, and active growth through talent, acquisition, innovation, facilities, and market expansion. That is exactly the kind of environment where a new leader benefits from a portfolio context map and a repeatable decision system.

### The AbarVa Offer

In 30 days, AbarVa can help create the first operating view:

- operating-company context map,
- initiative and vendor inventory,
- unresolved decision list,
- risk/value scan,
- evidence packet template,
- and weekly leadership packet.

In 60 days, AbarVa can help move from context to action:

- 3-5 candidate Moves,
- Source quick scan for vendor/sourcing decisions,
- AI/innovation governance ledger,
- named human decision owners,
- and first set of value/risk assumptions.

In 90 days, AbarVa can help produce a board-ready operating narrative:

- what changed,
- what decisions were made,
- what value/risk was surfaced,
- what needs executive approval next,
- and how the function will continue the decision cadence.

### Conversation Ask

> I put together a first-100-days framework for leaders stepping into IT, data, innovation, or portfolio operating roles. It is not a product pitch; it is a way to think through inherited work, quick wins, governance, and decision confidence. Worth 30 minutes to pressure-test against what you are walking into?

## Thoughtful Value Piece

Use this as the Week 2-3 value-add if outreach is already warm:

> The hidden challenge in a first-100-days role is not the strategy deck. It is decision hygiene. A new leader needs to know which inherited decisions are still reversible, which vendors are already shaping the roadmap, where data is reliable enough to act, and which moves need executive cover. The organizations that move fastest tend to make the operating layer explicit: evidence, owner, decision, next action.

## Discovery Questions

- Which inherited programs are already politically or financially hard to reverse?
- Which operating companies require immediate visibility?
- Where would better decision evidence change the conversation with finance or the CEO?
- What are the first three decisions that need to be made, paused, or escalated?
- What would a useful weekly leadership packet contain?
