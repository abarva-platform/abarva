## Discovery Call Scripts

### PHS Day 1-2 Usage Debrief

Goal: understand what the CDAO tried, what landed, what is missing, and whether the AI-strategy pressure is real.

Questions:

- What did you try in the product, and what felt immediately useful?
- What did not feel specific enough to your environment?
- What are the top 3 healthcare value levers you would care about first?
- Is the bigger problem AI strategy, modernization execution, sourcing/AMS decisions, or data readiness?
- Who else must be in the room before a pilot scope becomes real: CIO, CFO, sourcing colleague, legal, security, or data owner?
- Can you authorize a paid pilot directly, or does this need joint approval?

Required evidence to capture:

- Buyer pain priority.
- Budget authority and approval path.
- Actual or synthetic data stance.
- Healthcare value levers validated or rejected.
- Whether PHS is real public entity, confidential client, or synthetic placeholder.

### Delta / KK Day 1-2 Usage Debrief

Goal: decide whether to proceed with AbarVa as governance/measurement layer, pivot to Source, or pause because the need is developer methodology.

Questions:

- What were you hoping AbarVa would solve for AI-led product development?
- Is the harder gap knowing how engineering teams should use AI, or knowing whether AI-enabled work is creating governed value?
- Which modernization program would benefit most from evidence-backed weekly decisions?
- What AI tools are already in use or being evaluated?
- Who owns measurement: CTO, CDTO, engineering leadership, finance, risk, or transformation office?
- What would need to be true for a 60-day Lane A/Lane B pilot to be worth your time?

Decision fork:

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Governance/measurement pain is clear. | Proceed to Lane A + Lane B draft scope. |
| Vendor/IMS/source pain is stronger. | Pivot to Source-led entry point. |
| Methodology-only pain dominates. | Document no-go or future corpus-wave condition. |

### Surekha First-Touch / New-Leader Conversation

Goal: stay helpful and timely, not sales-heavy.

Light first-touch:

> Congrats on the new role. I put together a first-100-days framework for leaders stepping into IT, data, innovation, or portfolio operating roles. It is not a product pitch; it is a way to pressure-test inherited work, quick wins, governance, and decision confidence. Worth 30 minutes once you are in seat?

Discovery questions:

- What are the first three decisions you expect to inherit?
- Which operating companies or functions need visibility fastest?
- Where would a weekly evidence packet help with CEO/CFO alignment?
- Is your biggest first-quarter need portfolio clarity, AI/data strategy, vendor visibility, or operating cadence?
- What would make the first 100 days feel successful?
