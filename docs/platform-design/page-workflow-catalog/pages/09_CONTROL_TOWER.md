# Control Tower

## Page / Category Name

Control Tower.

## Purpose

Show operating pressure across initiatives, projects, staff augmentation, technology stack, volumetrics, risks, and executive decision needs.

## Primary User Question

Where is execution pressure building, and what operating decision is required?

## Agent Anchor

Atlas anchors operating intelligence. Nexus can explain program context. Sentinel flags risk. Steward flags governance or setup gaps.

## Journey / Workflow State

Operating state: healthy, watch, pressured, blocked, decision needed, owner assigned, mitigation active, or resolved.

## UI Zones

- Executive operating brief.
- Pressure cards.
- Portfolio table.
- Risk and dependency map.
- Owner/next action.
- Evidence and source links.

## Data Required

Projects, milestones, staffing, technology stack, volumes, dependencies, risks, owners, status, and evidence links.

## Seed Data Today

Seeded tower projects, seeded pressure signals, seeded staffing and technology examples, and deterministic Atlas brief.

## Real Data Tomorrow

Project management data, staffing data, technology inventory, operational metrics, risk logs, evidence ledger, and executive decisions.

## Actions Supported

Open pressure area, assign owner, review evidence, request mitigation, connect to program, and escalate decision.

## Missing-Data Behavior

Show unavailable data state by operating domain and identify the system or owner needed.

## MVP / V1 / V2 Classification

MVP: pressure brief and operating table. V1: dependencies and mitigation tracking. V2: forecasting and scenario planning.

## Dependencies

Tower data model, program links, evidence ledger, Atlas contracts, user/owner model, and Admin/Setup readiness.

## Wireframe Required

Yes.

## What Not To Show If Data Is Missing

Do not show operating forecasts, pressure scores, or executive recommendations without source metrics and ownership state.
