Workspace architecture · Apr 27 2026

One template, four jobs.

Setup, Programs, Intelligence, and Control Tower share the same chrome — left rail, top bar, slim middle strip, agent column on the left, working pane on the right. The agent never moves; the work changes. What follows is the same architecture filled four different ways.

Common chrome

What stays identical on every surface

Left icon rail (76px), top bar (48px), middle strip (44px), agent column (480px) with voice + suggested actions + input box, and a working pane that fills the rest. The aesthetic — paper, navy ink, Fraunces serif for moments of voice, Inter sans for working content — is locked.

What varies per surface

Lead agent · middle strip · working content

Steward leads Setup, Nexus leads Programs, Sentinel leads Intelligence, Atlas leads Control Tower. The middle strip's semantics shift (sub-nav · phase journey · tier filter · severity filter), and the working pane fills with whatever the maestro is operating on for that surface.
