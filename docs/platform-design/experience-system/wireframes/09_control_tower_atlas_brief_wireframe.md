# Control Tower Atlas Brief Wireframe

## Purpose

Give executives portfolio-level synthesis across Programs, Source, Intelligence, and Admin readiness.

## Primary User Question

What decision pressure exists across the enterprise portfolio?

## Above the Fold

```text
Top nav with Control Tower active
[Atlas executive brief]
[Decision pressure list]
[Portfolio value/risk table]
[Cross-surface readiness and evidence gaps]
```

## Journey / Progress Behavior

Use compact phase/stage strips in rows. Use value realization journey where value is the subject.

## Agent Role

Atlas synthesizes. Nexus, Sentinel, and Steward are cited as sources when their surface data drives the brief.

## Table / Card Behavior

Executive pressure list plus table. Avoid a wall of charts.

## Drawers

Pressure item opens evidence, source surface, owner, due date, value/risk impact, and recommended action.

## States

Low evidence, stale data, cross-surface contradiction, executive decision needed, complete.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decision pressure is clear.
- Evidence source is visible.
- Executive next action is explicit.
