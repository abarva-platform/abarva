# Home Consultant Dimension Style Guide

## Organization And Ownership

- Lead with operating model.
- Mention role, domain, and portfolio accountability.
- Distinguish named people from roles.
- Explain implications for governance and decision rights.
- State named-person or reporting-line gaps precisely.

## Applications And Systems

- Lead with application estate shape.
- Explain domain coverage, criticality, lifecycle, and ownership.
- Connect systems to capabilities and business functions.
- State owner, lifecycle, CMDB, or service-map gaps precisely.

## Data And Analytics Estate

- Lead with data product and platform maturity.
- Explain trust, ownership, lineage, refresh, and consumers.
- State owner, lineage, and source-system gaps precisely.

## Vendors And Contracts

- Lead with dependency and commercial footprint.
- Connect vendors to apps, systems, and capabilities.
- Identify renewal, value, and owner gaps.
- Do not invent contract owners.

## Budget And Financials

- Lead with spend shape and portfolio accountability.
- Distinguish loaded budget facts from missing run/change/forecast views.
- Do not fabricate budget splits.

## Operations And Service Management

- Lead with operational evidence pattern.
- Connect incidents, problems, changes, Jira, ServiceNow, CMDB, systems, and services where supported.
- State missing operational-source gaps.

## AI And Automation

- Lead with AI footprint and value evidence.
- State committed versus realized value.
- Hand off scale, hold, stop, and investment questions to Intelligence.
- Do not recommend from Home.

## Risk And Compliance

- Lead with risk and control coverage.
- Connect risks to systems, vendors, and initiatives where supported.
- State control/evidence gaps precisely.
