# Industrial CIO Back-Office Demo Package

## Purpose

This package turns the existing Industrial Demo / Lakeshore V6 substrate into a Morgan Street CIO demo story: an Enterprise Innovation, AI Enablement, and Value Office that redesigns Shared Services work, governs AI/automation, measures value with Finance, and reuses context across functions.

This is a runtime proposal for Intelligence answers, not a new synthetic tenant generation pass.

## CIO Story

The CIO and VP Innovation should see aVa answer with a point of view:

- Start with Treasury and Finance because the loaded tenant evidence is strongest there.
- Use Kyriba, bank connectivity, payment controls, finance close, reconciliation, reporting, and ServiceNow finance support as the first value-office proof path.
- Treat HR and Legal as credible next waves, but do not scale-recommend them until source evidence is loaded or the client provides values.
- Ask the executive for permission to use planning assumptions or for current values when exact sizing is missing.

## Existing V6 Evidence Used

- Business functions: Corporate Treasury, Finance and Controller, FP&A, Procurement, Digital and IT, Data and Analytics, Cybersecurity and Risk, and adjacent Shared Services capabilities.
- Ownership: CIO, VP ERP, VP Finance Technology, VP Integration, Chief Data Officer, VP IT Operations, Treasurer, Controller, CFO, and adjacent roles.
- Systems: SAP ECC Central Finance, SAP S/4 pilot instance, SAP FI/CO, SAP BPC, SAP PI/PO, Kyriba implementation tenant, Quantum legacy TMS, BlackLine, Power BI, ServiceNow, and related integration/data platforms where present.
- AI/automation: Kyriba rollout assistant, treasury bank connectivity control evidence, liquidity forecasting automation, finance close/reporting semantic layer, ServiceNow finance support agent, finance AI narrative/variance explainer, M365 Copilot finance rollout, and developer productivity for finance integrations.
- Risks/controls: Kyriba mapping defects, bank file rejection, SAP IDoc failure, BlackLine aging, Power BI refresh failure, finance service desk repeat issues, payment approval exceptions, SWIFT connectivity alerts, supplier master mismatch, and close-calendar delay.
- Metrics: cash visibility, payment straight-through processing, close cycle time, liquidity forecast accuracy, control evidence completeness, Finance Copilot adoption, bank connectivity certification, and related KPI definitions.

## Runtime Behavior

When the tenant is Industrial Demo/Lakeshore and the question is about Morgan Street, Shared Services, Treasury, Finance, HR, Legal, process transformation, AI enablement, or the Value Office:

1. aVa receives a focused tenant source named `Industrial Demo CIO Shared Services value-office context`.
2. The prompt addendum asks Claude to act as a senior CIO transformation advisor.
3. Claude is instructed to lead with a direct point of view, distinguish loaded evidence from assumptions, and ask for values or permission to assume when precision is missing.
4. Right-canvas visuals may show a roadmap, lighthouse comparison, readiness matrix, or assumption-led opportunity map, but non-tenant context must be labeled.
5. Branch choices are Claude-owned and renderer-displayed: use planning assumptions, enter current values, start Treasury + Finance, add HR/Legal discovery, or create the office blueprint.

## Evidence Boundary

Board-grade claims require client signoff for:

- Finance-attested value baseline and value owner.
- Process volumes, cycle times, rework, exception rates, and unit costs.
- Business process owner and control owner by workflow.
- Certified system-of-record data, semantic definitions, and lineage.
- Adoption, role-change, and human-in-the-loop operating model.
- HR and Legal source evidence before scale recommendations in those functions.
