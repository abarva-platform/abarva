P0
P0 · Originate
Convert a signal or hypothesis into a structured Move with a sponsor candidate
Entry point: Signal from leadership, market event, or Nexus proactive alert
Surface: /strategic-moves/new (Originate page)
Duration: ~1 week
Gate out to: P1 Charter (on approval)
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P0.1 — Paste or describe the signal
User pastes a leadership memo, market report, or describes a pain point. Nexus extracts and structures the problem hypothesis.
↝ Nexus: classifies archetype, surfaces 3 entry-context chips
2
P0.2 — Complete scaffold (7 sections)
Problem statement · Classification · Target outcome · Timeline horizon · Sponsor candidate · Foundation checks (F1–F4) · Pattern match review
↝ Nexus: auto-drafts each section; user refines; evidence cited per field
3
P0.3 — Foundation check
F1 Budget authority · F2 Data access · F3 Sponsor candidate availability · F4 Scope boundary check
↝ Nexus: surfaces red-flag if any foundation check fails
4
P0.4 — Pattern match review
Nexus surfaces similar programs from the pattern catalog: archetype match, typical value range, known failure modes for this archetype.
5
P0.5 — Review brief and submit for approval
User reviews the P0 brief on the canvas and clicks "Submit for P1 Charter." Approval creates Move in DB, transitions to P1.
6
P0.6 — Gate & Promote

P1
P1 · Charter
Convert the P0 hypothesis into a sponsor-committed charter with a locked value range
Entry: P0 gate passed · Move in DB
Surface: /strategic-moves/[moveId]?phase=1
Duration: ~2 weeks
Gate out to: P2 Discover & Diagnose
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P1.1 — Sponsor kickoff
Named sponsor reviews P0 brief and confirms commitment. Nexus captures confirmation and records in substrate.
↝ Nexus: cannot name sponsor without ACL evidence (AH rule)
2
P1.2 — Hypothesis testing
Test the P0 problem hypothesis against available data. Validate that the problem is real, in-scope, and addressable by this organization.
3
P1.3 — Value range definition
Define low/high value estimate with assumptions documented. Nexus generates ROM from archetype benchmarks; sponsor refines.
↝ Nexus: every value claim cites source (R1 evidence rule)
4
P1.4 — Stakeholder map
Identify program stakeholders: executive sponsor, steering committee, SMEs, affected teams. Capture roles in substrate.
5
P1.5 — Charter gate & promote
Sponsor signs off on charter. All hard criteria met. Team promotes to P2.

P2
P2 · Discover & Diagnose
Lock the current-state baseline with auditable evidence and produce a ranked root cause analysis
Entry: P1 charter signed · sponsor confirmed
Surface: /strategic-moves/[moveId]?phase=2
Duration: ~4 weeks
Gate out to: P3 Design OR Discontinue
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P2.1 — Current state baseline
Collect and upload baseline data: AHT by queue type, FCR, volume by query category, agent utilization. Nexus structures and cites each metric.
↝ Nexus: records FIN-BASE-P2 with source citations required
2
P2.2 — Root cause analysis
Structured analysis of WHY the baseline is where it is. Nexus surfaces ≥2 ranked root causes from evidence collected.
3
P2.3 — Stakeholder interviews & synthesis
Interview agents, supervisors, and IT leads. Nexus synthesizes patterns across sessions into a discovery report.
↝ Nexus: synthesize capability from cross-phase pack
4
P2.4 — Discovery report & gate
Publish the P2 discovery report. Gate verdict: CONTINUE_TO_P3 or DISCONTINUE.
⚠ Nexus authority: can recommend DISCONTINUE if evidence doesn't support hypothesis

Apex example:
Baseline: AHT = 8.2 min (top-25 queries = 6.1 min avg, complex queries = 12.4 min). FCR = 61%. Root cause 1: agents lack real-time knowledge (no integrated KB). Root cause 2: routing logic sends 40% of AI-resolvable queries to agents. Verdict: CONTINUE_TO_P3.

P3
P3 · Design Future State
Convert the P2 diagnosis into a signed decision: architecture, operating model, target capability
Entry: P3 gate · CONTINUE_TO_P3 verdict · signed RCA
Duration: ~3 weeks
Gate out to: P4 Roadmap & Business Case
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P3.1 — Root cause → design traceability
For each RCA root cause, document the design element that addresses it. Every design decision must trace to a root cause.
↝ Nexus: R6 — rejects tool proposals without operating model first
2
P3.2 — Operating model design
Define who does what differently: roles, responsibilities, workflows, handoffs. The AI capability must be named AFTER the workflow change.
⚠ R6: "We'll use [Tool]" without workflow design → rejected
3
P3.3 — Solution architecture
Given the operating model, what technology and AI capabilities enable it. Vendor shortlist only after workflow is defined.
4
P3.4 — Design sign-off
Sponsor reviews and signs. P3 design record created. Gate evaluated.
Artifacts Created
H

H

Who does what differently · workflow delta · handoff points
H
Solution Architecture Record
Technology stack · integration points · AI capability spec
H
Signed P3 Design Decision (P3_DESIGN_SIGNED)
Sponsor sign-off required to unblock P4
Apex example:
RC1 (no KB) → AI-powered KB surfacing in agent desktop. RC2 (routing) → AI auto-resolution for top-25 query types before agent routing. Operating model: agents shift from answering to confirming; KB authors shift to curating. Technology: conversational AI + routing engine integration.

P4
P4 · Roadmap & Business Case
Convert the P3 design into an executable plan with full economics — and lock the Tower metric plan before handoff
Entry: P3 gate · CONTINUE_TO_P4 · signed P3 design · locked P2 baseline
Duration: ~4 weeks
Gate out to: P5 Mobilize & Handoff
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P4.1 — Roadmap construction
For each P3 design element: workstream, responsible owner, estimated effort, dependencies, milestones, RACI.
↝ Nexus: no timeline without resource plan (AH rule)
2
P4.2 — Business case economics
Cost model (ROM from archetype benchmarks, user-refined). Value plan against P2 baseline. NPV/IRR where required. Change readiness plan.
↝ Nexus: ROM estimate labeled as ROM; every assumption listed
3
P4.3 — Tower metric plan (proactive, mid-P4)
For each value lever from P2+P3: measurable signal, data source, baseline, target, timeline. Atlas agent metric plan locked here — not at gate.
⚠ P4 authority: Nexus proactively surfaces Tower metric plan mid-P4, not at gate
4
P4.4 — Gate review & funding approval
5 hard + 6 soft gate criteria. Sponsor signs business case. Funding approval recorded. RACI, vendor, and Tower metric plan all locked.

P5
P5 · Mobilize & Handoff
Prepare the delivery team and hand off to Tower — explicit named acceptance required, not passive acknowledgment
Entry: P4 gate · approved business case · locked Tower metric plan
Duration: ~4 weeks
Gate out to: Tower (status = handed_off)
Activities & Workflow Steps
1
P5.1 — Team assembly & RACI confirmation
Confirm delivery team is in place. Every workstream from P4 roadmap has a named lead. RACI finalized.
↝ Nexus: verify named owner per workstream before marking ready
2
P5.2 — Handoff package assembly
Package: P0 brief + P1 charter + P2 baseline + P3 design + P4 roadmap + Tower metric plan. Nexus assembles and formats for Tower/Atlas consumption.
↝ Tower: Atlas agent receives this package at handoff
3
P5.3 — Readiness verification
Verify data access, tooling, environments, and change management readiness. No open blockers.
4
P5.4 — Explicit Tower acceptance
Named delivery owner explicitly confirms in writing that they've reviewed the handoff pack and accept it as executable. "Noted" or "present in room" does not count.
⚠ R7: explicit named acceptance required — passive acknowledgment is not acceptance
5
P5.5 — Gate-out & handoff
All gate criteria met. Status set to handed_off. Move transitions to Tower. Atlas agent activated with metric plan.
