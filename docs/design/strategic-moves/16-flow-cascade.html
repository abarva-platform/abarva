Reading this cascade: Each step shows a frame (the screen the user sees). The dashed blue/amber/green outline highlights what the user clicks or what just changed. The left column explains what's happening in user terms — what they did, what they see, what's next.

What stays consistent across both flows: Same global AbarVa nav. Same Move identity card at the top of every workspace. Same chat-left / canvas-right shell. Same phase rail (interactive — clicking past phases lets you review history, clicking future phases lets you preview what's coming). Same "+ New Move" CTA placement. The visual continuity is the point — the user is always in the "Moves" world, just at different stages of one Move's journey or starting a new one.

What we deliberately did NOT show here (out of scope for v0.1): Move switcher (cmd+K to jump to another Move from inside a workspace) · Past phase view (clicking a closed phase in the rail to review what was decided) · Future phase preview · The "viewing past" / "previewing future" banners from Workspace v0.2 spec. Those are in the workspace v0.2 reference at docs/design/strategic-moves/15-workspace-v0.2.html. This cascade focuses on the linear journey, not the navigation freedom.
