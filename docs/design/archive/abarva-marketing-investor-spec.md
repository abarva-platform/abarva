### 4 · Unit economics

**Per-engagement gross margin story.**

| Line | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per engagement (6-month, Meridian-scale) | $800K - $2M | Outcome-share pricing |
| LLM cost | $5K - $10K | 0.5-1% of revenue (see cost table in Agent Atlas) |
| Maestro labor (0.25 FTE per engagement, 6 mo) | $75K | Loaded senior cost |
| Platform infrastructure allocation | $8K | Share of fixed costs |
| **Engagement gross margin** | **$700K - $1.9M** | **~90% gross margin** |

**Maestro leverage.**
- 4-6 concurrent engagements per Maestro
- At 5 engagements × $1.5M average, one Maestro generates $7.5M ARR at $300K loaded cost
- **~25:1 revenue-to-cost ratio on Maestro labor**

**Why the economics are SaaS-like, not services-like.**
LLM and platform scale linearly with usage, but platform cost share per engagement stays low because fixed-cost amortization improves with scale. Unlike consulting, where revenue and cost rise together — AbarVa decouples them.
