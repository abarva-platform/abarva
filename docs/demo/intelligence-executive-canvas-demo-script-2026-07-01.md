# Intelligence Executive Canvas Demo Script — 2026-07-01

## Purpose

Use these prompts to demonstrate that Intelligence is no longer just an answer box. Claude supplies the advisory judgment and AbarVa renders a governed executive exhibit: sequencing, value/readiness, gate-to-value, or proof boundary.

## Airline / SkyHarbor CTO

1. Sequencing map:
   `For SkyHarbor, where should we fund AI next across IROPS, predictive maintenance, crew recovery, loyalty, and customer disruption recovery? Give me the executive answer and use the right canvas for the most useful adjacent views.`

2. Value/readiness matrix:
   `For SkyHarbor, which AI bets are high value but not ready yet across IROPS, crew recovery, predictive maintenance, loyalty, and customer disruption recovery? Give me the tradeoff and use the right canvas.`

3. Gate-to-value roadmap:
   `For SkyHarbor, what has to happen first before we scale IROPS AI or customer disruption recovery? Give me the executive roadmap and use the right canvas.`

4. Proof boundary:
   `For SkyHarbor, what evidence is strong enough for the board and what still needs CDAO or operations signoff before AI capital is released?`

## Industrial / Morgan Street CIO

1. Sequencing map:
   `For the Industrial Demo, how should the CIO prioritize AI and automation across HR, finance, treasury, legal, and shared services? Give me a point of view and use the right canvas.`

2. Value/readiness matrix:
   `For the Industrial Demo, which shared-services AI bets are high value but not ready yet across HR, finance, treasury, legal, and shared services? Use the right canvas.`

3. Gate-to-value roadmap:
   `For the Industrial Demo, what has to happen first before the CIO scales AI across Finance, Treasury, HR, Legal, and shared services? Use the right canvas.`

4. Proof boundary:
   `For the Industrial Demo, which claims can we make from loaded evidence, which are planning assumptions, and what does the CIO need to validate next?`

## Pass Criteria

- The answer starts with a direct executive point of view.
- The right canvas does not duplicate the left answer.
- The right canvas shows a native exhibit when the question is strategic.
- No raw JSON, `abarva-canvas`, `canvasType`, `<<<TAB:`, or `grounding:` text is visible.
- Industry or pattern context is labeled as context, not tenant proof.
