# CFO-Led Talking Track

Use when the buyer wants value, ROI, and funding discipline first.

## Opening

"The CFO risk in AI productivity is ghost value. People report saved hours, but those hours do not become margin, capacity, speed, or revenue unless there is a named reallocation queue. AbarVa makes value state explicit: projected, tracked, verified."

## What To Emphasize

- Productivity is not value: value requires reallocation.
- Verified-value tri-state: projected, tracked, verified, with attestation required for verified.
- License economics: Copilot/Cursor seats, activated users, DAU/MAU, cost per realized outcome.
- Business case gate: CFO signs only after baseline, segmentation, reallocation queue, sensitivity analysis, and kill criteria.
- Kill criteria: adoption floor, DORA delta floor, license ceiling, attrition trigger, productivity reversal.
- Source connection: AMS Optimization can fund or de-risk the productivity Move through renegotiation, consolidation, and outcome-based clauses.

## Close

"For the CFO, AbarVa prevents AI productivity from becoming a soft benefits story. It forces the executive team to say what value is expected, where it will show up, and when it has been verified."
