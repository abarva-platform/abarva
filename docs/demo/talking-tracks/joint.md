# Joint Sponsor Talking Track

Use when CTO, CFO, CIO, Procurement, or business sponsors are together.

## Opening

"AbarVa is useful here because IT productivity is not one sponsor's problem. The CTO owns engineering flow, the CFO owns verified value, the CIO owns governance, Procurement owns sourcing leverage, and business sponsors own reallocation. The product keeps those views connected."

## Sponsor Lens

| Sponsor | What they should hear |
|---|---|
| CTO | Portfolio-segmented productivity, not a blanket AI tool rollout. |
| CFO | Projected, tracked, verified value; no ghost productivity. |
| CIO / CISO | AI Egress Control Plane, policy, audit, and data-class boundaries. |
| Procurement | AMS Optimization is a separate Source workflow with vendor diagnostics. |
| Business sponsor | Saved hours convert only through named reallocation and process changes. |

## Close

"The point of the demo is the handoff chain: Intelligence forms the view, Sentinel shapes Moves, Tower shows dependencies and value state, Source handles vendor optimization, and audit records what happened. That is why this can support a real executive decision."
