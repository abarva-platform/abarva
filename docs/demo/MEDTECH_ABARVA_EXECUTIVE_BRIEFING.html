Enterprise AI decision platform

AbarVa turns enterprise context into evidence-backed decisions.

It connects scattered systems, documents, strategy, finance, vendors, quality evidence, org ownership, and transformation work into one trusted context layer that agents can reason over and leaders can act on.

In plain English

AbarVa is the operating layer between enterprise data and executive action: upload or sync the context, ask hard questions, shape Strategic Moves, create Source events, and track dollar outcomes with evidence. It is pattern-grounded, tenant-overlaid, and agent-collaborative by design.

Why this matters

Transformation decisions are increasingly cross-functional, but the evidence is trapped in silos.

ERP modernization affects manufacturing, quality, finance, commercial operations, product data, sourcing, cybersecurity, and regulatory posture. AI productivity affects delivery operating model, vendor contracts, engineering governance, and risk. Procurement decisions depend on app retirement plans and business outcome evidence. The platform exists to connect those threads before leaders make expensive decisions.

Common medtech decision friction

  • SAP, Oracle, JDE, Infor, and plant systems coexist for years.
  • Quality and regulatory evidence lives outside the IT decision flow.
  • AMS and SI contracts outlive the systems they support.
  • Initiatives are funded before evidence strength is clear.
  • AI adoption creates pressure faster than governance and context mature.
  • Board updates compress messy evidence into oversimplified status slides.

Workflow

The product loop from data to decision

Bring the messy truth

Files and system exports do not need to be perfect. The platform classifies and stages what it can.

PARSE

Extract structured facts

Contracts, charters, KPI sheets, org files, CMDB exports, and ERP extracts become normalized records.

APPROVE

Keep humans accountable

Stewards approve or correct extracted facts before they become trusted context.

REASON

Ask with context

Agents answer with evidence, confidence, dissent, and explicit not-enough-data behavior.

ACT

Create decision artifacts

Moves, Source events, Dossiers, Board Packs, and Tower metrics turn insight into action.

Journey map

Walk into the product as a CIO or VP with a real decision to make.

The strongest first conversation is not a generic AI demo. It is a practical case study: load enough context, ask the hard executive question, turn the answer into a Move, create a Source path where commercial leverage exists, and then watch Tower show whether the work is becoming measurable value.

Example seat

CIO / VP Technology

"We are carrying multiple ERP, legacy-app, cloud, data, and vendor modernization efforts. Where do we focus first, what should wait, and what procurement leverage do we have?"

  • Context loaded: systems, ERP, CMDB, contracts, spend, org, KPIs, risks, strategy.
  • Decision target: sequence work, avoid unsafe funding, expose vendor leverage.
  • Output: Move, Source event, Dossier, Tower value readout, and evidence gaps.

Why it compounds

The more context loaded, the better the decision layer becomes.

A first upload answers the first questions. A second upload closes gaps. A sourcing event adds commercial memory. A Strategic Move adds execution memory. A value outcome teaches what actually worked. Over time, the platform becomes the enterprise memory for why decisions were made and what evidence supported them.

Every upload adds evidence.

A contract quote, KPI row, QMS record, org owner, or ERP extract becomes a future citation, not just a file in a folder.

Every decision adds memory.

The system remembers what was recommended, what was challenged, what was approved, and what would change the view.

Every outcome improves value proof.

Savings, avoided risk, delivery performance, and adoption signals turn from claims into trackable states.

Pilot partnership

Early customers help shape the product while getting practical enhancements.

A pilot customer is not just a test account. It is a chance to make the product sharper against real enterprise operating conditions: messy data, real approval paths, actual CXO questions, and workflow patterns that matter in medtech. As appropriate, feedback from the pilot can become targeted product enhancements, new templates, better upload flows, stronger evidence views, and sharper workflow logic.

How the partnership works

  • Selected users get demo-environment access while real context loading is underway.
  • Weekly feedback identifies friction in modules, language, workflows, and artifacts.
  • High-value gaps become candidate enhancements, not one-off custom work by default.
  • New templates and context dimensions can be added when they improve the product for the broader medtech pattern.
  • The customer gets early value and a voice in shaping a product category still being formed.
