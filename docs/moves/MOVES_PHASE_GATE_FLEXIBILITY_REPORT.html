Moves Phase Gate Flexibility

Advance a Move through its phases without faking completeness — Maestro/Admin can approve with gaps, the gaps stay visible, and every decision is recorded.

1. Principle

Real engagements do not stall because one soft criterion is unmet — a lead makes a judgment call, advances, and carries the gap forward as a tracked obligation. AbarVa models exactly this: hard gate criteria block advancement (no fabrication, no bypass); soft criteria can be carried forward by an authorized approver. The gap does not disappear when the gate is crossed — it is written into a durable decision record and surfaced on downstream artifacts.

2. The three dispositions

DispositionConditionOutcome
Clean advance All gate criteria (hard + soft) satisfied. Advance proceeds. Decision record written, status approved.
Override with gaps Hard criteria satisfied; one or more soft criteria unmet, or explicit bypassGate. Authorized approver advances. Carried-forward gaps recorded; record status review_required.
Blocked Any hard criterion unmet. Advance refused (HTTP 409). No override path. The gap must be closed first.

3. Governance the override does NOT weaken

  • Hard-fail checks always block — evaluateGate runs first; a single hard fail returns gate_blocked.
  • A human rationale (≥ 20 chars) is required for every advance.
  • Gate approval requires the canApproveGates capability; under GATE_APPROVAL_STRICT_MODE it requires an admin/maestro role.
  • Approvals are bound to the specific transition (from/to phase) and approval type — no replay of a stale or unrelated approval.
  • The AI/operator never self-approves (rule R8).

4. The Phase Gate Decision Record (durable + visible)

On every successful advance, a Phase Gate Decision Record is written to the Artifact Vault (family approval_artifact) and appears in the Move's File Cabinet. It captures:

  • the transition (phase n → n+1) and disposition;
  • the approver (name + role) and the human rationale;
  • the carried-forward gaps — each unmet soft criterion with the reason it remains open;
  • stated assumptions and acknowledged missing inputs;
  • the bound approval ID, when an approval was consumed.

5. Gaps carry forward, not away

Because the carried-forward gaps live in the decision record (and in the registry metadata), they remain legible after the gate is crossed. Downstream deliverables that rest on an unmet criterion keep a preliminary / gap status rather than presenting as complete — AbarVa does not fake completeness, and it does not let a judgment-call advance erase the obligation that made it a judgment call.

6. Hard rules honored

  • Maestro/Admin can approve with gaps. enforced
  • Gaps remain visible. enforced
  • Downstream artifacts inherit preliminary/gap status. enforced
  • Do not fake completeness. enforced
  • AI/operator never self-approves. enforced
