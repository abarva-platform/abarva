# Compensation Framework

## Purpose

This framework gives AbarVa a consistent starting point before the first hire.
It is designed for a founder-led, pre-seed or early pilot-stage company where
cash is scarce, equity is meaningful, and role timing should follow revenue and
pilot-conversion triggers.

This is an operating framework, not legal, tax, or compensation advice. Final
offers require founder approval and should be reviewed with counsel or a
qualified compensation advisor when equity grants are issued.

## Principles

1. Hire only against a defined business trigger.
2. Keep cash conservative until recurring revenue is real.
3. Use equity to reward risk, scope, seniority, and timing.
4. Use the same vesting structure by default: four-year vest, one-year cliff.
5. Document every exception before the offer is sent.
6. Do not create title inflation to compensate for low cash.
7. Keep contractor, advisor, employee, and founder economics separate.

## Standard Vesting

| Grant type | Default vesting | Cliff | Acceleration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee option grant | Four years monthly vesting | One year | None by default |
| Early senior hire grant | Four years monthly vesting | One year | Founder-approved exception only |
| Advisor grant | Two years monthly vesting | Three months or none, by agreement | None by default |
| Contractor equity | Avoid by default | N/A | N/A |

Single-trigger acceleration should not be standard. Any double-trigger
acceleration or advisor-specific acceleration must be explicitly approved.

## Early Role Bands

These bands are planning ranges for initial offers. They should be calibrated
against current market data before use.

| Role | Trigger to hire | Cash planning range | Equity planning range | Notes |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Senior/staff engineer | First paid pilot signed or committed conversion path | $150k to $210k | 0.50% to 1.50% | Prioritize full-stack product velocity, data/security judgment, and ownership |
| Founding product/operator | Founder capacity bottleneck across customer delivery and roadmap | $130k to $190k | 0.30% to 1.00% | May be fractional first; avoid vague "chief of staff" scope |
| Customer success / implementation lead | Pilot client requires repeatable onboarding and weekly cadence | $110k to $160k | 0.15% to 0.50% | Hire after delivery motion is proven, not before |
| Sales engineer | Two or more active enterprise opportunities need technical support | $130k to $180k | 0.20% to 0.75% | Should support technical validation, not become generic sales capacity |
| Account executive | Repeatable pipeline and clear ICP conversion evidence | $110k to $160k base plus commission | 0.10% to 0.40% | Do not hire before founder-led sales motion is understood |
| Fractional finance/ops | Vendor spend, invoicing, payroll, and board reporting exceed founder bandwidth | $2k to $8k per month | None by default | Use contractors before full-time hire |

Cash and equity are tradeable only within founder-approved boundaries. Lower
cash does not automatically justify outsized equity.

## Offer Approval Checklist

Before any offer is sent:

- Business trigger is documented.
- Role scorecard is written.
- Hiring manager and decision owner are named.
- Cash band and equity band are selected from this framework.
- Any exception is documented with rationale.
- Equity pool impact is calculated.
- Employment classification is confirmed: employee, contractor, advisor, or
  vendor.
- Offer letter, proprietary information agreement, and IP assignment path are
  ready.
- Start date does not create a cash runway or delivery-risk surprise.

## Exception Policy

Any of these require explicit founder approval:

- Cash above the planning range.
- Equity above the planning range.
- Vesting shorter than four years for employees.
- No one-year cliff for employees.
- Acceleration rights.
- Guaranteed bonus, severance, or commission structure.
- Title above the actual operating scope.
- Contractor equity.

## Advisor Equity

Advisor grants should be narrow and tied to specific outcomes.

| Advisor type | Typical scope | Planning range |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Light advisor | Monthly call, occasional intros | 0.05% to 0.15% |
| Active advisor | Weekly help, buyer intros, material GTM/security support | 0.15% to 0.35% |
| Strategic advisor | Named role, repeated enterprise access, board-visible support | 0.25% to 0.75% |

Advisor agreements should include confidentiality, IP assignment for created
work, term, vesting, and termination language.

## Compensation Review Cadence

| Cadence | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Before first offer | Confirm bands, role trigger, vesting, and legal documents |
| After first paid pilot | Reconfirm hiring sequence and cash runway impact |
| Quarterly | Review compensation bands, equity pool impact, and contractor spend |
| Before seed financing | Recalibrate against current market benchmarks and option-pool plan |

## Do Not Pre-Hire

These roles should stay deferred until their trigger is real:

- Account executive before founder-led sales has repeatable objection handling.
- Full-time CSM before onboarding and support load exceeds founder/operator
  capacity.
- Dedicated recruiter before there are multiple active reqs.
- Chief-level hires before scope and authority are real.
- Full-time finance before bookkeeping, invoicing, and investor reporting
  exceed fractional support.

## Records To Keep

For each offer:

- Role scorecard.
- Approved compensation band.
- Approved equity grant.
- Exception memo, if any.
- Signed offer letter.
- Signed proprietary information and invention assignment agreement.
- Board or founder approval evidence.
- Start date and manager.
