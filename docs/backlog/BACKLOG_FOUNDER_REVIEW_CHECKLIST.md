# Founder Review Checklist

Use this after every major wave.

## Product story

- Does the page/workflow answer a real executive/operator question?
- Is the demo story coherent across surfaces?
- Are next actions obvious?

## Design

- Does it feel premium, calm, off-white, table-forward?
- Does it use the current name-only AbarVa wordmark?
- Is the agent guidance contextual and not generic?
- Is the page too cluttered?

## Data and trust

- What is seeded vs real?
- What evidence supports claims?
- Are missing data and caveats visible?
- Are we avoiding fake production/vendor claims?

## Readiness

- Did production-readiness.json change honestly?
- What remains blocked for pilot?
- What needs live route/persona/security validation?
