## P1 - Demo-Critical UX and Workflow Clarity

| ID       | Problem                                                         | Why It Matters                                                                                  | Fix Path                                                                                                                                                        | Acceptance Evidence                                                                                                   |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NE-P1-01 | Stage pages are visually busy and over-explained.               | CXO users cannot quickly see what to do next.                                                   | Apply Moves-style discipline: compact masthead, left journey tree, one active step, one primary action, no large headers, no competing cards.                   | At 1440px desktop, active stage intent, evidence table, current blocker, and next action are visible without hunting. |
| NE-P1-02 | Required vs optional uploads are not explicit enough.           | Users do not know what to load, whether alternatives are acceptable, or what blocks approval.   | Replace task-card pile with a table: requirement, required/optional, source system, owner, template, accepted formats, upload, parse status, done, next action. | Scope/RFP/Responses/Pricing show row-level evidence contracts and an unmistakable required/optional distinction.      |
| NE-P1-03 | After evidence completion, forward progress is too subtle.      | A user can reach 7/7 and still not know how to open the approval gate.                          | Promote `Open approval gate` as the only primary action once required evidence is complete; disabled with reason before that.                                   | On a completed stage, the primary action navigates to approval without requiring guesswork.                           |
| NE-P1-04 | Upload/readiness feedback can require reload.                   | Users cannot tell whether the upload counted.                                                   | Refresh evidence state in the same session after upload; show loaded, parsed, usable, or issue.                                                                 | Upload file; same-page UI updates with parse/readiness state and stage progress.                                      |
| NE-P1-05 | Files, Intelligence, Guidebook, and Approval feel disconnected. | The product should feel like an expert operating system, not separate tabs.                     | Standardize each stage around four panes: Evidence, Files, Intelligence, Guidebook; each must say what it uses, what it produced, and what unlocks next.        | RFP, Responses, Pricing, Executive Decision show coherent cross-pane state.                                           |
| NE-P1-06 | Artifact readiness and stage readiness are conflated.           | Users cannot tell if the current stage is blocked by evidence, artifacts, or old review states. | Separate stage gate readiness from artifact governance; move cross-stage artifact hygiene to a secondary drawer.                                                | Executive Decision and Selection show one current blocker in under 10 seconds.                                        |
