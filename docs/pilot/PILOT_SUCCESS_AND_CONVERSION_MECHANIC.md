# Pilot Success Criteria and Auto-Conversion Mechanic

Status: SOW source material
Owner: founder
Backlog task: T022

## Purpose

The pilot should not end with a vague "great demo" conversation. It should end
with a written decision: convert, expand, extend, or stop. This document
defines the success criteria and conversion mechanics that should be carried
into the pilot SOW.

## Pilot Success Criteria

| Dimension | Success target | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive activation | Named executive sponsor and funded personas use the product on a recurring cadence. | Login/session summary and weekly digest. |
| Workflow value | At least one strategic Move, Source event, or Tower decision reaches a human decision gate. | Decision packet, approval record, artifact/export. |
| Data readiness | Approved pilot data is loaded or blockers are documented with owners. | Data-load ledger, template status, quarantine/clarification notes. |
| Agent quality | Weekly answer samples are grounded, useful, and safe under the agreed rubric. | Quality sample log, citations, gaps, reviewer notes. |
| Security readiness | Required security, SSO, access, release, and data-use evidence exists. | Security packet, SSO test evidence, release records. |
| Business outcome | Customer can name decisions improved, risks reduced, or work accelerated. | 90-day outcome packet and sponsor quote. |
| Commercial path | Year-1 scope, modules, price, support, and timing are known by day 75. | Draft production SOW/order form. |

## Gate Reviews

| Timing | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 30 | Continue, unblock, or reset scope. |
| Day 60 | Confirm value path and production readiness gaps. |
| Day 75 | Present production SOW and conversion recommendation. |
| Day 90 | Convert, expand, extend, or stop. |

## Auto-Conversion Mechanic

Use this as SOW source language:

> If the pilot success criteria are substantially met by day 75, AbarVa and
> Client will enter good-faith production conversion review. The parties will
> use the pilot outcome packet, success metrics, security evidence, and
> production SOW draft to determine whether to convert to a year-1 production
> subscription. Conversion is not automatic without a signed production order
> form, but the commercial path, scope, and pricing should be prepared before
> the pilot end date.

This is intentionally not a silent auto-renewal. It is an auto-conversion
process: evidence-triggered review, pre-drafted order form, and no month-6
dead air.

## Conversion Outcomes

| Outcome | Use when | Commercial action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Convert | Success criteria met and buyer wants year-1 operation. | Execute production SOW/order form. |
| Expand | One module lands strongly and buyer wants broader scope. | Convert plus add module/client-scope expansion. |
| Extend | Value is visible but blocker remains: data, security, workflow, or buyer timing. | 30-90 day paid extension with named blocker closure. |
| Stop | Value does not land or buyer cannot sponsor production. | Close cleanly, return/delete data per policy, preserve lessons. |

## Anti-Drift Rules

- Do not wait until day 90 to discuss production.
- Do not treat usage alone as success; the customer must see business value.
- Do not convert without security/data readiness.
- Do not bury missing data or weak evidence.
- Do not let AI recommendations substitute for human buyer decisions.

## Evidence Packet

The day-75 conversion packet should include:

- pilot scope and timeline,
- usage summary,
- workflow value proof,
- data readiness state,
- agent-quality sample,
- security/release evidence,
- open risks and blockers,
- recommended production scope,
- pricing and add-ons,
- support and managed-services assumptions,
- production rollout plan.
