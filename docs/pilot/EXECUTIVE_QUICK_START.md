# AbarVa — Executive Quick Start

**Time to read:** 3 minutes  
**Audience:** CXO, VP, executive sponsor at an enterprise pilot client

---

## What AbarVa Does for You

AbarVa gives you a real-time command center for your AI programme portfolio.

In 30 seconds, you can see:

- Which AI programmes are on track and which are stalled
- What is blocking the next phase gate
- Whether your vendor selection is aligned to your programme timeline
- The top commercial risk this week

You do not need to attend status meetings to find this out.

---

## Log In

1. Go to `https://nexus-vert-kappa.vercel.app`
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your inbox

You will land on your **command center** — the AI Activity home page.

---

## Three Pages to Bookmark

### 1. Home — `/home`

Your executive entry point. Shows your AI activity queue, recent alerts, and top recommended actions across your portfolio. Check this before your Monday morning review.

### 2. Tower — `/tenant/<your-org>/tower`

The executive portfolio dashboard. Shows:

- Total AI programme portfolio health
- Cost and adoption signals (where are you getting value vs. where is spend at risk?)
- Governance exposure — are your AI programmes meeting your governance obligations?

This is the Atlas view — your CFO and CISO will find this useful.

### 3. Programme Detail — `/tenant/<your-org>/programs/<programme>`

Click any programme card to see its full status:

- Where it is in the delivery lifecycle (6 phases from Discovery to Deploy)
- What evidence is needed before the next phase gate advances
- The top risk this week
- Whether the linked commercial sourcing event is on track

---

## What You Will See

The platform currently shows your organisation's pilot data (if provided to AbarVa) or a reference demonstration environment.

> All data is either your pilot-provided information or representative demonstration data. No real external vendor data is included without your approval.

---

## Questions to Ask Your AbarVa Advisor

1. "Which programme is at the highest risk of slipping this quarter?"
2. "Is the vendor selection timeline aligned to our programme's phase gate?"
3. "What is the one action my team needs to take this week?"

AbarVa surfaces the data to answer these. Your advisor can walk you through the evidence behind each signal.
