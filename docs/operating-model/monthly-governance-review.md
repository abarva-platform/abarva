# Monthly Governance Review

Status: scaffold-ready

## Agenda

1. Review Product Dev, Product Preview, and Product Prod status.
2. Review Client Preprod and Client Prod environments separately.
3. Confirm no PHI and no unapproved PII posture.
4. Review budget actuals, forecasts, and alerts.
5. Review RBAC exports and broad-access exceptions.
6. Review Azure Policy compliance and drift.
7. Review Key Vault access and secret rotation.
8. Review diagnostic settings, logs, incidents, and problems.
9. Review context-bundle proof, agent-ready promotions, and blocked rows.
10. Review next approvals required.

## Outputs

Meeting notes, decision log, evidence links, open risks, owner assignments, and due dates. If no Azure mutation approval exists, all follow-up work remains scaffold or validation-only.
