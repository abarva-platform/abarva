# AI Litigation And Regulatory Monitoring

Status: pilot operating baseline
Owner: AbarVa platform owner
Last updated: 2026-06-01
Backlog row: T230

## Purpose

This document defines a lightweight quarterly process for monitoring legal,
regulatory, and enforcement developments that could change AbarVa's AI
decision-support risk posture.

It is not legal advice. Counsel should review material changes before AbarVa
updates MSA/SOW terms, public AI trust pages, prohibited-use rules, or product
controls.

## Monitoring Cadence

| Cadence | Activity | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly lightweight scan | Check for obvious AI-output liability, hallucination, procurement, privacy, security, employment, healthcare, credit, insurance, and model-risk developments | Platform owner |
| Quarterly formal review | Produce a short memo with cases, regulatory updates, control implications, and recommended policy changes | Platform owner with counsel review when needed |
| Immediate escalation | Escalate within 2 business days when a development affects advisor-vs-decider positioning, high-risk-use boundaries, warranty/indemnity, or client notice obligations | Platform owner |

## Sources To Review

Use a small, repeatable source set:

- Federal and state court developments involving AI-generated outputs,
  hallucinated citations, professional reliance, procurement, employment,
  healthcare, credit, insurance, or consumer decisions.
- FTC, EEOC, CFPB, HHS/OCR, SEC, DOJ, NIST, and state privacy/AI guidance.
- EU AI Act and major international AI governance updates when relevant to
  enterprise buyers.
- Insurance carrier guidance for E&O, cyber, technology errors, and AI-specific
  endorsements.
- Counsel or outside-advisor memos.
- Client security/procurement questionnaire themes.

Do not rely only on news summaries for policy changes. Link back to the primary
case, agency guidance, regulation, or counsel memo when available.

## Review Questions

Each quarterly memo should answer:

1. Does this change AbarVa's advisor-not-decider positioning?
2. Does this change prohibited or high-risk use language?
3. Does this require different public disclosure on `/responsible-ai`,
   `/model-card`, or `/known-limitations`?
4. Does this change SOW/MSA language, indemnity, warranty disclaimer, validation
   duty, or liability cap posture?
5. Does this require a new product control, test, disclaimer, approval gate, or
   evidence packet field?
6. Does this affect insurance coverage questions or renewal strategy?
7. Does this require client notice or pilot operating guidance?

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to counsel/founder review when a development involves:

- AI output relied on in a consequential business or regulated decision.
- Hallucinated citation, fabricated source, or false factual assertion causing
  professional or commercial harm.
- Vendor liability for AI recommendations, autonomous action, or unsafe
  delegation.
- Employment, healthcare, credit, insurance, legal, safety, or individual-rights
  AI use.
- Contract language on AI warranty, indemnity, validation duty, or model risk.
- Insurance exclusion or endorsement language that could affect AbarVa.

## Evidence To Retain

- Source links or stored PDFs.
- Quarterly memo.
- Counsel comments or approval when obtained.
- Updated docs, policies, or release records.
- Product backlog rows created from the review.
- Decision not to act, with rationale.

## Out Of Scope

This process does not replace counsel, insurance broker review, or client legal
negotiation. It creates a repeatable monitoring loop so AbarVa can show ongoing
care as the AI liability landscape changes.
