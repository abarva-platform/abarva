# AbarVa Trade Secret Policy

Status: active draft
Owner: founder
Backlog task: T070

## Policy Statement

AbarVa protects its non-public product, corpus, agent, operating, pricing, and
customer-specific materials as confidential information and, where applicable,
trade secrets. Access is limited to people with a business need, and disclosure
requires written approval or an approved contract.

## Covered Materials

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Product architecture | AgentContextBroker boundaries, data-plane patterns, release gates, governed data-load workflows. |
| Corpus and patterns | Industry patterns, playbooks, value levers, templates, scoring rubrics, pattern applicability logic. |
| Agent behavior | Prompts, agent doctrine, guardrails, answer-quality rubrics, evaluation corpora, failure-mode handling. |
| Customer overlays | Client-specific context, templates, strategy maps, evidence packs, pilot artifacts, source events. |
| Commercial materials | Pricing models, discount rules, negotiation strategy, target-account memos, SOW mechanics. |
| Security and operations | Non-public incident details, access paths, secrets handling, test results, security gaps. |

## Handling Rules

1. Store source materials in approved company-controlled locations.
2. Do not paste sensitive customer, corpus, pricing, or security material into
   uncontrolled public tools.
3. Mark externally shared drafts as confidential when appropriate.
4. Share customer-specific materials only with that customer or approved
   service providers under confidentiality terms.
5. Keep public demos, public docs, and marketing assets free of confidential
   customer facts.
6. Use release records or review notes for approved disclosure decisions.
7. Remove access promptly when a contractor, advisor, or employee no longer
   needs it.

## Permitted Sharing

| Recipient | Allowed material |
| --- | --- |
| Prospective customer | Approved one-pagers, security packet, public trust pages, and mutually approved pilot artifacts. |
| Counsel | Any material needed for legal review under attorney-client or confidentiality protections. |
| Contractor/advisor | Minimum necessary work materials under NDA/IP assignment. |
| Investor | Approved diligence packet; avoid customer-confidential data unless explicitly approved. |
| Public | Only approved marketing, public docs, public demos, and generalized non-client-specific narratives. |

## Prohibited Sharing

- Client-private data with another client.
- Non-public corpus or prompt details in public decks.
- Raw security gaps without buyer-safe context and owner approval.
- Pricing model internals outside a controlled negotiation context.
- Credentials, keys, tokens, secrets, or private URLs.
- Customer names or facts not approved for reference.

## Departure and Contractor Controls

Every contractor, advisor, and employee should have:

- signed confidentiality obligations,
- IP assignment terms appropriate to their work,
- least-privilege access,
- offboarding access removal,
- return/deletion confirmation for confidential materials.

## Enforcement

Suspected disclosure issues should be escalated to the founder immediately.
Containment should preserve evidence, identify affected materials, remove
unnecessary access, notify counsel when needed, and record corrective actions.
