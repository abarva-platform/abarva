# Expanded Worldview Scope

This worldview corpus is broader than public thought leadership. It is intended to become strategic grounding content for AbarVa's intelligence layer.

## Expanded Use Cases

1. Intelligence J0 cold-open grounding: failure-mode cards can cite worldview chunks when explaining why the failure mode matters structurally.
2. Sentinel strategic answers: answers about AI, ERP, consulting, enterprise software, and future of work should retrieve worldview chunks before model synthesis.
3. Nexus P0/P1 program framing: early-stage program decisions should pull worldview chunks as strategic context before vendor selection or charter drafting.
4. Anthology pitch assembly: founder/investor surfaces can assemble pitch material from canonical chunks rather than ad hoc copy.
5. External thought leadership: long-form markdown is an assembled artifact, not the canonical source.
6. Partner strategy: W4/W5 chunks should support direct conversations with consulting partners without pretending the displacement risk is not real.

## Expanded Evidence Standard

- Primary sources preferred: official filings, annual reports, vendor product announcements, regulator guidance, foundation-model lab publications.
- Secondary sources allowed only when they add interpretation or surface otherwise hard-to-find market context.
- Every forward-looking claim must include confidence and falsification conditions.
- Every major named company claim should be auditable by URL.
- No invented analyst quotes, private financials, benchmark scores, or customer outcomes.

## Retrieval Standard

Each chunk must stand alone. A single retrieved chunk should tell the agent:

- what claim AbarVa is making,
- what evidence supports it,
- what makes the point distinctly AbarVa,
- what the user should do with it,
- whether the claim is fact, interpretation, or forecast.

## Product Standard

The corpus should make the intelligence layer shine by showing the difference between generic LLM fluency and grounded strategic reasoning. The app should be able to display the context bundle: worldview chunk, tenant fact, graph path, and citations used to produce an answer.
