## Homepage narrative · Own it / Build it / Keep it

The homepage needs a story that carries the wow. Current homepage is serviceable. Demo-ready homepage needs the journey map.

### The three-beat structure

**YOU OWN IT · the enterprise owns the transformation outcome**

Enterprise transformation is your accountability. Not consultants, not vendors. Boards hold you accountable. Markets judge you. Customers feel the result. AbarVa doesn't take this accountability from you — that would be offensive, and we couldn't if we tried.

What AbarVa does: makes your accountability *executable*. The strategic intent stays yours. The decisions stay yours. The programs stay yours. We provide the operating infrastructure that makes the work work.

**YOU BUILD IT · AI-native transformation infrastructure, deployed in your cloud, tuned to your context**

AbarVa deploys into your cloud. Your identity provider, your KMS, your data residency. Your transformation intelligence builds on your real data — telemetry, financial reality, executive voice, strategic priorities.

The Intelligence Suite runs against your context. Situation Intelligence knows your operations. Strategy Intelligence knows your priorities. Pattern Intelligence matches your current state against the Transformation Genome. Every insight is grounded in your ground truth. Every recommendation is specific to your executives.

**YOU KEEP IT · your intelligence compounds inside your tenant and stays yours**

The Transformation Genome inside your tenant grows with every program, every decision, every outcome. Patterns detected, interventions run, lessons learned — these accumulate as your institutional intelligence. Not ours. Yours.

Your data never leaves your cloud. Your patterns anonymize outward only as published artifacts if you opt in — your raw intelligence is yours alone. Your memory compounds. Your value stays captured. Year over year, AbarVa becomes a higher-leverage asset because your intelligence deepens.

### Why this works as the homepage spine

- **Solves the "are you another consulting firm?" problem** — Own it emphatically reframes accountability
- **Solves the "will you lock us in?" problem** — Keep it emphasizes data sovereignty and compounding value
- **Solves the "is this just AI theater?" problem** — Build it grounds in customer cloud, customer context, customer data
- **Gives Prat a story he can carry back to his board** — three beats, memorable structure, no jargon
- **Differentiates from Harvey AI** — Harvey is SaaS-per-seat in their cloud; AbarVa is customer-cloud with compounding customer-owned intelligence. Same category structure, harder moat.
