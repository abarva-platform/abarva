## Page 1 - In-Scope Services

In-scope services cover cross-process governance, labor transition, knowledge transfer, and exit readiness; each service has a retained owner, supplier owner, system of record, volume baseline, and handoff gate.

## Page 2 - Out-of-Scope Work

Clinical judgement, policy exception approval, final finance signoff, employment decisions, and vendor award decisions remain retained Meridian responsibilities.

## Page 3 - Delivery Locations

Delivery may occur from Denver retained hub, transition war room, supplier delivery network only when role, data class, and access-control evidence are approved.

## Page 4 - Governance Cadence

Weekly operations review and monthly commercial review must reconcile scope, backlog, SLA, invoice, staffing, transition issue, and control-exception data.
