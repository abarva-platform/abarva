# Home Claude Architecture Review Decision

Status: changes requested. Do not merge or deploy generated Claude SVGs as production Home assets.

The retained Claude outputs prove generation, structural SVG validation, raw-output fidelity, and
audit retention. They do not prove factual architecture support, numeric reconciliation, authority
for gates, current-versus-target semantics, or human publication approval.

Runtime publication requires `HomeDiagramSemanticSpecV2`, semantic validation, deterministic SVG
compilation, and human approval.
