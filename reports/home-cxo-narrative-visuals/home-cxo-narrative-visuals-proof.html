Meridian Health is an integrated delivery network and health plan headquartered in Sacramento, California, operating across Northern California, Central California, and Nevada service areas. The enterprise runs real operating breadth across clinical care, health-plan operations, and enterprise analytics, with represented functions spanning member service, quality, and finance. Its clinical estate rests on Epic's clinical and analytics modules, while claims, eligibility, member-service CRM, and a knowledge base sit alongside fragmented on-prem reporting tools that thousands of analysts still depend on. Current analytics are on-premise and fragmented, and much analytics capacity is consumed by maintenance and ad hoc reporting rather than net-new delivery.

The strategic implication is clear: the enterprise cannot scale AI safely until a governed data spine exists beneath these fragmented sources. No patient/member identity spine, certified medallion architecture, or formal data governance operating model is in place, and the AWS plus Databricks lakehouse remains target-state, not current production. The same identity, claims, and eligibility data that would ground a member-service Agent Assist example also grounds payment integrity, cost transparency, and quality analytics — one foundation serves many use cases. The CDAO, with Finance and clinical leadership, should validate identity-spine ownership, medallion certification, and transcript governance as the first deliverable of a data governance workshop, before any use case advances to architecture. This is planning-grade synthetic context for demonstration, not client production evidence.
