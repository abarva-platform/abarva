Routing — What goes where
/maestro
Cross-client dashboard. Greeting, Today's Priority, My Clients, Pending Approvals, My Engagements table. NO sidebar.
/maestro/[clientId]
Client workspace. LEFT SIDEBAR + main content. Signal card, engagements table, data readiness row. All operational work.

Admin only. Users, sensitive data approvals, audit logs, security. LEFT SIDEBAR with admin-only sections. Maestro cannot access.
