# Home Dossier Proof Criteria

## Required Standard

Do not pass or fail a Home/aVa dossier answer from `factsBound` alone.

The proof verdict must use the shared usable-dossier-evidence standard:

- facts
- tables
- charts
- graphs
- citations
- source coverage
- sections
- rollups
- relationship paths
- metrics
- sourced gaps

## Pass Criteria

A proof case passes when:

1. `usableEvidence = true`
2. the answer is tenant-safe
3. raw IDs/internal labels are absent from user-facing prose
4. no false no-data fallback is rendered
5. unsupported questions disclose specific gaps or hand off to the right module

`factsBound = 0` is acceptable when another channel is populated.
