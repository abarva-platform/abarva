Plain-English Verdict

What feature was added?

Sentinel can now answer Apex Retail questions using a dedicated retail industry knowledge layer: store operations, omnichannel, merchandising, pricing, vendors, workforce, AI, risk, and retail format economics.

What changed operationally?

The system no longer depends only on generic patterns for Apex Retail. It retrieves retail-v1 chunks from the live tenant context store, cites them in Ask responses, and blocks other tenants from seeing that overlay.

Who receives it?

Apex Retail receives the retail overlay. Non-retail tenants remain on their own context and overlays. The isolation rule is universal across tenants.

What remains open?

The expert gauntlet passed the required 4 of 5 tests. The one known depth gap is peer benchmarking specificity: the answer used peer framing, basis-point ranges, and timeframes, but did not include a dollar range.
