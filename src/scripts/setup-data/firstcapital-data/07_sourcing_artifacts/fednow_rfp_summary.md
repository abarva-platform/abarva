# FedNow RFP Summary · First Capital Financial

First Capital evaluated five paths for FedNow and real-time payment integration: Finzly, Fiserv Now Gateway, Jack Henry PayZur, Q2 PTAP, and an internal FIS HORIZON API build.

The sourcing recommendation is Finzly. The reason is not that Finzly is the biggest vendor; it is the only option that credibly meets the commercial-client deadline while avoiding a full core replacement. Finzly is scored at 91/100, with a $4.2M three-year cost and 16-week implementation path.

The two most important decision facts are:

- $340M in commercial deposits are documented as attrition risk if FedNow is not deployed.
- FIS HORIZON cannot support FedNow natively without a middleware/API layer.

The selection risk is vendor maturity perception. That should be managed through contractual SLA, escrow, GLBA controls, OCC-ready documentation, and reference calls with similar FIS HORIZON banks.

Procurement should not let the process drift into a generic payments-platform RFP. The decision is specifically about protecting commercial deposits and closing regulatory control gaps under a deadline.
