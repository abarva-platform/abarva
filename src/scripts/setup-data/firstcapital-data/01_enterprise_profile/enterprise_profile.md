# Enterprise Profile · First Capital Financial

First Capital Financial is a regional bank headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland with approximately $18B in assets, $1.84B in annual revenue, 4,200 employees, and 84 branches across its Mid-Atlantic footprint.

The bank's current strategic posture is defined by five pressures:

- Cost-to-income ratio is 68% against a board target of 55%.
- Digital adoption is 41% against a peer median of 67%.
- FIS HORIZON is a 22-year-old core banking platform that constrains FedNow, real-time data, and API banking.
- Three OCC MRAs remain open, with real-time payment controls and BSA/AML automation as the most material regulatory gates.
- $340M in commercial deposits are documented as attrition risk if FedNow capability does not land.

First Capital's AI and data opportunity is not a broad innovation portfolio. The highest-confidence agenda is to use AI and data modernization to reduce cost-to-income, close regulatory remediation gaps, protect commercial deposits, and modernize digital banking selectively where the economics are clear.

Primary executive context:

- CEO David Morrison is consensus-led and reputation-focused, but under pressure on digital transformation and regulatory readiness.
- CIO Patricia Huang is the strongest sponsor for FedNow, API banking, and data modernization.
- CFO Michael Torres controls capital and requires credible payback under 18 months before funding AI or modernization spend.
- CRO James Park controls model-risk and examination posture, especially for AML, fraud, underwriting, and FedNow controls.
- CDO Sandra Liu owns digital adoption and customer experience, but is blocked by batch core/data constraints.

The board-level narrative: First Capital can avoid a risky full core replacement in the near term if it funds a pragmatic API/data modernization layer, uses FedNow as the forcing event, and sequences AI into fraud, AML, deposit pricing, and regulatory reporting before customer personalization.
