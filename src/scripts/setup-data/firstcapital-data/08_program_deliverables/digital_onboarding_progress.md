# Digital Onboarding Redesign · Value Tracking Note

The Digital Onboarding Redesign program has improved account-opening abandonment from 64% to 51% over six months, but it is behind the committed 35% target.

Completed work:

- Removed three redundant fields.
- Added progress indicator.
- Reduced Socure timeout rate from 8% to 2.4%.
- Improved mobile abandonment by 12 points.

The remaining blocker is not form design. Accounts opened digitally are still not visible until the next business day because the batch core sync is unresolved. Modeling suggests the abandonment floor is approximately 44-46% unless the real-time account visibility dependency is funded.

Recommended executive message: the team delivered a real 13-point improvement, but the final 16 points require the FedNow/API layer dependency to be funded. Treat this as an integrated digital banking and payments modernization decision, not a UX-only program miss.
