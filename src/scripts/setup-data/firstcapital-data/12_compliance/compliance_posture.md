# Compliance and Regulatory Posture · First Capital Financial

Primary regulator: Office of the Comptroller of the Currency.

Overall position: satisfactory with significant items requiring attention. The Q4 2026 examination window is the forcing event. First Capital has three open MRAs, and two are directly connected to technology modernization.

Open regulatory issues:

- MRA-1: Real-Time Payments Risk Management and Controls. Board-level issue. Requires board-approved real-time payments strategy, FedNow vendor selection, control framework, and incident response plan.
- MRA-2: BSA/AML System Deficiency. NICE Actimize 8.1 is two major versions behind, false positives are materially above benchmark, and automated disposition is below peer standard.
- MRA-3: Transaction Monitoring Gap. Relationship-based activity, beneficial ownership, and network analytics are not adequately integrated into transaction monitoring.
- CFPB Section 1033 readiness is not started and requires customer-authorized data sharing capability.
- Fair-lending model documentation is in progress but not yet exam-ready.

Implication for AbarVa reasoning: AI recommendations in fraud, AML, underwriting, deposit pricing, or customer personalization must name the regulatory path. A technically attractive model is not executable unless the CRO/model-risk gate is explicit.
