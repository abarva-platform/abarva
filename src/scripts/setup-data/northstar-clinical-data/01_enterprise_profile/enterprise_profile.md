# Northstar MedTech Enterprise Profile

Northstar MedTech is a synthetic diversified medtech manufacturer with three operating divisions: surgical instruments, connected-care consumables, and patient-monitoring accessories. The migration rehearsal assumes a $8.6B revenue enterprise, 24,500 employees, 38 operating units, and a global technology estate split across corporate ERP, quality management, regulated manufacturing, commercial operations, and clinical-support data platforms.

The current executive priority is post-separation simplification: consolidate overlapping ERP and quality platforms, reduce duplicated vendor spend, and create a reliable AI investment baseline without inventing missing operational facts.
