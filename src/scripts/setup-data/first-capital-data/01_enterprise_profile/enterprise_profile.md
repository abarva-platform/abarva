## Strategic priorities — FY2026

Five priorities communicated to the Board at the January 2026 strategic planning session:

1. **Digital Banking Transformation.** Consumer digital adoption at 68% vs. competitor median 79%. Target 78% by year-end 2026. Key investment: mobile-first redesign, digital account opening, Zelle/payment modernization, banker digital productivity tools.

2. **Core Banking Modernization.** Legacy core system (FiServ Cleartouch, 1998 vintage) is an architectural constraint for real-time payments, open banking, and API-first integrations. Decision gate for replacement vendor Q3 2026. Estimated 3-year investment $42–65M.

3. **AML/KYC Enhancement — Regulatory Priority.** OCC examination (November 2025) cited BSA/AML program deficiencies (2 MRAs, 1 MRA with commitment). Mandatory enhanced due diligence improvements, transaction monitoring tuning, and CDD refresh required by Q4 2026. This is the #1 regulatory priority.

4. **Wealth Platform Consolidation.** Three wealth technology platforms (Advent/Geneva, Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, Orion) have suboptimal integration creating advisor friction and reporting gaps. Consolidation to unified platform (Envestnet or Orion core) under evaluation.

5. **Data Governance & Analytics.** Credit decision quality, AML detection accuracy, and wealth performance attribution all require a governed data platform. Current state: 6 unconnected data warehouses, no enterprise data dictionary, manual regulatory reporting for 4 of 8 key reports.
