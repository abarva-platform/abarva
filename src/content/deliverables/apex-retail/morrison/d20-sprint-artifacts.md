## Sprint cadence

Two sprints inside the Phase 4 window. Sprint 1 (Weeks 1-2) is scaffold: workstream charters, RACI ratification, reporting pilots, contract templates. Sprint 2 (Weeks 3-4) is first-live-execution: first category cut, first supplier term-sheet signatures, first margin-gated promotional event at -22%. The cadence is deliberately **two weeks**: one-week sprints manufacture ceremony during a ramp window; three-week sprints give workstreams too long to hide a blocker [E64].

Each sprint opens with a 90-minute Monday kickoff and closes with a 90-minute Friday closeout. Between bookends, the Tuesday governance forum handles cross-workstream coordination; each workstream runs its own Friday retro. With three parallel workstreams and overlapping dependencies, the Tuesday cross-workstream forum is more consequential than any intra-workstream ceremony [E60].

## Review + retro rhythm

The weekly rhythm distinguishes cross-workstream coordination from intra-workstream improvement. Monday 0800: Nexus publishes the telemetry package (burn-down, velocity, blocker register, Tuesday agenda) to the sponsor pair, Diane R., and co-owners. Tuesday 0900: 60-minute governance forum chaired by Marcus T., with Katherine P. joining every second week. Standard agenda: blocker review, cross-workstream dependencies, sponsor decisions, upcoming week. Friday 1600: parallel 60-minute retros [E64].

Sprint closeouts replace the retro on sprint-end Fridays and review artifacts, velocity, open blockers, and Sprint N+1 readiness. Sponsor pair attends closeouts; retros are workstream-internal.

Each workstream ships a fixed set of artifact categories per sprint [E67]. Each sprint, each workstream produces: a 1-page brief; 2-4 execution artifacts (contract templates, cut-target models, reporting dashboards); a risk register update; a decision log entry; a Sprint N+1 readiness document.

## Escalation + blocker protocol

Morrison uses a **5-day blocker protocol** ratified 2026-05-06 [E60]. Blockers log on identification. At 24 hours: co-owner pair owns resolution. At 72 hours: the blocker surfaces in the next Tuesday forum regardless of agenda. At 5 days: escalation direct to Marcus T. [E60].

The protocol triggered once in Sprint 1: Supplier C redraft crossed 72 hours on 2026-05-11, surfaced in the 2026-05-12 forum, and the mitigation authorised before the 5-day threshold [E66]. The Tuesday forum catches blockers at 72 hours so they never reach 5 days — sponsor cycles are the escalation of last resort, not the default.
