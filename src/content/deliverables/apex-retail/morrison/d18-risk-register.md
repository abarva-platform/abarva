## Executive summary

The Morrison risk register catalogues **14 risks** sorted into three categories — operational (6), market/consumer (4), and execution (4) — with **2 critical risks**, **5 high**, **5 medium**, and **2 low** per impact/probability scoring [E56]. The register is deliberately compact: risks are here if they can materially affect the $6.8M capital envelope, the $14-22M/yr steady-state impact, or the 2027-Q1 attestation timing, and not otherwise. Each risk has a named **owner**, a **leading indicator**, a **mitigation action**, and an **escalation trigger** that routes to the governance forum or directly to Marcus T. [E57]. The **top 8 risks by severity-adjusted exposure** (surfaced below) are the ones tracked in every biweekly governance forum; the remaining 6 are reviewed monthly. The register was finalized on 2026-04-22, as recorded in `_timeline.json`, with 2 mitigations already triggered pre-launch: the supplier-concentration mitigation (tier-2 activation acceleration) and the category-leader-pushback mitigation (Reese A. air cover commitment). Escalation triggers are explicitly named so the register functions as a live decision aid rather than a one-time artifact [E58].

## Mitigation roadmap

The mitigation roadmap is phased against the D12 gate calendar [E57]. **Pre-G1 (Phase 3 close, 2026-04-30)** — 2 mitigations triggered: supplier concentration (tier-2 activation acceleration) and category-leader pushback (Reese/Diane air cover). **G1 to G2 (Phase 4 launch to 90 days)** — mitigations active for R1 (supplier response, early indicator), R2 (parallel-track load, daily cadence), R6 (category-leader pushback, bi-weekly signal). **G2 to G4 (90-180 days)** — mitigations active for R5 (T+7 reporting, data-engineering discipline) and R7 (depth-cap overrides, rotating authoriser). **G4 to G5 (180-270 days)** — mitigations active for R3 (brand-equity, Wave 2 re-calibration) and R4 (compounding, month-9 analytics review). **G5 to G6 (Phase 4 exit to attestation)** — mitigations active for R12 (dual-ledger drift) and R14 (attestation methodology). The roadmap is structured so that mitigation attention is front-loaded on the highest-severity risks and moves to execution-discipline risks as the program stabilises [E57].

## Escalation triggers

Every risk has a named escalation trigger, and the triggers follow a consistent pattern. **Workstream lead level** — mitigation status signals (leading indicator breach, cycle-time drift) are surfaced by workstream leads in the Tuesday sync; this is the first-line attention. **Governance forum level** — any critical or high risk with leading-indicator breach is promoted to the Thursday biweekly forum as a named agenda item; the forum has authority to approve mitigation recalibration without CFO escalation [E58]. **Marcus T. level** — any risk crossing the kill-switch threshold (R1, R5, R7 have explicit kill-switches) auto-escalates to Marcus T. with 48-hour turnaround SLA captured in D17 [E49][E58]. **CMO level** — R3 (brand-equity) escalates to Katherine P. rather than Marcus T., reflecting the stakeholder tiering that brand-equity decisions sit with the CMO not the CFO [E11][E58]. The escalation matrix is therefore not single-chain; it routes to the decision-owner most qualified to resolve the specific risk category, which keeps decision latency low and attention cost to Marcus T. bounded.

## Risks and mitigations (meta-risks)

- **[High]** **Register discipline degrades mid-program if mitigations are treated as one-time actions.** Pattern-library precedent: programs that do not re-test mitigations quarterly show 40% mitigation drift by month 9 [E57]. Mitigation: monthly governance forum dashboard includes a "mitigation health" row per top-8 risk.
- **[Medium]** **Leading indicators may drift from the underlying risk.** As the program executes, the chosen indicator may become a lagging proxy. Mitigation: monthly retrospective reviews indicator-to-risk mapping; workstream leads have authority to propose indicator changes without sponsor escalation [E59].
- **[Low]** **Below-the-fold risks (R9-R14) may be under-attended at monthly cadence.** If a monthly-review risk escalates rapidly, the biweekly forum may miss the window. Mitigation: any below-fold risk with probability change >1 tier auto-promotes to biweekly tracking without sponsor action [E58].
