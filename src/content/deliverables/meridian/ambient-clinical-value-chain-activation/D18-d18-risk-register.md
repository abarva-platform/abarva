## Executive summary

The Meridian ambient risk register catalogues **16 risks** sorted into four categories — clinical quality (5), regulatory + compliance (4), execution (5), and market/competitive (2) — with **3 critical risks**, **6 high**, **5 medium**, and **2 low** per impact/probability scoring [E57]. The register is deliberately compact: risks are here if they can materially affect the $4.2M capital envelope, the $8-14M/yr steady-state value-at-stake, the Phase 5 attestation timing, or the clinician-outcome commitments to the Board Clinical Quality and Technology Committee, and not otherwise [E56][E57]. Each risk has a named **owner**, a **leading indicator**, a **mitigation action**, and an **escalation trigger** that routes to the governance triad (Dr. Morales + Dr. Larsson + Sarah Chen monthly alignment cadence, H11), the four-principal steering forum (biweekly), or directly to Sarah Chen for critical-tier escalation [E20][E58]. The **top 8 risks by severity-adjusted exposure** are tracked at every biweekly steering; the remaining 8 are reviewed monthly. The register was finalized on 2026-04-21 (Phase 3 gate), with 3 mitigations already triggered pre-launch: the integration-engineering staffing mitigation, the unified vendor-council cadence mitigation, and the D13 pilot cohort-engagement mitigation [E42][E57]. Escalation triggers are explicitly named so the register functions as a live decision aid rather than a one-time artifact [E58][E59].

## Regulatory + compliance risks

The four regulatory risks concentrate on HIPAA, 42 CFR Part 2 (substance-use-disorder confidentiality), and the Info Blocking rule under the 21st Century Cures Act — the three frameworks with the most direct bearing on ambient clinical documentation at a US IDN [E58]. **R6 · HIPAA exposure from ambient-transcription data handling across three vendors · Critical.** Owner: Sarah Chen + Meridian Privacy Office. Probability: low-medium. Impact: breach risk if any of three vendor environments has a data-handling incident; reputational + regulatory exposure [E58]. Leading indicator: vendor-council quarterly attestation on data-handling posture; third-party audit readiness assessment per vendor. Mitigation: Meridian standing Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each of Abridge, DAX Copilot, and Suki, re-audited on Phase 4 launch; unified vendor-council cadence (H10) includes a named privacy-officer attendee at every session; any PHI-handling change in vendor product commissioning requires Privacy Office pre-approval [E24][E58].

**R7 · 42 CFR Part 2 compliance on substance-use-disorder documentation in ambient transcripts · High.** Owner: Meridian Privacy Office + Dr. Larsson. Probability: low. Impact: regulatory exposure if ambient transcribes a Part 2-protected encounter without proper redaction or consent pathway [E58]. Leading indicator: monthly audit of ambient-transcribed encounters flagged for Part 2 protection; vendor-council review of redaction-pathway compliance. Mitigation: Part 2 consent pathway integrated into Epic ambient-active toggle before Phase 4 launch; ambulatory-site behavioral health clinicians excluded from ambient coverage until vendor-council can attest to Part 2 redaction capability on all three vendors. **R8 · Info Blocking rule compliance on clinician-facing note review windows · High.** Owner: Dr. Morales + Meridian Compliance. Probability: low-medium. Impact: if the 72-hour clinician review window (R2 mitigation) is classified as information blocking under the 21st Century Cures Act, regulatory exposure follows [E58]. Leading indicator: quarterly Compliance Office review of note-release cadence at pilot and non-pilot sites; any patient portal delay exceeding 3 business days flagged. Mitigation: review window structured as "automatic release after 72 hours unless clinician actively flags inaccuracy" — preserves clinician review authority without becoming a blanket delay; pre-cleared with Meridian Compliance Office 2026-04-10 [E58]. **R9 · Vendor data-use secondary-purpose audit failures · Medium.** Owner: Sarah Chen. Probability: low. Impact: if a vendor uses Meridian ambient data for training beyond the BAA-permitted scope, regulatory + contractual exposure [E58]. Leading indicator: annual vendor data-use attestation; contract amendment triggers if vendor product changes materially. Mitigation: BAA scope restricts all vendor use to permitted purposes; monthly vendor-council cadence includes a named data-use standing item.

## Mitigation roadmap

The mitigation roadmap is phased against the D12 gate calendar [E57]. **Pre-G1 (Phase 3 close, 2026-04-21)** — 3 mitigations triggered: integration-engineering staffing (R10), unified vendor-council cadence (R6), D13 pilot cohort engagement (R4) [E42]. **G1 to G2 (Phase 4 launch to 90 days)** — mitigations active for R10 (CDI-queue ramp, daily engineering cadence), R14 (D13 cut-over readiness), R6 (HIPAA vendor attestation baseline), R3 (clinician adoption, weekly pulse). **G2 to G3 (90-180 days)** — mitigations active for R11 (HCC feedback-loop commissioning), R1 (template drift, monthly audit), R2 (ambient accuracy, weekly CDI-queue sample). **G3 to G4 (180-240 days)** — mitigations active for R8 (Info Blocking compliance), R5 (quality-measure capture regression), R13 (governance triad cadence). **G4 to G5 (240-305 days)** — mitigations active for R4 (pilot-to-non-pilot leak monitoring), R7 (42 CFR Part 2 audit), R9 (vendor data-use audit). **G5 to G6 (Phase 4 exit to attestation)** — mitigations active for R12 (dual-ledger drift), R15 (vendor M&A watch). The roadmap is structured so that mitigation attention is front-loaded on the critical-tier and execution risks and moves to regulatory and competitive risks as the program stabilises [E57].

## Escalation triggers

Every risk has a named escalation trigger, and the triggers follow a consistent pattern [E58]. **Workstream lead level** — leading-indicator breach or cycle-time drift is surfaced at the Tuesday operational sync; this is the first-line attention. **Governance triad level (H11)** — any high or critical risk with leading-indicator breach is promoted to the monthly CMIO/CMO/CIO triad as a named agenda item; the triad has authority to approve mitigation recalibration without four-principal steering escalation [E20][E58]. **Four-principal steering level** — any critical risk with mitigation failure or any kill-switch threshold breach (R10, R11, R2) auto-escalates to the biweekly steering with 48-hour turnaround SLA [E45][E58]. **Sarah Chen level** — any risk crossing a kill-switch threshold and remaining unresolved after one steering cycle auto-escalates to Sarah Chen with 48-hour turnaround SLA captured in D17 [E16][E58]. **Dr. Larsson level** — any clinical quality risk (R1-R5) escalates to Dr. Larsson rather than Sarah Chen, reflecting the CMO's standing authority on clinician-facing decisions [E4][E16][E58]. **Privacy Office + Compliance level** — regulatory risks (R6-R9) route to the Meridian Privacy Office and Compliance Office with a 24-hour SLA on any active compliance breach signal, and only then to sponsor-level escalation [E58]. The escalation matrix is therefore not single-chain; it routes to the decision-owner most qualified to resolve the specific risk category, which keeps decision latency low and bounds the attention cost to any one principal.

## Risks and mitigations (meta-risks)

- **[High]** **Register discipline degrades mid-program if mitigations are treated as one-time actions.** Pattern-library precedent: ambient programs that do not re-test mitigations quarterly show 35-45% mitigation drift by month 9 [E7][E57]. Mitigation: monthly governance triad dashboard includes a "mitigation health" row per top-8 risk.
- **[Medium]** **Regulatory landscape may shift during Phase 4 (Info Blocking, 42 CFR Part 2 guidance, state-level AI documentation rules).** New guidance could re-classify existing mitigations or add new compliance surfaces. Mitigation: Meridian Compliance Office standing quarterly read-out to the governance triad; ambient-specific regulatory watch on the Nexus external-intelligence surface [E58].
- **[Low]** **Below-the-fold risks (R9, R12, R13, R14, R15, R16) may be under-attended at monthly cadence.** If a monthly-review risk escalates rapidly, the biweekly forum may miss the window. Mitigation: any below-fold risk with probability change >1 tier auto-promotes to biweekly tracking without sponsor action [E58].
