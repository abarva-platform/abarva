## Executive summary

This decision memo commits Meridian Health System to a **three-lever ambient value-chain activation program** executing in parallel across the 14-month Phase 4 window (2026-05-27 through 2027-03-31), backed by a **$4.2M capital envelope** and a projected **$8-14M/yr steady-state value-at-stake** against the $7.8B revenue base and 55% MA-and-commercial-risk payer mix [E9][E16][E18][E50]. The memo **recommends Option C — preserve the three-vendor operating envelope (Abridge, Microsoft DAX Copilot, Suki) through Phase 4 while executing documentation governance, clinician workflow redesign, and a structured 18-site consolidation pilot (D13)** — and defers the vendor-rationalisation decision to the **2027-Q3 Phase 5 ratification workshop** where pilot evidence, integration-driven outcome signal, and CMIO-sponsored consolidation evidence are all reasoned against together [E6][E42][E54]. Three options were reviewed (A Abridge consolidation, B DAX Copilot consolidation, C hybrid with redesign); the recommended path is Option C with **CMIO dissent formally captured** on rationalisation timing and a pre-ratified decision matrix governing the Phase 5 ratification workshop [E6][E55]. The decision is signed by Sarah Chen (CIO, primary sponsor), Dr. Larsson (CMO, co-sponsor with clinician-cognitive-load authority), and Priya Raman (VP Revenue Cycle, HCC and DRG specificity authority) on 2026-04-14 [E16]. The decision is conditional on three readiness items before Phase 4 launches at G1: integration-engineering team fully staffed, unified vendor-council cadence ratified, D13 pilot cohort engagement plan signed by medical-staff leadership [E42][E43].

## Decision statement

Meridian Health System commits to the **three-lever parallel-track portfolio** defined in D15, executed on the 14-month Phase 4 roadmap defined in D12, and financed against the business case ratified in D16. The vendor-rationalisation question (H12 held open from D11) is **explicitly deferred to Phase 5**, with the D13 ambient consolidation pilot producing the Meridian-specific evidence the 2027-Q3 ratification workshop will reason against [E6][E31][E42][E54]. The three-vendor operating envelope is preserved through Phase 4, not as an endorsement of three-vendor operation as a steady state, but as a **structured deferral** that captures integration-driven outcome lift (~70% of single-vendor value per D10 Peer-3 decomposition [E30]) without paying the 14-20 month clinician-retraining cost that a Phase 4 consolidation would impose across ~2,400 providers [E31].

## Executive summary for the Board

The program commits Meridian to activating ambient clinical documentation as a **value chain** — integrated with clinical documentation improvement, HCC capture, and quality-measure capture — rather than operating three ambient vendors as parallel scribe products [E3][E7]. The business case projects $8-14M/yr steady-state value-at-stake against a $4.2M capital ask, with payback in 12-18 months and a 36-month NPV of $14-32M at Meridian's 7.5% cost of capital [E50][E51]. The program **preserves the three-vendor operating envelope through Phase 4** and **defers the vendor-rationalisation decision to Phase 5** after a structured 18-site consolidation pilot produces Meridian-specific evidence [E42][E54]. The clinician-outcome focus — a 25-35 minute/day pajama-time reduction in primary care and a 4-7 point MBI-HSS emotional-exhaustion reduction — is the program's north-star, not a downstream co-benefit [E4][E17][E29]. The Board read-out on 2026-05-12 seeks the Clinical Quality and Technology Committee's affirmation of the Phase 4 commitment and its acknowledgment that the Phase 5 rationalisation decision returns to the Committee in 2027-Q3 with pilot evidence [E56].

## 3 options reviewed

**Option A · Abridge primary-care consolidation.** Consolidate to Abridge as the single ambient vendor for all primary care within Phase 4 (~1,400 primary-care clinicians), sunsetting DAX Copilot and Suki at primary-care sites by month 9. Specialty outpatient continues on DAX Copilot through Phase 4; outpatient procedural (Suki) separated to a Phase 5 decision [E12][E52]. **Expected outcome envelope** — pajama-time reduction 30-40 min/day (primary care), HCC capture lift 4-6 points, MBI-HSS reduction 6-10 points, driven by both integration-specific and consolidation-specific outcome components [E28][E29][E30]. **Cost** — $4.2M Phase 4 capital is insufficient; Option A requires an additional $1.8-2.4M for clinician-retraining and Abridge scale-up, pushing the envelope to $6.0-6.6M. **Risk** — 14-20 month clinician-transition exposure; clinician-friction escalation on a ~2,400-provider footprint with no prior Meridian-specific consolidation evidence [E23][E31]. **CMIO position** — preferred option; Dr. Morales argues earlier consolidation captures the ~30% consolidation-specific outcome component that Option C by design does not [E6][E30][E52].

**Option B · DAX Copilot specialty consolidation.** Consolidate to DAX Copilot as the single ambient vendor for specialty outpatient (~600 specialty clinicians); sunset Suki at specialty sites by month 9; primary care continues on Abridge + DAX Copilot split through Phase 4. **Expected outcome envelope** — pajama-time reduction 15-22 min/day (specialty), DRG specificity lift 2-4 points, limited MBI-HSS lift because specialty pajama time is already closer to cohort parity [E13][E17][E28][E53]. **Cost** — $4.2M envelope is adequate but reallocates $1.1M from Lever 2 to consolidation execution, thinning HCC feedback-loop commissioning. **Risk** — value envelope is thinner than Option A because specialty pajama time is already near cohort median; consolidation on the thinner-value surface is not well-supported by Phase 2 evidence [E17][E53]. **CMIO position** — not preferred; Dr. Morales regards specialty consolidation as a lower-priority rationalisation target than primary care [E6][E53].

**Option C · Hybrid · three-vendor preserved + full redesign + pilot.** Execute the D15 three-lever portfolio (documentation governance + CDI integration; clinician-workflow redesign; vendor-rationalisation pilot-first) with the three-vendor operating envelope preserved through Phase 4; run the D13 18-site consolidation pilot as a structured counterfactual; route the rationalisation decision to 2027-Q3 ratification [E42][E47][E54]. **Expected outcome envelope** — pajama-time reduction 25-35 min/day (primary care), HCC capture lift 3-5 points, MBI-HSS reduction 4-7 points, driven primarily by the integration-specific outcome component (~70% of single-vendor lift per D10 Peer-3 decomposition [E30]). **Cost** — fits the $4.2M envelope exactly; Phase 5 continuation capital is separate and not drawn against Phase 4. **Risk** — forgoes the ~30% consolidation-specific outcome component in Phase 4; recaptures it in Phase 5 if the D13 pilot produces positive signal; if pilot returns mixed signal, Phase 5 ratification still has better evidence than the Phase 3 baseline [E42][E45][E54].

## Recommendation

**Recommended: Option C.** The recommendation rests on three convergent considerations. **First**, the D10 Peer-3 decomposition establishes that ~70% of single-vendor outcome lift is **integration-driven** and portable to a multi-vendor footprint; only ~30% is **consolidation-driven** and requires single-vendor operation [E30]. Option C captures the 70% integration component in Phase 4 without paying the 14-20 month clinician-retraining cost Option A imposes [E31]. **Second**, the $4.2M envelope fits Option C exactly; Option A requires a $1.8-2.4M supplemental commitment that exceeds the sponsor-ratified capital envelope and triggers a new Board authorisation cycle [E41][E52]. **Third**, the D13 pilot is designed to produce the Meridian-specific consolidation evidence the 2027-Q3 ratification workshop needs, so deferring the rationalisation decision to Phase 5 is not a deferral of the decision — it is a **sequencing of the decision against better evidence** [E42][E54]. The recommendation is signed by the primary sponsor (Sarah Chen), co-sponsor (Dr. Larsson), and revenue-cycle lead (Priya Raman); Dr. Morales's dissent is formally captured and does not block the decision under the charter's tiered governance architecture [E6][E16][E55].

## Dissent captured · CMIO + primary care

Dr. Morales's (CMIO) dissent is formally captured in this memo and carried forward to the 2027-Q3 ratification workshop. **Dr. Morales's position**: earlier consolidation to Abridge in primary care (Option A or a scaled variant) would capture the ~30% consolidation-specific outcome component that Option C by design does not, and the 14-20 month clinician-transition window is recoverable if started in Phase 4 rather than Phase 5 [E6][E52][E55]. **Dr. Morales's specific concerns** — (1) three-vendor operation creates governance fragmentation even with the unified cadence (H10), because vendor-council decision-making is a bilateral process each vendor operates differently [E11][E24]; (2) template-drift audit at 23% error rate is a present clinician harm, not a future risk, and Option C's template-governance lever reaches only 11-14% residual error while Option A reaches 7-10% [E11][E47][E52]; (3) deferring the rationalisation decision risks losing the sponsor-attention coherence the Phase 3 window currently carries, and Phase 5 ratification inherits lower decision intensity [E6][E55]. **Counterposition preserved in the memo**: the sponsor group (Sarah Chen, Dr. Larsson, Priya Raman) agrees with the substantive weight of Dr. Morales's concerns and disagrees only with the timing. The 2027-Q3 ratification workshop will be chaired by Dr. Morales specifically to ensure the CMIO position has structural authority at the point where the rationalisation decision is actually made [E54][E55]. The **primary-care service-line readout** also registered a concern about clinician-transition fatigue under Option A, supporting Option C timing; that readout is included in the Phase 5 packet alongside the CMIO dissent so both positions are preserved in full context [E55].

## Risks and mitigations

- **[High]** **Phase 4 runs on a three-vendor operating envelope that Dr. Morales regards as an unstable equilibrium.** If vendor-council governance fragmentation overwhelms the unified cadence (H10), decision latency at 18-day median reasserts and cross-workstream coordination defaults to ad-hoc sponsor emails [E6][E11][E24]. Mitigation: unified vendor-council cadence ratified pre-G1; Dr. Morales chairs the cadence with escalation authority; monthly CMIO/CMO/CIO triad (H11) acts as the second-line governance forum [E20][E40].
- **[High]** **D13 pilot may return mixed-signal at month 6, leaving the 2027-Q3 rationalisation workshop with ambiguous evidence.** If 2 of 4 scale-out triggers meet, the workshop inherits a less-decisive evidence base than the recommendation assumes [E42][E45]. Mitigation: pilot design explicitly separates consolidation-specific and integration-specific outcome components; even a mixed-signal pilot produces better evidence than the Phase 3 baseline; 2027-Q3 workshop has three pre-ratified decision paths (scale Option A, preserve Option C, extend pilot) [E54][E55].
- **[Medium]** **Board read-out on 2026-05-12 may surface governance concerns that delay Phase 4 launch past G1.** If the Clinical Quality and Technology Committee requests additional clinician-voice evidence or a deeper rationalisation rationale, Phase 4 launch could slip 4-8 weeks [E56]. Mitigation: pre-read-out briefing with Committee Chair scheduled 2026-05-01; two-page clinical-outcome summary pre-shared; D13 pilot cohort engagement plan available as on-request appendix.
