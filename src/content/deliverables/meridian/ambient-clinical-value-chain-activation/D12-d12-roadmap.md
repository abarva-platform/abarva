## Executive summary

The MRD-01 roadmap sequences the seven validated Phase 2 hypotheses (D11) into a **three-workstream parallel track** running from the Phase 4 launch gate on **2026-05-27** through the Phase 5 attestation target of **2027-Q1**, on a **36-month horizon** [E31][E40]. The first 14 months (Phase 4) are the committed execution envelope — CDI integration, HCC feedback loop commissioning, and specialty-specific template governance executing concurrently across the three ambient vendors (Abridge, Microsoft DAX Copilot, Suki) — and the trailing 22 months cover Phase 5 attestation, vendor rationalisation ratification, and the deferred-hypothesis (H3, H8) continuation window [E40][E41]. The **$4.2M capital envelope** is phased **50% in H1, 28% in H2, 14% in H3, 8% in H4**, front-loading the CDI-telemetry and HCC-feedback-loop build [E41]. Six gates (G1-G6) separate windows; every gate has a CIO/CMO pass/no-pass criterion tied to pajama-time recovery, CDI-queue routing volume, HCC capture lift, or documentation quality. The roadmap deliberately rejects a "consolidate first, integrate later" shape because the pattern-library compounding analysis (D11) shows parallel integration recovers clinician time **25-35% faster** than sequential execution [E32] — a decision ratified by Sarah Chen on 2026-03-18 alongside the D15 portfolio approval [E40].

## 36-month horizon

The horizon spans four distinct execution windows. **Window 1 · Phase 4 build (2026-05-27 to 2026-10-31)** — three workstreams stand up; WS-A commissions the unified ambient-to-CDI telemetry surface, WS-B opens HCC feedback loops on Abridge primary-care and DAX Copilot specialty, WS-C rebuilds the quality-measure documentation capture pipeline at encounter level. **Window 2 · Phase 4 early harvest (2026-11-01 to 2027-03-31)** — first measurable pajama-time reduction on primary care; CDI-queue routing lifts from ~40 queries/week baseline toward 140-180 cohort median [E15][E25][E28]; HCC capture begins to lift on the MA panel. **Window 3 · Phase 5 attestation (2027-04-01 to 2027-09-30)** — dual-ledger reconciliation with Meridian Finance and the CFO office; sponsor attestation against the D16 envelope; vendor rationalisation decision ratified from the D17 held-open position [E6]. **Window 4 · Continuation optionality (2027-10-01 to 2029-04-30)** — carried as an option, not a commitment, for deferred hypotheses H3 (specialty consolidation) and H8 (in-basket/portal-message coverage) [E40]. The horizon framing is longer than the 14-month Phase 4 envelope because Phase 5 attestation and the vendor rationalisation ratification are where the program's real value accretes — and because Dr. Morales co-signed the frame that rationalisation is a Phase 5 transition, not a Phase 4 disruption [E6][E16].

## Vendor rationalisation path

The roadmap treats the three-vendor envelope as a **preserved operating state through Phase 4**, not as an execution blocker [E16][E31]. The rationale is explicit: the pattern-library analysis in D10 shows ~70% of single-vendor outcome lift is **integration-driven** and portable to a multi-vendor footprint, while only ~30% is **consolidation-driven** and requires single-vendor operation [E30]. Phase 4 captures the integration lift without paying the 14-20 month clinician-retraining cost that H1 (full consolidation) would impose across ~2,400 providers [E31]. The rationalisation question is **held open for Phase 5** with three path-ways prepared in D17: Option A (Abridge primary-care consolidation), Option B (DAX Copilot specialty consolidation), Option C (three-vendor operating state preserved, with redesign captured). The recommended path in D17 is Option C for Phase 4 with a **transition decision in Q4 2027** based on Phase 4 outcome signal [E54][E55]. Dr. Morales's dissent — a preference for earlier consolidation — is carried into the Phase 5 packet with named evidence points from H2 and D10, so the ratification workshop in 2027-Q3 inherits a textured decision context rather than a binary revisit [E6].

## Documentation governance rollout

Documentation governance rolls out in three staged cadences mirroring the workstream architecture. **Stage 1 · unified vendor-council cadence (G1 onwards)** — Dr. Morales chairs a single weekly cadence bringing Abridge, DAX Copilot, and Suki vendor-council representatives together with Meridian-side leads; replaces the three parallel cadences that generated median 18-day cross-vendor decision latency (H10) [E24]. **Stage 2 · specialty-specific template governance board (G3 onwards)** — quarterly board ratifies template updates across the three vendors on a single Epic-library spine; target: template-selection errors drop from 23% baseline to 11-14% residual (H6) [E11]. **Stage 3 · CMIO/CMO/CIO alignment triad (G1 onwards, monthly)** — separate from the biweekly four-principal steering; pre-empts the governance drift that multi-principal ambient programs exhibit in the pattern library (H11) [E7][E20]. The governance rollout is explicit infrastructure, not a standalone outcome lever — its job is to keep WS-A, WS-B, and WS-C coherent while they execute in parallel and to prevent the classical failure mode of vendor-council drift crystallising before steering can reconcile it [E16].

## Dependencies

The second critical dependency is **Epic integration engineering capacity** inside Theo J.'s team; G1 cannot open without three integration engineers fully dedicated to ambient work, which is the single mitigation Sarah Chen committed to on 2026-03-18 [E40]. Non-critical but watched: the unified vendor-council cadence (H10) must be ratified by G1 or the cross-workstream coordination defaults to ad-hoc sponsor emails — a pattern-library failure mode [E7][E24]. The CMIO/CMO/CIO alignment triad (H11) must be running by G2 so that any workstream-level escalation has a pre-existing decision forum rather than having to stand one up under pressure. The full dependency map is maintained by the Nexus orchestrator on the shared workstream surface; this roadmap surfaces only the critical-path edges that could move Phase 4 exit dates.

## Risks and mitigations

- **[High]** **Parallel-track execution concentrates Q3 2026 operational load on Dr. Morales's informatics team.** Three workstreams plus unified vendor-council cadence plus CMIO/CMO/CIO triad could overwhelm a single CMIO's bandwidth. Mitigation: Theo J. co-chairs WS-A and WS-B; Kevin B. chairs the WS-C clinician-coaching sub-workstream with Dr. Larsson reviewing biweekly; two additional integration engineers committed pre-G1 [E40].
- **[Medium]** **Gate criteria calibrated to pattern-library cohort medians, not Meridian-specific counterfactuals.** G2's 80 queries/week threshold is a midpoint between Meridian's ~40/week baseline and cohort median 165/week; Meridian-specific response may undershoot. Mitigation: G2 has a pre-announced re-forecast trigger if day-60 signal is in the 55-75 query band; re-forecast does not halt but updates the D16 envelope downward toward the $8M/yr floor scenario [E9][E49].
- **[Medium]** **Capital phasing 50/28/14/8 assumes no Phase 4 re-scope.** H1 spend is largely committed by 2026-09; if sponsor appetite shifts mid-program, the phasing must be replanned. Mitigation: Sarah Chen standing commitment in D17; Phase 5 continuation capital (for H3, H8) is separate and not drawn against the $4.2M envelope [E41][E54].
