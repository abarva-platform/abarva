# Scope Memo with Boundaries

> The contract this event makes with the rest of the organization about what's in and out.

**Stage:** Scope · Step 2 of 11
**Lead agent:** Nexus
**Owner role:** Sponsor

---

## §1 · In-scope applications

The applications, services, and capabilities this sourcing event covers. Reference `d04_app_inv` for the full tiered list.

## §2 · Out-of-scope

What this event does NOT cover. Examples: custom-built revenue platform (separate sourcing path), in-house security operations, applications already in another sourcing event (cross-reference event code).

## §3 · Target support tier

Sized from `d07_ticket_synth`. Example: 24×7 for Tier 1 apps, 12×5 for Tier 2/3, follow-the-sun for Tier 1 incidents.

> While `d07_ticket_synth` is **Loaded · not Parsed**, this section is *Outline-tier* and the RFP cannot be drafted at full fidelity.

## §4 · Transition assumptions

Working assumption set: knowledge-transfer window, parallel-run period, vendor staffing model, on-shore / near-shore / off-shore mix, language coverage.

## §5 · Pricing assumption set anchors

Anchors that the pricing normalization in Step 6 will lock to: hours/year per tier, escalator caps, FX, on-call premiums, volume bands.

## §6 · Vendor relationship constraints

Concentration limits, conflict-of-interest exclusions, regulatory holds (e.g. data residency) that constrain vendor selection downstream.

## §7 · Decision rights

Who can change scope mid-event. Sponsor + EA must co-sign any scope amendment after this memo signs.
