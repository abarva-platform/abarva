# Locked Assumption Set

> Sponsor-approved horizon, FX, escalators, in-scope assumption set.

**Stage:** Pricing · Step 6 of 11
**Lead agent:** Nexus
**Owner role:** Sponsor

---

## §1 · Time horizon

3-year / 5-year. Justification for the choice (peer benchmark, contract term, value-realization window).

## §2 · FX

Currency basis, hedging assumption, rate snapshot date.

## §3 · Escalators

Per-year escalation cap (e.g. 4%), index reference (CPI / market basket / fixed). Vendor-side caps and floors.

## §4 · Volume bands

Volume assumption per year. Below-band and above-band pricing rules from RFP.

## §5 · Scope-tied assumptions

Anything in `d05_scope_memo` that drives pricing: hours/year per tier, on-call premiums, language coverage, geographic mix.

## §6 · Sponsor sign-off

Sponsor signature locks this assumption set. Any change after lock requires sponsor + Steward co-sign.
