## §1 · Scoring rubric

The criteria with weights (locked from `d17_weight_log`) and scoring scale (1-100). Each criterion has an evidence-citation requirement.

## §3 · Per-criterion rationale

For each criterion × vendor cell, the rationale and the evidence cited. No score without evidence.

## §4 · Deviation log

Where multiple raters disagreed by more than 5 points, the reconciliation. Either:
- Both raters agree on a final score after discussion (with the new score logged)
- Steward arbitrates with rationale

## §5 · Sensitivity analysis

How rankings shift under alternative weight sets. Stewards run at the disputed weights to surface where the decision is fragile.

## §6 · Steward sign-off

Confirms scoring governance complete, weights locked, rationale auditable.
