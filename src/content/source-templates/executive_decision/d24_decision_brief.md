## §1 · Recommendation

One sentence: which vendor, conditional on what. Example: *Recommend Northstar at $13.21M normalized TCO with conditional security uplift.*

## §2 · Why this vendor

Two paragraphs. The deciding axis (capability · security · cost · transition · roadmap). What evidence is anchoring the call. What the alternative would have given up.
