# Sourcing Strategy Memo

> The why-now for this sourcing event. What we're sourcing, the value target, and the rigor we'll apply.

**Stage:** Strategy · Step 1 of 11
**Lead agent:** Atlas
**Owner role:** Sponsor

---

## §1 · Why now

What changed that makes this sourcing event necessary today, not next quarter? Cite the trigger: contract expiration, value pressure, capability gap, regulatory deadline, peer benchmark, executive ask.

## §2 · What we're sourcing

The category, scope band, and rigor level. State whether this is a renewal, consolidation, displacement, or net-new event. Name the archetype (AMS / Cloud / Data / Enterprise SW / Custom).

## §3 · Value target

Projected value range (low / high) with the levers that drive it: labor arbitrage, automation, consolidation, license rationalization, decommissioning. Stamp confidence band (`v2 PENDING` until substrate is bound).

## §4 · Rigor level

Standard / Enhanced / Strategic. Justify against deal value, business criticality, and risk posture. Strategic rigor unlocks longer cycle times but adds Atlas decision brief and Sentinel risk attestation.

## §5 · Constraints + non-goals

Time bounds, budget envelope, things this event explicitly does NOT cover. Future-state initiatives that depend on but are outside this event's scope.

## §6 · Sponsor commitment

Sponsor signature block. Without this, Strategy → Scope gate cannot pass.
