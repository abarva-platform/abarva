## Walkthrough

Operations leaders upload open shifts, acuity, premium labor, and incident
history. The context layer ties AI scheduling opportunities to clinical safety
and operational load rather than treating workforce as a generic cost pool.

## Agent Value

Tower can show workforce pressure by unit. Sentinel can distinguish staffing
forecasting opportunities from unsafe automation claims.

## Evidence Checks

- Units are named consistently across scheduling and acuity files.
- Burnout and clinical-impact signals remain visible in agent answers.
- Incident root causes are not collapsed into generic productivity language.
