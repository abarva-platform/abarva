## Walkthrough

The pilot team uploads the files needed to answer board-level questions about
value-based care, pharmacy and supply-chain exposure, AI tool sprawl, and
engineering capacity. The context layer highlights which claims are grounded
and which require a follow-up data request.

## Agent Value

Sentinel can produce an executive answer that is useful without fabricating:
it can cite what is loaded, identify missing evidence, and recommend the next
highest-leverage upload.

## Evidence Checks

- AI tool spend distinguishes PHI and non-PHI workflows.
- Value-based care patterns include covered lives and risk-pool size.
- DORA metrics are linked to the teams expected to deliver AI programs.
