# Apex Retail Corporate Policies and AI Use Context

Synthetic source document for context-layer ingestion.

## Policy Context

Apex's omnichannel, retail media, store operations, and AI agenda is governed by privacy, customer consent, data classification, labor policy, loss-prevention rules, cyber architecture, third-party risk, and responsible AI policies.

The responsible AI policy requires business-owner approval, human oversight, and monitoring before AI can affect customer pricing, promotions, loyalty offers, retail media targeting, workforce scheduling, shrink investigations, returns decisions, or store associate coaching. AI-generated recommendations must be explainable enough for store, marketing, finance, and legal owners to review.

The data classification policy treats loyalty identity, purchase history, payment tokens, employee scheduling data, camera/loss-prevention events, supplier terms, retail media clean-room data, and customer service records as confidential or restricted. Restricted data cannot be used in unapproved AI tooling, and customer personalization requires consent and privacy review.

The sourcing policy requires AI, retail media, workforce, and loss-prevention vendors to disclose data usage, subprocessor list, model-training terms, clean-room controls, audit rights, breach-notification terms, and exit rights.

## Controls That Affect AI Use Cases

- Inventory and fulfillment AI requires item-location accuracy, store-task reliability, exception controls, and operational owner approval.
- Retail media AI requires consent, clean-room governance, incrementality measurement, and margin impact evidence.
- Shrink AI requires POS exception evidence, camera-event governance, investigation workflow controls, and bias/customer-impact review.
- Store labor AI requires labor-law compliance, manager override, associate-impact monitoring, and schedule-change audit trail.
- Customer personalization requires identity resolution, preference/consent management, and privacy review.

## Sourcing Implications

Apex should sequence AI around trusted inventory, consented identity, store execution, and evidence-linked operations. Policy constraints should be visible in the Context Explorer because they decide which AI use cases can be sourced, piloted, or scaled.
