## Base Entitlements

Initial order establishes committed quantities, UOM, SKU/service code, unit rate, billing cadence, and service-period alignment.

## Billing Cadence

Seat-month and capacity-month lines bill monthly; annual commitments amortize monthly and must not multiply by seat quantity.

## Base Price Protection

Base order uplift is capped at 5.0 percent unless superseded by a later executed pricing amendment.
