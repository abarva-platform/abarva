# Public Company Strategy Evidence Template Pack v1

Purpose: align client/tenant intelligence with the public corporate record: annual reports, quarterly earnings, investor presentations, investor conference remarks, risk disclosures, segment KPIs, capital allocation, and management commentary.

These templates are source-control templates, not seed data. For a real public company, attach the actual source document and capture citation location. For a synthetic public-company analog, generate document-native evidence that behaves like a public filing without claiming to be a real filing.

2. `02_corporate_strategy_and_growth_agenda.template.csv`

## Source Discipline

Every public-source claim needs:

- Source type: annual report, quarterly earnings release, transcript, investor day, investor conference, proxy, ESG/sustainability report, cyber/risk disclosure.
- Source date and period.
- Citation location: page, section, exhibit, timestamp, speaker, table, or slide.
- Claim text or KPI.
- Confidence and freshness.
- Internal implication: which IT system, initiative, vendor, risk, or cost center it should constrain.

## Why This Matters

An enterprise IT landscape is not credible if it is disconnected from the company narrative. Public filings and investor materials tell us what management has committed to: growth, margin, customer experience, productivity, regulatory risk, M&A, capital intensity, modernization, AI, cyber, and operating resilience. The internal synthetic substrate should make those promises testable.
