Synthetic demo evidence. No real client confidential data.

- Board wants decision readiness before the Q3 planning cycle.
- Treasury blackout: last five business days of each month.
- Payroll and disbursement accounts cannot cut over during quarter close.
- Bank onboarding must begin with Northern Trust, BMO, and Wintrust archetypes before long-tail accounts.

## Dependency Constraints
- ERP feed owners must sign mapping before SIT starts.
- Security must approve SSO, MFA, and privileged access model before UAT.
- FP&A must accept Tower reporting grain before value ledger activation.
- Procurement must preserve vendor response comparability through a mandatory pricing template.

## Mobilization Recommendation
Run a 3-wave rollout: Wave 1 daily cash visibility, Wave 2 payment controls, Wave 3 long-tail account cleanup and Tower value measurement.
