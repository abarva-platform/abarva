# Scenario 07 - Regulatory Interoperability And Downtime

## Uploads

- `17-upload-templates/cms-interoperability.csv`
- `17-upload-templates/security-downtime-readiness.csv`

## Walkthrough

Compliance, security, and integration leaders upload CMS readiness, downtime
tiers, and clinical incident history. The context layer produces a combined
view of regulatory deadline risk and clinical resilience.

## Agent Value

Sentinel can explain whether CMS interoperability blockers are technical,
operational, payer-specific, or downtime-resilience related. Tower can surface
regulatory pressure without turning it into generic compliance theater.

## Evidence Checks

- Every regulatory requirement has deadline and blocker.
- Downtime workarounds exist for tier-0 and tier-1 systems.
- Clinical impact is preserved in incident summaries.
