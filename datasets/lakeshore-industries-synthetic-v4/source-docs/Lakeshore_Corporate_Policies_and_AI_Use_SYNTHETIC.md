## Policy Context

Lakeshore's Kyriba, finance, treasury, sourcing, and AI work is governed by treasury policy, SOX controls, payment approval rules, data classification, procurement policy, cyber architecture standards, vendor risk, and responsible AI guidance.

The treasury policy requires approved bank connectivity, signer controls, payment-format validation, segregation of duties, fraud controls, and audit evidence before payments or cash-positioning automation can go live. Kyriba workflows that initiate, approve, transmit, or reconcile payments must preserve source evidence and approver history.

The data classification policy treats bank account details, payment files, supplier banking records, treasury forecasts, GL balances, hedge data, payroll files, customer pricing, and board reporting as confidential or restricted. Restricted finance data cannot be used in unapproved AI tools or external model services without security and legal approval.

The sourcing policy requires TMS, ERP, bank-connectivity, and AI vendors to disclose hosting, data retention, subprocessors, breach notification, audit rights, exit provisions, and integration responsibilities. Finance and treasury vendors must show SOX control alignment before expansion.

## Controls That Affect AI Use Cases

- Kyriba rollout requires bank-connectivity proof, SAP feed reconciliation, payment approval evidence, signer matrix validation, and SOX testing.
- Cash forecasting AI requires certified finance data products, source lineage, forecast explainability, and Treasurer approval.
- Automated close and reporting require GL/source reconciliation, control evidence, and CFO signoff on semantic definitions.
- Supplier/payment anomaly detection requires fraud-policy alignment, human review, and exception tracking.
- Finance copilots require restricted-data protection and approved knowledge repositories.

## Sourcing Implications

Lakeshore should treat policy readiness as a launch gate for Kyriba and finance AI. The strongest move is to connect treasury processes, SAP feeds, bank evidence, controls, and vendor obligations before scaling automation.
